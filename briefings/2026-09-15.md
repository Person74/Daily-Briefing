## Foreign Policy

A Pentagon watchdog report released Monday detailed US weapons shortfalls, and Saudi Arabia shut its East-West oil pipeline after repeated drone attacks. *Confirmed unresolved as of Sept 14.* ([GlobalSecurity.org, Sep 14](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/ops/iran-war-oprep.htm))

## AI

**Bottom line:** Dario Amodei's call to deliberately slow frontier AI development triggered a rare cross-industry safety pact this week — and rattled AI-linked stocks in the process.

- **Amodei's "Pace the Frontier" essay reshapes the industry.** Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a 3,800-word essay on Sept 12 proposing embedded independent evaluators inside frontier labs, industry-wide safety coordination, and eventually an international agreement including China; OpenAI's Sam Altman and xAI's Elon Musk publicly agreed within a day, and Microsoft's Satya Nadella welcomed "deliberate pacing" the next day. ([MarkTechPost, Sep 13](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/13/anthropics-3-step-pace-the-frontier-plan-wins-openai-xai-and-microsoft-support-is-it-too-late-to-slow-ai-down/))
- **Rival labs cosign a new evaluator standard.** Following the essay, xAI, OpenAI and Anthropic jointly cosigned **"AEF-1,"** a voluntary standard for independent third-party AI evaluations covering evaluator access, independence and transparency. ([Latent Space, Sep 15](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-aef-1-standard-emerges-for))
- **Anthropic commits to permanent evaluator access.** Anthropic said it will unilaterally give third-party evaluators, including safety group **METR**, permanent employee-level system access so outside verifiers can independently confirm its safety commitments are being met. ([Winzheng, Sep 13](https://www.winzheng.com/en/article/amodei-pace-the-frontier-ai-slowdown-third-party-evaluation))
- **Chip stocks sold off on slowdown fears.** AI-linked and semiconductor stocks fell sharply Monday as the essay raised concerns a coordinated pullback in AI capability growth could cool GPU demand — **Nvidia** fell nearly 3%, **Intel** dropped almost 6%, and **Micron** declined about 5% in premarket trading. ([CNN Business, Sep 14](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/14/business/ai-stocks-slide-slowdown-development-amodei-altman-intl))
- **Beijing casts the call as strategic.** China's state-backed Global Times characterized Amodei's essay as a strategy to preserve US dominance in AI rather than a genuine safety measure. ([Daily Caller, Sep 14](https://dailycaller.com/2026/09/14/china-dario-amodei-anthropic-safety-essay-cold-war/))
