# Morning Brief — Tuesday, August 25, 2026

*Washington opened an "economic D-Day" sanctions campaign on Iran that has Tehran threatening to shut the Strait of Hormuz, while the US-Canada tariff fight escalated to 50% duties — both landing the same week as Nvidia's earnings and new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's first Jackson Hole speech, making this the heaviest 48 hours of the year for markets and foreign policy together.*

## National

**Bottom line:** The Supreme Court handed Trump a win on mail-ballot restrictions while a separate ruling voided his 75-country visa ban, and the RNC now sits on an eight-figure cash advantage over a debt-laden DNC.

- **SCOTUS backs mail-voting limits.** In a **6-3** ruling along ideological lines, the Court lifted a Massachusetts judge's injunction that had blocked Trump's executive order restricting mail-in ballots ahead of the 2026 midterms in California and 22 other states; a separate, broader nationwide injunction from a different judge still limits the Postal Service's compliance, so the legal fight continues. ([NPR, Aug 24](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/24/nx-s1-5909592/trump-mail-voting-order-supreme-court))
- **Judge voids visa ban.** U.S. District Judge Jeannette Vargas ruled Trump's suspension of immigrant-visa processing for nationals of **75 countries** — including Afghanistan, Iran, Russia and Somalia — was "patently unlawful," finding Secretary of State Marco Rubio exceeded his statutory authority. ([CNN, Aug 22](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/08/22/politics/judge-voids-trumps-75-country-visa-ban-hnk))
- **DNC-RNC cash gap widens.** New FEC filings show the DNC holding just **$16.05 million** cash against **$17.9–18.3 million** in debt (including a $15 million loan collateralized by its own headquarters), versus the RNC's **$130.4 million** cash on hand with no debt. ([Washington Examiner, Aug 21](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/campaigns/congressional/4536836/rnc-holds-117-million-coffers-dnc-fundraising-still-in-debt/))
- **49ers owner pleads no contest.** San Francisco 49ers owner Jed York was arrested Sunday in East Palestine, Ohio, after responding to an ad as part of a human-trafficking sting; he pleaded no contest Monday to reduced misdemeanor charges, was fined **$1,150**, and was credited with time served. ([CBS Sports, Aug 24](https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/49ers-jed-york-arrested-ohio/))

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** Treasury unveiled an "economic D-Day" sanctions blitz on Iran that has Tehran threatening to close the Strait of Hormuz, the US-Canada trade war deepened into open hostility, and neither the Ukraine nor Gaza ceasefire situations show a stable truce.

- **Iran sanctions blitz.** Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent unveiled sweeping new sanctions Monday, dubbed "Operation Economic Outcast," targeting Iran's oil, shipping, gold, crypto and aviation revenue. Iran's Supreme National Security Council called cooperation with the sanctions an **"act of war"** and threatened to halt oil exports through the **Strait of Hormuz** if the pressure continues — a threat *as of Aug 24; unconfirmed since*. ([Al Jazeera, Aug 24](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/24/trump-administration-announces-global-economic-war-on-iran); [Washington Post, Aug 24](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/08/24/bessent-unveil-economic-d-day-sanctions-against-iran/))
- **US-Canada trade war deepens.** After talks collapsed Aug 21, the US imposed **50% tariffs on roughly $20 billion** of Canadian goods starting Aug 22. PM Mark Carney said the US wants to "destroy" Canadian firms and is weighing retaliation on autos on top of dollar-for-dollar countermeasures set to begin **Sept 8**. ([Bloomberg, Aug 24](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/carney-says-us-wants-to-destroy-canada-firms-examines-retaliation-moves); [Al Jazeera, Aug 22](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/22/us-imposes-50-tariffs-on-20bn-worth-of-canadian-goods-after-talks-fail))
- **New China tariffs floated.** The US is preparing a **7.5% tariff** on Chinese goods over alleged manufacturing overcapacity ahead of a planned Trump-Xi meeting next month — a fresh irritant even after the two leaders' May summit produced a soybean/rare-earths truce. ([Bloomberg, Aug 24](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/us-eyes-china-overcapacity-tariffs-of-7-5-before-xi-trump-talks))
- **Ukraine: no ceasefire, active fighting.** Russia struck Ukraine on its Independence Day (Aug 24), killing 8 and wounding 43, including hits on Odesa hypermarkets, while Ukraine struck a rocket-propellant plant in Russia's Rostov region overnight; Ukraine's Air Force logged **143 drones** and multiple missile types fired by Russia in the same period. *As of Aug 24.* ([Al Jazeera, Aug 24](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/14/ukraine-liberates-dnipropetrovsk-as-russia-intensifies-missile-attacks))
- **Gaza ceasefire fraying.** The nominal ceasefire (in place since Oct 10, 2025, now in its "phase two" disarmament stage) was violated again Aug 23 when Israeli strikes killed two Palestinians, including a child, in central/southern Gaza; aid groups say continued strikes have made the truce **"an illusion."** *As of Aug 23; unconfirmed since.* ([Middle East Monitor, Aug 23](https://www.middleeastmonitor.com/20260823-2-palestinians-including-child-killed-in-israeli-attacks-on-gaza-despite-ceasefire/))
- **Elections abroad.** Haiti holds general elections **Aug 30 and Dec 6**, its first since the 2021 assassination crisis, while pressure builds on Zelensky to hold wartime elections in Ukraine even as fighting continues. ([CNN, Aug 19](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/19/europe/ukraine-wartime-presidential-election-zelensky-intl))
