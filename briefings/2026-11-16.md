# Morning Brief — Monday, November 16, 2026

*This is one of the busiest weeks for AI and markets this year. Nvidia has a leadership crisis and reports earnings on Tuesday, and Anthropic plans to price its IPO on Wednesday and start trading on Thursday. All of it lands with the 10-year Treasury above 5% and a December Fed hike widely expected. Overseas, the Iran war has flared up again and Hormuz is still restricted ahead of Thursday's Xi-Trump-Putin meeting, which keeps oil, and with it cruise fuel costs, high.*

## Markets

**Bottom line:** Futures are soft and led lower by tech. The week turns on Nvidia's Tuesday earnings amid a CEO crisis, the Anthropic IPO, and Tuesday's retail sales report, all with 10-year yields above 5%.

- **Futures tilt lower.** Before the open, Nasdaq-100 futures are down about **0.7–0.9%**, S&P 500 futures are off about 0.2%, and Dow futures are roughly flat. Nvidia is lower premarket after the FT reported that Jensen Huang and his allies are discussing a campaign to reinstate him ([TipRanks, Nov 16](https://www.tipranks.com/news/stock-market-today-nov-16-u-s-stocks-mixed-ahead-of-nvidia-results-and-anthropic-ipo); [Bloomberg, Nov 16](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-15/stock-market-today-dow-s-p-live-updates)).
- **Friday close, strong week.** On Friday the S&P 500 fell 0.4% to **7,561.67**, the Nasdaq fell 0.9% to 25,136.27, and the Dow edged up to 52,347.55. For the week the S&P still gained **2.5%**, its first winning week since October and its biggest weekly gain since August. Tech led Friday's decline as Nvidia fell again, a day after Huang resigned ([AP via WTOP, Nov 13](https://wtop.com/national/2026/11/how-major-us-stock-indexes-fared-friday-11-13-2026/)). *One PRNewswire commentary gives slightly lower closes (S&P 7,535.72); the AP figures are the better-supported ones.*
- **Rates are the overhang.** The 10-year Treasury yield rose 3 bp on Friday and **27 bp on the week to 5.13%**, which keeps pressure on equity valuations and mortgage rates ([AP via WTOP, Nov 13](https://wtop.com/national/2026/11/how-major-us-stock-indexes-fared-friday-11-13-2026/)). The driver is a hotter-than-expected October CPI released Nov 10: headline **+0.4% vs 0.3% expected** and core +0.3% vs 0.2%. That strengthened expectations of a December 8–9 Fed hike from the current 3.75–4.00% range ([Fox Business, Nov 10](https://foxbusiness.com/economy/cpi-inflation-october-2026)). Abroad, an ECB policymaker called a December hike the baseline, so this is not just a US story ([Newsquawk, Nov 16](https://newsquawk.com/headlines/newsquawk-daily-us-opening-news---16th-november-2026)).
- **Oil stays elevated.** Brent is near **$95.49** and WTI near $88.56, slightly lower this morning but still high because Hormuz remains restricted (see Foreign Policy). That matters for airlines, cruise lines, and the inflation outlook ([Bloomberg, Nov 16](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-15/stock-market-today-dow-s-p-live-updates)).
- **Data and earnings this week.** October **advance retail sales** come out Tuesday at 8:30 a.m. ET ([FedRateCalc calendar](https://fedratecalc.com/us-economic-calendar/november-2026/)). **Nvidia** reports Tuesday ([TipRanks, Nov 16](https://www.tipranks.com/news/stock-market-today-nov-16-u-s-stocks-mixed-ahead-of-nvidia-results-and-anthropic-ipo)). A Fed minutes release is also expected this week, but its exact date and the retail sales consensus could not be confirmed. Asian markets were mostly lower overnight after weak Chinese activity data and a reported surprise contraction in Japan's Q3 GDP ([Newsquawk, Nov 16](https://newsquawk.com/headlines/newsquawk-daily-us-opening-news---16th-november-2026)).
- **Anthropic IPO.** The IPO is the largest test of AI appetite in public markets. Details are in the AI section.

**Bottom line:** The Iran war is active again and Hormuz is still restricted. Trump meets Xi and Putin in Shenzhen on Thursday, with nuclear arms and Iran on the agenda and Ukraine left off.

- **Iran war flares up.** On Nov 15, Bahrain said it intercepted Iranian munitions, and the IRGC said it hit a US-linked base there with drones. Saudi Arabia and the UAE condemned the strikes. Saudi-led forces reported intercepting missiles fired toward Riyadh ([Khaleej Times, Nov 15](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/mena/us-israel-iran-saudi-arabia-yemen-war-live-updates-november-15-2026); [Fox News, Nov 15](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/iran-war-strikes-donald-trump-strait-hormuz-irgc-november-15)). - **Xi-Trump-Putin in Shenzhen.** China confirmed the first three-way summit for **Thursday, Nov 19**, on the sidelines of APEC ([Hong Kong Free Press, Nov 16](https://hongkongfp.com/2026/11/16/china-confirms-first-xi-trump-putin-summit-in-shenzhen-on-thursday/)). Ukraine is largely off the agenda, though a Kremlin aide framed the talks as continuing toward a negotiated settlement. Trump also has a bilateral lunch with Xi that day and no separate meeting with Putin ([GreekReporter, Nov 16](https://greekreporter.com/2026/11/16/trump-xi-putin-summit/); [HNGN, Nov 14](https://www.hngn.com/articles/273717/20261114/china-confirms-talks-first-xi-putin-trump-summit-apec.htm)).
- **Tariffs at ASEAN.** Tariffs are central at the ASEAN summit, which Trump is expected to attend around **Nov 17**. Brazil's Lula said US tariffs pushed Brazil toward new trading partners ([Canadian Inquirer, Nov 16](https://canadianinquirer.net/2026/11/16/trumps-trade-tariffs-take-center-stage-at-asean-summit-as-leaders-seek-new-markets/)).
