## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The Strait of Hormuz remains shut by Iran's account, and talks are stalled but expected to resume this week — a state that has changed repeatedly, so treat it as fragile.

- **Hormuz still closed.** Iran says the strait stays closed until the US meets its conditions; Iran's parliament speaker said it will not open until seven conditions based on the "Islamabad memorandum" are met. *As of Oct 4; unconfirmed since.* ([Al Jazeera, Oct 4](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/4/iran-says-strait-of-hormuz-to-remain-closed-until-us-meets-conditions))
- **Trump rejects Iran's offer.** Tehran proposed reopening the strait in exchange for the US lifting its **naval blockade**, waiving sanctions, releasing about **$12 billion in frozen assets**, and a wider regional ceasefire including Lebanon. Trump told Axios it was "not the deal that I want to make," yet said he expects talks to resume this week. *As of Oct 4; unconfirmed since.* ([CBS News, Oct 4](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-strait-of-hormuz-7-day-proposal/))

- **US implications.** A closed Hormuz feeds oil and bunker-fuel prices, which bear directly on cruise cost guidance and consumer fuel prices.
