# Morning Brief — Friday, November 27, 2026

*Hot October inflation and a 4.3% GDP revision have made a **December Fed rate hike** close to certain. That leads this morning because it sets the price of money for everything below it, from AI-heavy equities to cruise-line financing. Futures point to a sharp rebound in today's half-day session, but oil is rising again on Hormuz tanker attacks.*

## Markets

**Bottom line:** Wednesday's AI-led crash mostly reversed by the close, futures are up strongly for today's 1 p.m. ET half-day, and the market now treats a December 9 rate hike as nearly settled.

- **Wednesday's whipsaw.** The Dow erased an intraday loss of about **1,100 points** and closed **up 0.33% at 50,182.95**. The S&P 500 fell 0.25% to 7,182.78 and the Nasdaq fell 0.62% to 23,585.80. Small caps outperformed, with the Russell 2000 up 0.71%, and most S&P stocks rose even as AI leaders sank ([AP via Yahoo Finance, Nov 25](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/world-indices/articles/major-us-stock-indexes-fared-211718440.html); [Motley Fool, Nov 25](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/25/stock-market-today-nov-25-dow-erases-1-100-point-loss-as-wall-street-stages-dramatic-late-day-comeback/)).
- **Why tech broke.** Three things hit at once: hot PCE data, the new Section 301 tariff proposal (see National), and renewed doubts about AI spending and profitability. Nvidia slid as much as 8.5% after the open, two days after a rally of more than 30% on **Jensen Huang's reinstatement as CEO** ([CNN, Nov 25](https://us.cnn.com/2026/11/25/business/ai-sell-off-despite-huang-nvidia-return); [CNBC, Nov 25](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/nvidia-ai-stock-tech-selloff.html)). Accounts of its close differ: one puts it **down 3.91% at $149.30** and CNBC says about 5% ([Gotrade, Nov 26](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/news/nvidia-nvda-stock-news-november-26-2026/)).
- **Credit flashing red on AI.** Default-swap spreads on AI-linked borrowers hit records. Oracle's five-year CDS reached about **356 bp** and CoreWeave's went above 1,000 bp, the level usually treated as distressed. Moody's and Fitch have not cut Oracle, which they rate two notches above junk ([Disruption Banking, Nov 25](https://www.disruptionbanking.com/2026/11/25/why-are-ai-credit-default-swaps-hitting-record-spreads/); [Esscher, Nov 26](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-26-daily-credit-default-swaps-on-ai-borrowers-reach-record-spreads/)). This is the funding risk underneath the equity volatility.
- **Data: hot across the board.** Q3 GDP was revised up to **4.3%** from 3.4%, ahead of the roughly 3.5% consensus, driven by investment (notably software) and government spending ([The Boston Report, Nov 25](https://thebostonreport.com/2026/11/25/us-gdp-q3-2026-second-estimate-4-3-percent)). October headline PCE rose 3.8% y/y, and core PCE came in at **3.3% vs. 3.2% expected**. Initial jobless claims fell to **189,000 vs. 200,000 expected**, tying the year's low ([Babypips, Nov 27](https://www.babypips.com/news/headline-the-feds-favorite-inflation-gauge-just-locked-in-a-december-hike-2026-11-27); [StockAnalysis, Nov 25](https://stockanalysis.com/news/jobless-claims-november-25-2026)).
- **Fed: hike priced in.** CME FedWatch-based estimates put the odds of a hike at the **Dec 8–9 FOMC** at about 94–97%, with a small chance of 50 bp. The policy rate has been 3.75–4.00% since the October hold ([Babypips, Nov 27](https://www.babypips.com/news/headline-the-feds-favorite-inflation-gauge-just-locked-in-a-december-hike-2026-11-27)). Higher-for-longer rates hit leveraged, long-duration stories hardest, which helps explain why AI names absorbed the selling.
- **Today's setup.** Early Friday, Nasdaq-100 futures were up about **2.8%**, S&P futures nearly 2% and Dow futures about 1.3%. The session closes at **1 p.m. ET** on thin volume, which can amplify moves ([TipRanks, Nov 27](https://tipranks.com/news/u-s-stock-futures-in-focus-ahead-of-a-shortened-black-friday-session)). Reports put the 10-year yield at about **4.89–4.92%**, up 4–6 bp. On oil, WTI is in the mid-$80s, while Brent quotes range from about $91 to $96 depending on source and timestamp ([Trading Economics, Nov 26](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/brent-crude-oil); [Invezz, Nov 26](https://invezz.com/news/2026/11/26/oil-price-today-hormuz-tanker-attacks-brent)).

## National

**Bottom line:** The administration opened a new tariff front against 14 trading partners, a federal judge blocked the FCC's review of ABC's licenses, and Thanksgiving football produced two historic comebacks.

- **Section 301 tariffs proposed.** USTR proposed excess-capacity tariffs on **14 economies**: China, the EU, Japan, India, South Korea, Taiwan, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Cambodia, Bangladesh and Switzerland. Mexico and Norway were investigated but left out for now ([Reuters via Investing.com, Nov 25](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/us-proposes-new-tariffs-on-14-trading-partners-to-fight-excess-factory-capacity-5050714)). China's proposed rate is 20% on top of existing first-term duties. Allies such as the EU, Japan, Korea, Switzerland and Taiwan face up to 15%, and Vietnam faces **up to 35%** without a deal. Goods already under Section 232 tariffs are exempt ([Global Trade & Sanctions Law, Nov 25](https://www.globaltradeandsanctionslaw.com/ustr-proposes-section-301-tariffs-on-14-economies-in-structural-excess-capacity-investigations/)). USTR's Jamieson Greer said they likely won't take effect for months ([CNBC, Nov 25](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/us-tariffs-trump-section-301.html)). *Reports differ on the China ceiling and start date.*
- **Judge blocks FCC on ABC.** U.S. District Judge Loren AliKhan issued a **preliminary injunction** halting the FCC's early review of ABC's broadcast licenses. She found the agency likely retaliated against protected speech and wrote that it was "no coincidence" the review came a day after Trump demanded Jimmy Kimmel's firing ([NPR, Nov 26](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/26/nx-s1-6000576/fcc-disney-abc-first-amendment-trump-brendan-carr)). The FCC called the ruling "wrong on the facts and wrong on the law" and expects to prevail ([Forbes, Nov 26](https://www.forbes.com/sites/siladityaray/2026/11/26/judge-blocks-trumps-fcc-from-targeting-disney-owned-abc-stations-this-is-not-a-close-case/)).
- **Economy in one line.** Growth is strong, inflation is sticky and layoffs are low, which is the mix pushing the Fed toward a hike. Details are in Markets.
- **Thanksgiving football.** The Bears came back from 11 points down in the fourth quarter to beat the Lions **39–35** and take first place in the NFC North. The Eagles rallied from 31–10 to win **39–38** in Dallas, the biggest comeback in Thanksgiving history. The Bills beat the Chiefs 13–10 to reach 10–1 ([NFL.com, Nov 26](https://www.nfl.com/news/thanksgiving-tripleheader-what-we-learned-from-bears-lions-eagles-cowboys-chiefs-bills); [Philadelphia Eagles, Nov 26](https://www.philadelphiaeagles.com/news/game-recap-eagles-at-cowboys-thanksgiving-2026-nfl-week-12-saquon-barkley-devonta-smith-joe-milton-dak-prescott)).

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The U.S. blockade of Iran remains in place with no talks scheduled, Hormuz attacks are at wartime highs and lifting oil, and Ukraine peace talks are stuck on territory.

- **Iran: blockade on, talks frozen.** *As of Nov 26*, Trump told troops on Thanksgiving that Washington is giving Tehran a chance to deal. He called the naval blockade "the most successful in maritime history" ([Qatar Tribune, Nov 26](https://www.qatar-tribune.com/article/266803/latest-news/we-are-seeking-agreement-with-iran-our-forces-will-continue-deployment-in-middle-east-trump)). Tehran's chief negotiator Qalibaf says there will be **no talks until seven conditions are met**. Those conditions have not been published, but state media cite lifting the blockade, removing sanctions and unfreezing assets ([US News/AP, Nov 24](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-11-24/iran-insists-no-talks-with-the-us-until-its-7-conditions-are-met-and-other-mideast-news)). Trump has said strikes could resume after the new year.
- **Hormuz: attacks escalating.** UKMTO classified the Nov 25 strike on an outbound tanker **18 nm east of Khasab, Oman** as an attack ([gCaptain, Nov 26](https://gcaptain.com/another-tanker-hit-in-strait-of-hormuz-as-maritime-attacks-surge-worldwide/)). Reported attacks in and around the strait are at their **highest level since the war began**, though a U.S. official says over 95% of ships pass without incident ([CNN, Nov 22](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/22/middleeast/hormuz-attacks-highest-levels-iran-war-intl)). Iran claims many mine strikes, but its counts are unverified and likely include duplicates. **The strait is not reopened under any agreement.** Trump has declared the earlier Islamabad memorandum void.
- **Ukraine: deadlock on territory.** U.S. envoy Jared Kushner said talks are **stuck on territory**. He blamed a December 2025 incident that both U.S. intelligence and Trump say was not an attack on Putin's residence ([Meduza, Nov 26](https://meduza.io/en/news/2026/11/26/kushner-blames-stalled-peace-talks-on-alleged-ukrainian-attack-on-putin-s-residence-an-attack-both-u-s-intelligence-and-trump-say-did-not-happen)). Trump called a deal a "strong possibility" and said he pressed Putin in China to halt aggression in Europe ([Washington Times, Nov 26](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/nov/26/trump-tells-putin-pull-back-military-aggression-europe-negotiate-deal/)). **No ceasefire is in place**, and Russia hasn't agreed to a proposed four-party round.
- **Trump–Rutte at Mar-a-Lago.** On Nov 25, Trump and NATO's Mark Rutte jointly urged Russia to avoid actions that would **"expand the war"**. They also discussed Iran and alliance defense production ([Washington Times, Nov 26](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/nov/26/natos-rutte-meets-trump-amid-conflicts-ukraine-iran/)).
- **Israel coalition-building.** Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar party and Yair Golan's Democrats announced a coalition agreement giving the Democrats **four ministries**. Yashar and United Torah Judaism reportedly agreed on principles for a Haredi draft law ([Times of Israel, Nov 26](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-november-26-2026/)).
- **Korea hikes.** The Bank of Korea raised its benchmark rate **25 bp to 3.25%** ([SBS, Nov 26](https://news.sbs.co.kr/english/article.do?news_id=N1008864795)). That makes another central bank tightening alongside the expected Fed move.

## Florida and Miami

**Bottom line:** DeSantis is fighting his own legislature over a special session to protect the Amendment 3 property-tax cut before he leaves office, and MIA got through a Thanksgiving-eve runway overrun with only a brief ground stop.

- **Amendment 3 standoff.** DeSantis is pushing for a **special session** before his term ends in January. He wants to stop local governments from raising other taxes and fees to make up for revenue lost under the property-tax cut voters approved with **62%** ([WLRN, Nov 27](https://wlrn.org/government-politics/2026-11-27/gov-desantis-pushes-for-special-session-on-property-tax-cut-fallout-as-lawmakers-hesitate)). Incoming Senate President Boyd and House Speaker Garrison say it can wait for the regular session. Whether DeSantis calls the session himself is unresolved. Miami-Dade and city budgets face the cuts directly.
- **MIA runway overrun.** A **FedEx MD-11** overran Runway 26R, the airport's shortest, at about 8:30 p.m. Wednesday in rain and wind. That closed every runway except 9R and triggered an FAA ground stop on one of the busiest travel nights of the year ([CBS Miami, Nov 26](https://www.cbsnews.com/miami/news/miami-international-airport-ground-stop-fedex-md-11-runway-excursion-faa/)). The NTSB is investigating. It is MIA's second cargo-jet overrun since September's fatal Amazon crash ([Simple Flying, Nov 26](https://simpleflying.com/fedex-mcdonnell-douglas-md-11-overshoots-runway-miami-triggers-ground-stop/)). *No injuries were reported in the coverage found.*
