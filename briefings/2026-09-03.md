# Morning Brief — Thursday, September 3, 2026

*The US struck Iranian government tankers near the **Strait of Hormuz** on September 1 under a new "tanker-for-tanker" policy, and the chokepoint remains effectively closed to normal traffic — the single biggest driver of oil prices, Treasury yields, and market risk sentiment this morning, compounded by a widening US naval campaign off Venezuela.*

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US is now engaged in active hostilities on two fronts — a shooting war with Iran that has shut Hormuz to normal commercial traffic, and an expanding naval campaign off Venezuela — while Ukraine's war grinds on unresolved and a US-Canada tariff deadline hits September 8.

- **Hormuz war escalates.** The US struck roughly **100 IRGC targets** around the Strait of Hormuz on September 1, including — for the first time — two Iranian government tankers hit in the engine room by drone-launched missiles, under a new Trump-approved "tanker-for-tanker" policy. *As of Sept 2–3*, the strait remains effectively closed to normal commercial traffic: only 8 vessels transited in a 24-hour window versus roughly 85/day pre-crisis, and Q2 oil flow fell to **4.9 million bpd** from over 20 million pre-crisis. ([Axios, Sep 2](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/02/iran-tankers-hormuz-attacks-oil)); ([Times of Israel, Sep 1–2](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-september-1-2026/))
- **Venezuela strikes widen.** Trump announced a September 2 US Navy strike on a boat off Venezuela allegedly run by Tren de Aragua, killing **11** — breaking from the prior Coast Guard-led interdiction norm and drawing legal questions since lethal force is normally reserved for imminent threats. The US then moved a fleet carrying **4,500+ sailors and Marines** toward Venezuelan waters. ([CBS News, Sep 2](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/boat-strike-sept-2-trump-administration-statements/)); ([ABC7/AP, Sep 2](https://abc7.com/post/president-nicols-maduro-says-venezuela-ready-respond-us-military-presence-caribbean/17722314/))
- **Ukraine strikes continue, talks frozen.** Russia fired 174 attack drones plus four missiles at Ukraine overnight into September 2, hitting central Kyiv and killing at least one; Putin ordered preparations for a new wave of strikes on Ukraine's energy grid. Peace talks remain stalled, with Russia's demand for all of Donbas the core sticking point. ([Al Jazeera, Sep 2](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/2/putin-and-zelenskyy-issue-threats-as-strikes-rock-ukraine-russia))
- **Canada tariff deadline nears.** After talks collapsed August 21, the US imposed **50% tariffs** on roughly $20B of Canadian goods; Canada's retaliatory tariffs (15–50% on 700+ US products) take effect **September 8**, with no deal yet reported. ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/economy/trump-canada-tariffs-carney-rcna593510)); ([Al Jazeera, Aug 22](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/22/carney-canada-will-enact-retaliatory-us-tariffs-starting-september-8))
- **Gaza ceasefire holds, violence continues.** Nearly a year into the October 2025 ceasefire, Israel controls *an estimated 54–70% of Gaza (sources conflict)*; on September 2, Israeli strikes/gunfire killed two Palestinians in Jabalia despite the ceasefire, part of a toll Gaza's Health Ministry puts at **1,326+** killed by "ceasefire violations" since October. ([Anadolu Agency, Sep 2](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/israel-kills-2-palestinians-in-strikes-gunfire-in-gaza-despite-ceasefire/4044987))
