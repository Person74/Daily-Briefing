# Morning Brief — Monday, November 23, 2026

*Markets lead. Futures are bouncing hard after the worst week for the Nasdaq and Dow in more than six years, which wiped out 2026's gains. Thanksgiving week is short, and PCE inflation lands Wednesday with a December Fed hike almost fully priced. In the background, attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz rose over the weekend to their highest level of the Iran war. That matters for oil and for cruise fuel costs.*

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** Attacks on shipping in Hormuz hit a war high over the weekend, and Trump said offensive action could resume if there is no Iran deal by year-end. Ukraine diplomacy is stalled under heavy Russian strikes, and COP31 collapsed.

- **Hormuz escalation.** Maritime risk firm Marisks says reported attacks in and around the Strait of Hormuz are at their **highest level since the Iran war began**. It counts three tankers hit by projectiles leaving the strait on Friday and Saturday, with Iranian involvement "assessed as likely." ([CNN, Nov 22](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/22/middleeast/hormuz-attacks-highest-levels-iran-war-intl)) The IRGC says **two more supertankers** struck mines in a "restricted zone" east of the strait, putting its own Nov 19–22 tally at **at least six** vessels. Accounts conflict: Iran blames mines, while UKMTO has confirmed projectile strikes in three incidents. ([Al Jazeera, Nov 21](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/tankers-hit-in-strait-of-hormuz-as-iran-tightens-grip-on-shipping); [The Hawk/IANS, Nov 23](https://www.thehawk.in/news/world/middle-east/irans-irgc-says-two-violating-supertankers-exploded-after-hitting-mines-in-hormuz-strait))
- **State of play with Iran.** *As of Nov 22 reporting:* the strait is effectively closed to normal traffic, and the **US naval blockade of Iranian ports** remains in place, with US escorts for tankers. Trump said he would consider **renewing offensive action** if no deal is reached **by year-end**. ([Times of Israel, Nov 22](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-november-22-2026/)) No talks are confirmed as scheduled. Tehran has made lifting the blockade a precondition. *Earlier reporting; unconfirmed since:* ([Al Jazeera, Nov 12](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/12/no-war-no-peace-can-iran-break-the-deadlock-with-the-us)) **US implication:** oil, shipping insurance and bunker costs. For cruise operators, Middle East and Suez repositioning remains impractical.
- **Ukraine.** Russia launched **one of its largest aerial assaults** of the war on Kyiv and other regions over the weekend. ([Situation Room brief, Nov 22](https://situationroom.io/brief/ukraine/2026-11-22-0604)) Zelensky called for **four-way talks** with the US, Europe and Russia "as soon as possible," ideally before Christmas. Russia still rejects a European seat at the table, and no date or venue for a next round has been set. ([Insider Paper, Nov 23](https://insiderpaper.com/zelensky-calls-for-four-way-talks-with-russia-as-soon-as-possible/))
- **COP31 collapses.** The Antalya climate summit ended Sunday **without adopting a political declaration**. It is the first such failure since Copenhagen in 2009 and, at 44 hours past its scheduled close, the longest COP on record. The EU and others rejected a draft that did not mention fossil fuels. Earlier COP commitments and frameworks remain in force. ([RTÉ, Nov 22](https://www.rte.ie/news/world/2026/1122/1600166-cop31-turkey/); [Al-Monitor, Nov 22](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/cop31-climate-talks-break-down-eu-and-others-reject-deal))
