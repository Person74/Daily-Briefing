## Florida and Miami

**Bottom line:** The Panhandle takes the hurricane; South Florida is sending responders, and Tallahassee has a Hope Florida court win and an execution warrant withdrawn.

- **Isaias and the state response.** Landfall is expected near the Panhandle late Friday or early Saturday, with storm surge of **5–7 feet from Ocean Springs to Indian Pass** and **4–8 inches of rain, locally up to 15**, from southern Alabama to the Panhandle.
