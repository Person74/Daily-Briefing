*Wall Street ended October with a sharp tech-led sell-off, a Fed leaning toward another rate hike and Brent near $117 on Iran/Hormuz risk, all heading into Tuesday's midterms. That combination leads because it moves rates, fuel costs and cruise equities at once.*

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** US–Iran diplomacy is live but unresolved, with Hormuz shipping still contested and a possible resumption of US strikes after the midterms.

- **Iran talks.** Qatari mediators returned to Tehran Oct 30 with a revised US draft; a response is expected around the weekend ([American Conservative, Oct 30](https://www.theamericanconservative.com/iran-war-day-245-qatari-mediators-in-tehran-as-renewal-of-war-looms/); [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-diplomacy-threat-resume-attacks/)). A reported framework would move Iran's enriched uranium abroad and reopen Hormuz within seven days in exchange for sanctions relief, but a senior Iranian official told Reuters Tehran's nuclear stance is unchanged and an unconfirmed Arabic-press report claims a preliminary understanding ([Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/world/us-delegation-expected-in-doha-for-direct-iran-talks-after-reported-breakthrough-reports)). *Status as of Oct 30; no deal confirmed.*
- **Hormuz.** CENTCOM says southern lanes are open and safe, while the IRGC said it stopped two tankers on "unauthorized routes" and a tanker was struck by a projectile ([The National, Oct 30](https://thenationalnews.com/business/markets/2026/10/30/oil-prices-set-for-sharp-monthly-rise-on-hormuz-strikes-and-us-threat-to-hit-iran)). *As of Oct 30; the passage is not normal.*
- **Ukraine energy truce fraying.** A US-brokered energy ceasefire announced Oct 11 has not held cleanly: Ukraine paused refinery strikes about three days at US request, said Russia kept hitting its grid ([Kyiv Independent, Oct 28](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-paused-attacks-on-oil-refineries-for-3-days-as-russia-continued-strikes-on-energy-infrastructure-zelensky-says/)), and on Oct 30 a Russian deputy PM confirmed strikes on three refineries ([Kyiv Independent, Oct 30](https://kyivindependent.com/russian-oil-refinery-reportedly-attacked-amid-renewed-ukrainian-strikes-on-energy-infrastructure/)). US implication: Washington is pressing Kyiv to lower fuel prices before the midterms.
- **Tariffs.** The Section 122 10% global tariff expired July 24 after the Supreme Court struck down IEEPA tariffs in February; I could not find reporting confirming what currently replaces it or the status of the US–China truce, so **no current tariff state is asserted** ([Allianz, Jul 22](https://www.allianz.com/content/dam/onemarketing/azcom/Allianz_com/economic-research/publications/specials/en/2026/july/2026_07_22_Trade-war-update.pdf)). *Unconfirmed since July.*
