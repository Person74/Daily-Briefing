## National

**Bottom line:** July private hiring badly missed expectations, setting up a high-stakes jobs report Friday, while Washington juggles a 25-state tariff lawsuit, a Fauci contempt vote, and the ballooning cost of the National Guard's D.C. deployment.

- **ADP hiring miss.** Private employers added just **44,000 jobs** in July, far below the ~70,000 consensus and down from June's revised 95,000 — the weakest month of the year. Services carried all the growth (education/health +36,000) while goods-producing industries and leisure/hospitality shed jobs; annual pay growth held at 4.4%. Friday's official BLS report (8:30am ET) is expected to show **+120,000** payrolls and unemployment ticking up to **4.3%**. ([CNBC, Aug 5](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/private-companies-added-just-44000-workers-in-july-below-expectations-adp-reports.html))

It follows February's Supreme Court ruling striking down the original "Liberation Day" tariffs, after which the administration shifted to new legal authorities to keep tariff revenue flowing. ([Al Jazeera, Aug 4](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/trump-administration-sued-by-25-states-over-new-tariffs-on-trading-partners))
- **Fauci contempt vote.** The Senate Homeland Security Committee, chaired by Rand Paul, was voting Thursday morning on holding Anthony Fauci in contempt of Congress after he invoked the Fifth Amendment 111 times at a subpoenaed hearing. *Result not yet confirmed as of publication.* ([Washington Post, Aug 6](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/08/06/fauci-congress-justice-department/7386102a-914c-11f1-9fdc-0a725c989a7b_story.html))

Haley Stevens **48.5%–47.5%** in the Aug 4 Democratic primary, overcoming ~$65 million in outside spending against him. He faces Republican Mike Rogers in a top-tier midterm battleground. - **Hormuz deal "close" but not done.** *As of Aug 5; unconfirmed since.* Bessent said a deal to fully reopen the strait could land "today or tomorrow," but Iran's demand for a new governance/transit-fee regime — opposed by Oman — remains unresolved as of this morning. ([Washington Post, Aug 5](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/08/05/taiwan-han-kuang-drills-china-military/9264a176-90ae-11f1-9fdc-0a725c989a7b_story.html))

## Cruise Industry

- **Carnival upgraded, guidance trimmed.** S&P upgraded Carnival's credit to **BBB-** on strong forward bookings (93% of 2026 inventory booked), but the company cut FY26 net-yield guidance to **1.75%** growth from 2.75%, citing softer close-in Mediterranean demand tied to Middle East conflict.
