## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US-Iran war has passed six months with the Strait of Hormuz still a contested, partially blockaded chokepoint keeping oil markets on edge, while the Gaza ceasefire frays, Ukraine peace talks inch forward without a deal, and a US-Canada tariff war and Panama Canal drought cuts add fresh friction to global shipping.

- **Strait of Hormuz remains contested.** *As of Aug 29:* Iran's IRGC navy asserts "decisive control" of the strait while US Central Command says it is enforcing a naval blockade of Iranian ports and disputes Iran's claim. Traffic is running far below the pre-war baseline of ~130 ships/day (about 236 total transits over 19 days in August); at least 6,000 seafarers remain stranded in the Gulf and 19 have been killed since the war began Feb 28. Direct US military and sanctions pressure are driving the standoff. ([Al Jazeera, Aug 29](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/8/29/iran-war-live-irgc-asserts-strait-control-us-enforces-blockade); [Fox News, Aug 29](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/iran-war-us-sanctions-trump-strait-hormuz-oman-oil-prices-08-29-2026))
- **Ukraine: no ceasefire, but a new push.** Fighting continued into Aug 29 — Russian missile and drone strikes hit Ukraine (including a Kyiv-region fire that injured six) and Ukrainian strikes hit targets in Russia's Rostov Oblast. Diplomatically, US envoys Witkoff and Kushner have been coordinating a proposed partial *air* ceasefire that officials believe Moscow may now be more open to. *As of Aug 29, this remains a proposal, not an agreed truce.* ([Kyiv Post, Aug 29](https://www.kyivpost.com/thread/83300))
- **Gaza ceasefire holding on paper, fraying in practice.** The October 2025 ceasefire and Trump's 20-point plan remain nominally in force, but implementation has stalled for three months. *As of Aug 28*, the IDF reported killing Hamas/Islamic Jihad operatives in Gaza despite US calls to halt strikes, and a separate strike killed a Hamas commander in the West Bank; Hamas has not disarmed and Israel still controls significant portions of Gaza. ([Times of Israel, Aug 28](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-august-28-2026/))
- **US-Canada trade war reignited.** Talks collapsed Aug 21-22 over critical minerals and sovereignty disputes; the US has imposed **50% tariffs** on roughly $20B of Canadian goods, and PM Carney says Canada will retaliate "dollar for dollar" starting **Sept 8** on steel, dairy, appliances, and other goods. Separately, the US is preparing a 7.5% "overcapacity" tariff on Chinese goods ahead of a planned Xi-Trump summit, which would push total China duties back to roughly 20%. ([CNN, Aug 21](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/21/economy/us-canada-trade); [Bloomberg, Aug 24](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/us-eyes-china-overcapacity-tariffs-of-7-5-before-xi-trump-talks))
- **Shipping chokepoints under fresh strain.** El Niño-driven drought will cut daily Panama Canal transits from 36 to **34 starting Sept 3**, and to 32 from Sept 15, with 131 vessels already queued as of Aug 18. In the Red Sea, Suez Canal transits are gradually recovering (1,088 transits Jul 20-Aug 16, up from 1,070 the prior month) but remain fragile — Houthi forces claimed an attack on a Saudi-linked tanker off Yanbu around Aug 24, tied to the broader Iran war spillover. *Both are evolving states worth monitoring for itinerary/routing impact.* ([Al Jazeera, Aug 21](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/21/panama-canal-to-limit-shipping-ahead-of-extreme-weather-during-el-nino); [Al Jazeera, Aug 24](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/24/yemens-houthis-report-attack-on-saudi-ship))
