# Morning Brief — Wednesday, October 21, 2026

*The Strait of Hormuz is again the biggest swing factor: new tanker strikes and mine reports on Oct 20–21 pushed Brent back near $110, while Treasury yields above 5% and a Fed decision next week frame everything in markets.*

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** No US–Iran ceasefire is confirmed in force, Hormuz shipping is under fire again, and Trump's waiver of Russia sanctions has opened a rift with Congress and Kyiv.

- **Hormuz tanker attacks.** Reports from Oct 20–21 describe several incidents: Iran's Revolutionary Guards said mines stopped three tankers on routes Tehran calls unauthorized, and a supertanker carrying **over 2 million barrels** reportedly exploded after hitting a mine (Iranian claim, not independently confirmed). Separately, UKMTO said an unidentified projectile hit a tanker **11 nautical miles west of Bukha, Oman**, with all crew safe. Brent rose toward **$109.73** early Wednesday. ([CNBC, Oct 21](https://cnbc.com/2026/10/21/oil-price-today-wti-brent-rise-on-middle-east-worries.html); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/middle-east-war-iran-rejects-us-terms-as-tanker-explodes-near-hormuz-1.500717832))
- **Iran talks status.** Iran's foreign minister says Hormuz stays closed unless Washington accepts Tehran's conditions, and indirect talks via Qatar and Pakistan continue. Trump has reportedly pledged no strikes before the **Nov 3** midterms while the Pentagon prepares for possible renewed combat. *As of reports dated Oct 19–21; the pledge's original date is unverified, so confirm before relying on it.* ([CBS News live updates](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-talks-russia-strait-of-hormuz/))
- **Russia sanctions waiver.** Trump signed an order waiving the new **Graham Act** sanctions and tariffs two days before they took effect, citing national interest and ongoing peace talks; coverage also ties it to lowering fuel prices before the midterms. Zelenskyy called it "wrong," and Republican senators including McConnell, Tillis, Collins and Cornyn urged enforcement. ([CNBC, Oct 17](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/17/trump-waives-russia-related-sanctions-tariffs-before-they-take-effect-.html); [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-signs-order-waiving-graham-act-sanctions-russia/story?id=137341529))
