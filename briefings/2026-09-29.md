Administrator Bedford said the agency has not concluded whether it is a safety-of-flight issue. **Bottom line:** The Iran war and the Strait of Hormuz remain unresolved, with Trump rejecting Iran's proposal but signaling talks this week; the China truce was extended but produced little substance.

([Axios, Sep 27](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/27/trump-iran-war-hormuz-blockade-negotiations)) Iran had offered to reopen the strait and restart nuclear talks in a week if the US lifted its naval blockade, eased oil sanctions and reinstated a regional ceasefire; Washington wants nuclear concessions.
