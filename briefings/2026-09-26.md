# Morning Brief — Saturday, September 26, 2026

*The seven-month-old war with Iran remains the biggest swing factor in the global economy — the Strait of Hormuz is still effectively closed, the Senate just voted to keep US strikes going, and the resulting oil and yield pressure is landing on a Fed that only started hiking again this month.*

## National

**Bottom line:** The Senate narrowly voted to continue military action against Iran, the Fed delivered its first rate hike since 2023, and government funding is locked in through December — all against a backdrop of playoff baseball and a winless Dolphins team hosting the NFL's last unbeaten squad.

- **Senate blocks Iran resolution.** The Senate voted **49-50** on Thursday to reject a concurrent resolution that would have directed Trump to end US military action against Iran, part of a naval blockade and air campaign now in its **seventh month**. Four Republicans (Collins, Murkowski, Paul, Tillis) crossed over to support it; Democrat John Fetterman voted against. ([CNBC, Sep 16](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-september-2026.html))
- **Government funded to Dec. 11.** Trump signed a stopgap on Sept. 2 keeping the government open through **Dec. 11**, timed to keep a shutdown fight off the ballot ahead of the midterms. *No newer funding action has occurred since.* ([CNN, Sep 1](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/01/politics/congress-government-shutdown-midterms))

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The Strait of Hormuz remains closed with US-Iran talks underway but no deal, Trump and Xi wrapped a summit heavy on ceremony and light on substance, and fighting continues in both Ukraine and Gaza despite nominal truces.

- **Hormuz still closed.** The strait has been almost entirely shut since the US and Israel launched an air war on Iran on **Feb. 28** — the largest oil-supply disruption on record, per the IEA, with just one transit recorded Sept. 20 versus 85/day pre-crisis. Iran's foreign minister has floated a phased seven-day reopening in exchange for the US lifting its naval blockade and sanctions; Bloomberg reports both sides are "exploring" it but *no breakthrough has been confirmed as of Sept. 25*. ([Al Jazeera, Sep 25](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/25/whats-in-irans-seven-day-plan-to-reopen-the-strait-of-hormuz), [Bloomberg, Sep 24](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-24/us-iran-said-to-be-exploring-phased-deal-to-open-hormuz))
- **Trump-Xi summit ends light.** Xi's three-day state visit to Washington concluded Sept. 25 with an extension of the existing trade truce but little movement on AI or Taiwan; USTR Jamieson Greer said details of a deal covering a "subset" of goods will land **Monday, Sept. 28**. ([CNBC, Sep 25](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/25/trump-xi-summit-takeaways.html))
- **Ukraine fighting intensifies, not paused.** Despite a brief pause on long-range strikes brokered by US envoys in early September, Russia launched **295 drones** overnight into Sept. 25, killing seven in Kyiv; Ukraine struck fuel plants in three Russian regions. Zelensky said Trump made a "final decision" to license Ukraine's own Patriot-interceptor production. ([Kyiv Post, Sep 25](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/85442))
- **Gaza ceasefire "in name only."** Nearing its one-year mark, the truce continues alongside daily violations — Israeli gunfire, shelling and demolitions injured Palestinians on Sept. 25, and Gaza's health ministry says **1,300+** have been killed since the ceasefire began. ([WBUR, Sep 23](https://www.wbur.org/hereandnow/2026/09/23/gaza-ceasefire-one-year), [Times of Israel, Sep 25](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-september-25-2026/))
- **New tariffs land next week.** Revised **50%** Section 338 tariffs on Canadian goods (in effect since Sept. 15) tighten further with a product prohibition starting Sept. 29; Canada has retaliated on 700+ US products. A **100%** Section 232 pharmaceutical tariff extends to all remaining drugmakers the same day. ([Trade Compliance Resource Hub, Sep 10](https://www.tradecomplianceresourcehub.com/2026/09/10/trump-2-0-tariff-tracker/))
