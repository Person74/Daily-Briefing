## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The Strait of Hormuz remains effectively closed to normal traffic amid the reignited Iran war, Washington is preparing a fresh China tariff ahead of a Trump-Xi summit, and the Panama Canal starts cutting transit capacity this week — a trio of shipping-lane and trade frictions that bear directly on fall cruise and cargo routing.

- **Strait of Hormuz effectively shut.** *As of Aug 31; unconfirmed since.* Iran's Deputy FM says vessels transit the strait only "in coordination with Iran"; just ~3 ships transited on Aug 23 versus a normal ~85/day. Roughly **6,000 sailors** remain stranded in the Persian Gulf, at least 70 attacks on shipping have been recorded since the war began, and 19 seafarers have been killed. Oman-Iran talks on a toll-based temporary corridor continue but require the US to lift its naval blockade. ([Fox News, Aug 29](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/iran-war-us-sanctions-trump-strait-hormuz-oman-oil-prices-08-29-2026)) ([CNN, Aug 18](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/18/business/iran-strait-of-hormuz-oil))
- **New China tariff ahead of Trump-Xi summit.** The US is preparing a **7.5% "overcapacity" tariff** on Chinese goods, on top of the 10-12.5% Section 301 tariffs from July, timed not to derail a planned late-September Trump-Xi meeting. The move would push effective US tariffs on China back toward **~20%**; China already faces the highest effective US tariff rate of any major partner. ([Bloomberg/US News, Aug 24](https://www.usnews.com/news/top-news/articles/2026-08-24/us-eyes-china-overcapacity-tariffs-of-7-5-before-xi-trump-talks-bloomberg-news-reports))
- **Panama Canal caps transits starting this week.** The Panama Canal Authority will cap daily transits at **34 vessels** from September 4 (dropping to 32 on Sept 15) as El Niño-driven drought leaves watershed rainfall 34% below average — only the second capacity cap in the canal's history, raising booking-delay and premium-pricing risk for fall cruise and cargo routing. ([Al Jazeera, Aug 21](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/21/panama-canal-to-limit-shipping-ahead-of-extreme-weather-during-el-nino))
- **Ukraine talks: no restart confirmed.** Ukrainian presidential aide Kyrylo Budanov said Kyiv hopes trilateral Ukraine-Russia-US talks can resume in September; Russia has not confirmed, and FM Lavrov says Moscow won't accept a ceasefire that freezes the current front line. No ceasefire is in effect and fighting continues. ([Kyiv Post, Aug 27](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/83218))
- **Suez still diverted.** *As of late Aug; unconfirmed since.* Suez transits remain roughly 60% below pre-crisis levels, with most carriers still running the longer Cape of Good Hope route; industry expects the diversion to persist through at least 2027. ([Splash247/BIMCO](https://splash247.com/suez-traffic-still-60-down-100-days-after-last-houthi-attack/))
