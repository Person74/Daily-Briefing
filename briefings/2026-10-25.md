## Weather and Disruptions

**Bottom line:** Expect a wet, flood-prone Monday commute in Miami-Dade, with king-tide peaks Oct 26-27; the Atlantic tropics are quiet.

- **Flood watch and king tides.** The NWS Coastal Flood Watch covers Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach and the Keys from **2 a.m. Sunday to 8 p.m. Monday**, with the threat running through **Tuesday, Oct 27** ([Hoodline, Oct 2026](https://hoodline.com/2026/10/king-tides-storms-raise-flooding-risk-near-miami-beach-this-weekend/); [CiberCuba, Oct 24](https://en.cibercuba.com/noticias/2026-10-24-u1-e43231-s27061-nid343675-lluvias-intensas-rafagas-40-mph-mareas-reales-sur)). Friday's high tides already put inches of water on streets in Miami-Dade and Broward ([WSVN, Oct 2026](https://wsvn.com/news/local/miami-dade/king-tides-flood-south-florida/)). Sources disagree on the peak day (Oct 26-27) and on rainfall: one report says up to **7 inches** with **40 mph gusts**, others far less, so treat totals as unsettled.
- **Forecast.** NBC6 (updated Oct 24) shows on-and-off storms Sunday and Monday, **70% rain chance each day, highs near 84°** ([NBC 6, Oct 24](https://www.nbcmiami.com/weather/nbc-6-todays-forecast-first-alert-weather-south-florida/1883450/)). Weather Underground is cooler, with an 80° high and 74° low. Plan for low-lying streets near the river and Brickell to pond at high tide.
- **Tropics quiet.** The NHC's 8 a.m. EDT outlook on Oct 24 expects **no Atlantic, Caribbean or Gulf formation over the next 7 days** ([NHC, Oct 24](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/gtwo.php?basin=atlc&fdays=7)). Hurricane Vance is active in the Eastern Pacific only.
