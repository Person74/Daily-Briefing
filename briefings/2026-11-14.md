# Morning Brief — Saturday, November 14, 2026

*A nationwide FAA ground stop on Friday cancelled thousands of flights, and airlines are still catching up today. It leads because it affects travel now and raises questions about the air traffic control system. Nvidia's leadership crisis is the main market story going into a week with Nvidia's earnings Tuesday and Anthropic's IPO pricing Wednesday.*

## National

**Bottom line:** Air travel is still recovering from Friday's first nationwide ground stop since 2023, Nvidia's CEO has resigned in a scandal, and Democrats flipped a Mississippi Senate seat.

- **FAA ground stop.** The FAA stopped departing flights nationwide on Friday afternoon, citing a communications failure at air traffic control centers. The stop was **lifted after roughly four to five hours** ([Axios, Nov 13](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/13/faa-nationwide-ground-stop); [CNN, Nov 13](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa)). The cause is still unsettled. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy blamed overloaded routers and outdated radar-communications equipment. DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin pointed to clashes between old equipment and new technology and said there is no evidence of a cyberattack. Some controllers reportedly "don't buy" Duffy's explanation ([CNN, Nov 13](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa)). Friday ended with roughly **9,100 cancellations**. By 6 a.m. today another **3,374 cancellations** were logged, and Duffy said a return to full capacity will "take us some time" ([ABC7, Nov 14](https://abc7ny.com/live-updates/faa-ground-stop-live-updates-what-know-nationwide-flights-are-grounded/20038887/)).
- **Huang out at Nvidia.** Nvidia's board said **Jensen Huang resigned** as president, CEO and director, effective immediately. CFO **Colette Kress is interim CEO** while the board searches inside and outside the company ([TechCrunch, Nov 12](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/nvidias-jensen-huang-resigns-chipmaker-names-cfo-colette-kress-as-interim-ceo/)). The company gave no reason. Press reports tie his exit to an admitted affair and reporting that he was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. Those allegations remain unproven ([ABC News, Nov 12](https://abcnews.com/Business/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-amid-alleged-affair/story?id=138076256); [Al Jazeera, Nov 12](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/12/why-did-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resign-and-who-is-colette-kress)). Q3 results are still scheduled for Tuesday.
- **Microsoft 365 outage.** A global outage starting around 1 p.m. ET Thursday took down Outlook/Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint and Copilot. Microsoft says **there is no sign of a cyberattack** and calls it a technical issue. It has promised a full post-incident report ([BleepingComputer, Nov 12](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/massive-microsoft-365-outage-disrupts-outlook-teams-copilot-worldwide/); [Forbes, Nov 13](https://www.forbes.com/sites/kateoflahertyuk/2026/11/13/microsoft-outage-what-happened-and-what-to-do-next/)). By Friday, service was "approaching normal levels" ([CNN, Nov 13](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/12/business/live-news/microsoft-global-outage-intl-hnk)).
- **Mississippi Senate flip.** Democrat **Scott Colom defeated Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith** by about **5,000 votes** after a week of counting affidavit and absentee ballots. He will be the state's first Democratic senator since 1982 ([NPR, Nov 11](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/11/nx-s1-6037840/democrat-scott-colom-wins-the-closest-senate-race-in-the-country)). NBC reports Democrats will hold **at least 55 seats** in January, with Alaska still uncalled ([NBC News, Nov 11](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/scott-colom-mississippi-senate-election-win-democrat-hyde-smith-rcna605729)). *The results reviewed do not say whether Hyde-Smith has conceded.*
- **Hegseth impeachment fails.** A House effort to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth failed this week ([Democracy Now, Nov 13](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/13/headlines)).
