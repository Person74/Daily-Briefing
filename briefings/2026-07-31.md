## National

**Bottom line:** The Fed held rates steady in a rare three-way hawkish dissent, GDP growth slowed sharply in Q2, and stocks staged a huge rebound on Microsoft's earnings — a mixed macro picture the market is choosing to read bullishly for now.

- **Fed holds, dissents want a hike.** The FOMC voted **9-3** on Jul 29 to hold its benchmark rate at **3.5%–3.75%** for a fifth straight meeting, but three regional bank presidents (Cleveland's Beth Hammack, Minneapolis's Neel Kashkari, Dallas's Lorie Logan) dissented in favor of *raising* rates, citing inflation stuck above the Fed's 2% target for five-plus years. Chair Kevin Warsh called it "a good family fight." ([CNBC, Jul 29](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/fed-rate-decision-july-2026.html))
- **GDP growth slows to 1.5%.** Commerce Department's advance Q2 estimate showed the economy growing at a **1.5%** annualized rate, down from 2.1% in Q1 and below the 2.1% economists expected, dragged down by an **11.5%** import surge (largely AI chips); consumer spending, by contrast, accelerated to a robust 3.2% pace. ([CBS News, Jul 30](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/gdp-economic-growth-second-quarter-2026/))
- **Stocks rally hard, Microsoft leads.** The Nasdaq jumped **2.8%** to 25,122.18, the Dow rose 1.2% to 52,208.06, and the S&P 500 gained 1.7% to 7,437.63 on Jul 30, reversing the post-Fed selloff. Microsoft alone added roughly **$450 billion** in market value after Azure cloud revenue topped $100 billion annually for the first time. ([CNBC, Jul 30](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/stock-market-today-live-updates.html))
- **AG nomination in jeopardy.** The Senate Judiciary Committee postponed its vote on Todd Blanche's attorney general nomination after GOP Senators Cornyn and Tillis withheld support pending written DOJ assurances on a $1.8 billion Trump-IRS settlement fund; with August recess looming, Trump has floated pulling the nomination. *As of Jul 30.* ([CNN, Jul 29](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/29/politics/todd-blanche-nomination-cornyn))

([AP/Record-Eagle, Jul 30](https://www.record-eagle.com/sports/today-in-sports-07-30-2026/article_79c29961-61cd-4443-85be-627d607eddfe.html))

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US-Iran war is escalating regionally with the Strait of Hormuz effectively closed to normal traffic, Gaza ceasefire talks are showing fragile progress without a final deal, and Trump's new 50% Canada tariffs take effect Aug 19.

- **US-Iran war spreads regionally.** US forces conducted a "heavy wave" of strikes on Iran overnight into Jul 30 (Bandar Abbas, Kish Island, missile/drone facilities) after an Iranian ballistic-missile attack on US positions; the conflict, running since **Feb 28, 2026**, is now pulling in neighbors — an Iranian strike hit a Chinese-owned building in Kuwait, Jordan shot down five Iranian missiles, and a drone struck a US-owned fuel tanker at Egypt's Damietta port, the first such hit on Egypt. *As of Jul 30:* Iran says the **Strait of Hormuz remains closed** to normal traffic (~15 vessels transiting in 24 hours vs. a ~120 pre-war average), and the US Treasury added fresh sanctions on Iranian tanker networks. ([CNN, Jul 30](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/30/world/live-news/iran-war-trump))
- **Gaza talks show "rare progress," no deal yet.** Egyptian, Qatari and Turkish mediators met Hamas in Cairo on phase two of Trump's peace plan, reportedly moving toward a weapons-decommissioning roadmap, though an Israeli official called current terms "not satisfactory." *As of Jul 30*, only the fragile October 2025 truce holds — Israeli strikes killed at least six Palestinians in Gaza that day alone, and aid remains below the agreed 600-truck daily target. ([Al Jazeera, Jul 30](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/30/israeli-strikes-kill-at-least-four-including-children-across-gaza-strip))
- **No Russia-Ukraine ceasefire; Poland scrambles jets.** *As of Jul 30*, only temporary humanitarian pauses have occurred in 2026 (Orthodox Easter, Victory Day) — no standing ceasefire exists. Overnight into Jul 30, Russia launched more than **70 missiles** and roughly 280 drones at Ukraine, killing eight, forcing Poland to scramble fighter jets to secure its airspace. ([CNN, Jul 30](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/07/30/world/live-news/iran-war-trump))
- **New 50% Canada tariffs land Aug 19.** Trump signed proclamations on Jul 20 imposing an additional **50% tariff** on select Canadian goods (wine, hockey sticks, cement), effective **Aug 19**; separately, 10–12.5% tariffs apply across roughly 60 major trading partners, and a Section 301 probe targets EU treatment of Google, Apple, Meta and Amazon. ([White House, Jul 2026](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/07/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-imposes-additional-tariffs-on-canada/))
- **Zambia, Haiti elections ahead; Cyprus talks revived.** Zambia holds combined presidential/parliamentary elections **Aug 13**, watched as a regional democratic bellwether, with Haiti voting **Aug 30**; UN Secretary-General Guterres announced new talks aimed at breaking the long diplomatic stalemate over divided Cyprus. ([CNN, Jul 30](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/07/30/world/live-news/iran-war-trump))
