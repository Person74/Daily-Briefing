## Weather and Disruptions

**Bottom line:** Expect flooded low-lying streets around high tide (about 10:56 a.m. today) and windy, humid weather with a few showers; check routes before leaving.

- **Record king tide.** Miami and Fort Lauderdale each recorded **over two feet of water above normal high tide on Tuesday**, which coverage describes as the most floodwater Miami has seen outside a hurricane (other reports say about 2.5 feet, so the exact figure varies). NWS Miami said flooding would peak Tuesday and Wednesday, with **coastal flood warnings from Palm Beach County to the upper Keys through Wednesday**. Saltwater stalled cars and closed streets such as North Bay Road on Miami Beach. ([CBS Miami, Oct 27](https://www.cbsnews.com/miami/news/south-florida-king-tide-fort-lauderdale-miami-october-2026/); [Tampa Bay Times, Oct 27](https://tampabay.com/news/environment/2026/10/27/king-tide-south-florida-flooding-miami-fort-lauderdale))
- **Cause and outlook.** University of Miami's Brian McNoldy said a passing low-pressure system was "bulldozing water onto the coast" on top of the tide. Conditions are expected to improve gradually, but elevated peak tides are forecast **through Nov. 12**, with Miami high tides at 10:11 p.m. Tuesday and **10:56 a.m. Wednesday**. ([iHeart, Oct 28](https://www.iheart.com/content/2026-10-28-florida-king-tides-cause-record-flooding/); [CBS Miami, Oct 27](https://www.cbsnews.com/miami/news/south-florida-king-tide-fort-lauderdale-miami-october-2026/))
- **Forecast.** AccuWeather's Miami page showed a high near **85°F**, windy and humid, with a couple of showers, plus dangerous surf and coastal flooding this week. I could not retrieve the official NWS forecast, so treat precipitation odds and wind speeds as unconfirmed. ([AccuWeather, Oct 27](https://www.accuweather.com/en/us/miami/33128/weather-forecast/347936))
- **Tropics.** The most recent NHC outlook I could find (Oct 16) expected no tropical cyclone formation over the following 7 days. *As of Oct 16; I could not confirm today's outlook, so check nhc.noaa.gov.* ([NHC, Oct 16](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/text/refresh/MIATWOAT+shtml/150537_MIATWOAT.shtml))
