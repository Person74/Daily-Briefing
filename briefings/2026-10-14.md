## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The Iran war is still choking Hormuz and lifting oil, Trump's claimed Russia–Ukraine energy truce is contested, and the US–China trade truce runs to January 10.

- **Hormuz and Iran.** Oil climbed Wednesday after **two vessels were struck in the Strait of Hormuz**, and Iranian officials told Reuters they are using new tactics against ships. Attacks have risen over the past ten days, and some analysts say Tehran wants high oil prices to influence the US midterms ([Arab News, Oct 14](https://www.arabnews.com/energy/oil-prices-rise-as-iran-deploys-new-tactics-to-target-ships-transiting-hormuz-3006260); [Euronews, Oct 14](https://euronews.com/2026/10/14/oil-prices-rise-after-reported-attacks-on-ships-in-strait-of-hormuz)). - **Saudi–Houthi front.** Saudi officials said **Aramco facilities were attacked**, and a Saudi-led coalition struck Houthi energy terminals and fuel reserves in Yemen ([CNBC, Oct 14](https://cnbc.com/2026/10/14/oil-rises-amid-growing-tensions-between-saudis-and-iran-backed-houthis.html)). The IEA's monthly report also weighed on the market.
- **Ukraine energy truce.** Trump said Oct 11 that Russia and Ukraine agreed to an **immediate energy ceasefire**. Russia has not confirmed it, Lavrov vowed to press on, and Zelensky said Russia hit six energy facilities on Monday ([Axios, Oct 11](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/11/trump-russia-ukraine-energy-ceasefire-zelensky); [Al Jazeera, Oct 13](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/10/13/trump-insists-energy-ceasefire-holds-as-ukraine-reports-new-russian-strikes)). - **Russian diesel sanctions.** Trump announced a deal to **ease sanctions on Russian diesel exports** to lower fuel prices, which angered Ukraine, and lawmakers in both parties say it appears to violate US law ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/ukraine/trump-russia-ukraine-agree-energy-ceasefire-zelenskyy-diesel-rcna602766); [Al Jazeera, Oct 13](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/10/13/trump-insists-energy-ceasefire-holds-as-ukraine-reports-new-russian-strikes)).

They also extended the pause on escalating tariffs until **January 10** ([Axios, Sep 26](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/26/us-china-tariffs-trade-30-billion); [CNN, Sep 28](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/28/business/us-china-tariff-cuts-60-billion-intl)). *I found nothing newer; unconfirmed since Sep 28.*
