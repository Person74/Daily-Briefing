# Morning Brief — Sunday, November 8, 2026

*Wall Street is digesting a violent week: a post-midterm rally reversed into an AI-led selloff that put the Nasdaq in correction, and Tuesday's CPI now decides whether the Fed hikes again in December. It leads because it moves everything else on this page, including cruise equities.*

## Markets

**Bottom line:** An AI/chip meltdown erased the midterm rally and left stocks down for the week, while a hot jobs report pushed a December Fed hike to roughly 78% odds, so Tuesday's CPI is the pivotal print.

- **AI selloff.** The Dow fell **1,833 points (3.44%) to 51,523** on Thursday Nov 5, a day after a **1,569-point** post-midterm rally; the Nasdaq entered correction (worst day since April 2025) and Nvidia closed down about **9%**. Drivers cited: chipmakers and data-center names reversing, concern that newly empowered Democrats are critical of AI data centers, and hedge funds locking in gains. ([Yahoo Finance, Nov 5](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-november-5-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-plunge-chip-stocks-melt-down-193726491.html); [ABC News, Nov 6](https://abcnews.com/Business/dow-plunges-1200-points-shedding-midterm-results-stock/story?id=137880888)) Outlets differ slightly on the S&P decline (4.3% vs 4.4%). I could not confirm Friday's closing levels.
- **Week in numbers.** The S&P 500 lost about **2.7%** and the Nasdaq about **3.4%** on the week, with the VIX near **25.6**. ([TipRanks, Nov 8](https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-week-that-was-the-week-ahead-macro-and-markets-november-8))
- **Jobs report.** October payrolls rose **165,000** against forecasts of roughly 59,000–70,000; unemployment fell to **4.1%** from 4.2%; average hourly earnings rose **0.4%**. Long-term unemployment is the soft spot: average duration hit **27.1 weeks**, the longest outside the pandemic in a decade. ([Morningstar, Nov 6](https://www.morningstar.com/economy/october-us-jobs-report-165000-rise-payrolls-beating-expectations); [CNBC, Nov 6](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/long-term-unemployment-october-jobs-report.html))

([Reuters via Investing.com, Nov 6](https://ca.investing.com/news/economy-news/fed-seen-sticking-to-december-rate-hike-after-jobs-data-4939832); [CNBC, Sep 16](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-september-2026.html)) One summary describes a hold at the October meeting with two dissents; I could not confirm that against a second source.
