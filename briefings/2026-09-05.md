# Morning Brief — Saturday, September 05, 2026

*The August jobs report blew past every forecast, and instead of celebrating, markets sold off — the print revived odds of a Fed **hike** rather than a cut at this month's meeting, the dominant thread running through today's national and markets news.*

## National

**Bottom line:** A blowout jobs report reshuffled the Fed outlook, Congress bought five months of breathing room on the shutdown clock, and the Supreme Court is weighing an emergency mail-ballot fight with direct midterm stakes.

- **August payrolls surge.** Nonfarm payrolls rose **162,000** in August, more than **triple** the roughly 53,000–55,000 consensus estimate and the strongest monthly gain since March, per Friday's Labor Department report. Unemployment held at **4.1%**, average hourly earnings rose 0.3% to $37.75 (+3.1% y/y), while information-sector jobs fell — plausibly reflecting AI-driven headcount cuts. ([CBS News, Sep 4](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/august-jobs-report-us-labor-market/))
- **Shutdown averted through Dec. 11.** Congress passed and President Trump signed a stopgap funding bill on Sept. 1 — the House vote was **370–48** — funding the government through **Dec. 11** and averting an Oct. 1 shutdown ahead of the midterms. The bill also blocks a contested OMB rule that would have put federal grant funding under political-appointee review. ([Bloomberg, Sep 1](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/congress-sends-funding-bill-to-avert-us-shutdown-to-trump))
- **DOJ asks Supreme Court to unblock mail-ballot rules.** The Justice Department filed an emergency application Thursday asking the Court to lift a Boston federal judge's order blocking new USPS rules requiring ballot ID/barcode matching against state voter lists. The case is unresolved as of the filing, and midterm mail ballots are already going out, putting the Court under pressure to rule fast. *Unconfirmed whether a ruling has landed since.* ([CNN, Sep 3](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/03/politics/trump-asks-supreme-court-to-ok-sweeping-changes-for-mail-ballots))
- **Manufacturing cools but keeps expanding.** The ISM Manufacturing PMI registered **54.6%** in August, down a point from July, marking an eighth straight month of expansion even as new orders and hiring growth both slowed and input-cost pressure (prices-paid index at 71.1) stayed elevated — a data point feeding directly into the Fed debate below. ([ISM/PR Newswire, Sep 2](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-54-6-august-2026-ism-manufacturing-pmi-report-302865127.html))
- **College football's Week 2 slate.** Ranked matchups run through the weekend: No. 7 Miami at Stanford and No. 14 USC vs. Fresno State played Friday night, with Saturday featuring No. 6 Indiana, No. 13 Alabama, and No. 23 Houston vs. Oregon State. ([ESPN schedule, Sep 5](https://www.espn.com/college-football/schedule/_/week/2/year/2026/seasontype/2))
