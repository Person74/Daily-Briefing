## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US-Iran war grinds toward its six-month mark with the Strait of Hormuz still under a US naval blockade and diplomacy stalled, while Gaza's ceasefire technically holds but is fraying under near-daily Israeli strikes, Ukraine sees no truce at all, and the US-Canada trade relationship has broken into an open tariff war.

- **Hormuz blockade persists, talks stalled.** The US-Israel war on Iran nears its six-month mark with roughly **50,000 US troops** maintaining a naval blockade that has redirected some 75 ships from Iranian ports; the White House said Thursday there are **no active negotiations with Tehran**, even as Iranian state media reported Iran and Oman had struck a deal for a temporary alternate shipping route through the strait — contingent on the US lifting its blockade, which Washington has not agreed to. Qatar's prime minister visited Tehran Aug 27 to try to restart dialogue, and Treasury Secretary Bessent is set to unveil what Trump calls the "toughest sanctions in history" on Iran. ([CNN, Aug 27](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/27/world/live-news/iran-war-trump))
- **Gaza ceasefire holding on paper, not in practice.** The formal ceasefire from October 2025 remains nominally in effect, but Israeli strikes killed at least three Palestinians on Aug 27 alone, and the US official overseeing Trump's ceasefire plan publicly criticized Israel's continued attacks, warning the alternative is "the next war." Cumulative Israeli strikes since the truce began have reportedly killed **more than 1,300 Palestinians**. *As of Aug 27; still no resolution on Phase 2 implementation.* ([NPR, Aug 27](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/27/g-s1-140400/israel-gaza-ceasefire)) ([Al Jazeera, Aug 27](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/27/three-killed-in-strikes-on-gaza-as-israel-renews-threats-over-kite-flying))
- **No Ukraine ceasefire; heavy strikes continue.** Russia hit Kyiv and at least nine Ukrainian cities overnight into Aug 27 with drones and missiles, damaging residential buildings and a school across four Kyiv districts and wounding people in Kryvyi Rih and Zaporizhzhia; US-mediated trilateral talks have been suspended since February and **no ceasefire is currently in place**. ([KSAT/AP, Aug 27](https://www.ksat.com/news/world/2026/08/27/russia-bombards-9-ukrainian-cities-overnight-calling-the-strikes-massive/))
- **US-Canada trade war escalates.** After trade talks collapsed, the US imposed **50% tariffs on roughly $20 billion** of Canadian goods on Aug 22; PM Mark Carney said Canada will retaliate "dollar for dollar" on US steel, dairy, appliances and electronics starting **Sept 8**, calling the US move a "miscalculation." ([Al Jazeera, Aug 22](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/22/us-imposes-50-tariffs-on-20bn-worth-of-canadian-goods-after-talks-fail)) ([NPR, Aug 22](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/22/nx-s1-5941584/us-canada-tariffs))
- **Red Sea attacks continue.** Houthi forces claimed an attack on the Saudi oil tanker *Amzan* off Yanbu on Aug 24 (Saudi operator Bahri confirmed the strike but said all crew were safe), the latest in a string of attacks including one that killed six people on a cargo ship on Aug 12 — the first Red Sea shipping fatalities in over a year. *Strait of Hormuz remains technically transitable but under heavy US military control, not a normal open lane.* ([Al Jazeera, Aug 24](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/24/yemens-houthis-report-attack-on-saudi-ship))
