## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US-Iran standoff over the Strait of Hormuz remains unresolved *as of early October*, and Houthi-Saudi fighting escalated this week; both keep oil near $100.

- **Iran-Hormuz impasse.** Trump publicly rejected Iran's seven-day plan to reopen the strait in late September; Iran then received a formal US answer via Qatari mediators, with reports that the sides agree on the elements but not the sequence. Iran's latest stated position is that it will **not reopen Hormuz until the US meets seven conditions** tied to a June memorandum. *Status as of early October; I found no reporting dated Oct. 7 on any breakthrough.* ([Al Jazeera, Sep 26](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/26/trump-rejects-irans-seven-day-roadmap-to-reopen-strait-of-hormuz); [HNGN, Sep 30](https://www.hngn.com/articles/273398/20260930/iran-gets-formal-us-answer-hormuz-plan-sequencing-splits-sides.htm); [Benzinga, Oct](https://www.benzinga.com/news/politics/26/10/62153579/iran-strait-of-hormuz-us-7-conditions-brent-oil))
