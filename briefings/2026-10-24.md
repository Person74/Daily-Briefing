## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US-Iran war is unresolved and Hormuz is still effectively shut; this is the main source of the oil shock.

- **Hormuz still disrupted.** On Oct 23 Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed two supertankers (over **4.5 million barrels** combined) hit naval mines on a southern route it calls unauthorized. **CENTCOM has repeatedly called such mine claims false**, and I found no independent confirmation of this specific incident. A live tracker described the strait as effectively closed, with 4 transits on Oct 18 versus about 85/day normally. ([gCaptain](https://gcaptain.com/iran-claims-more-tankers-hit-mines-in-hormuz-but-evidence-remains-elusive/); [anews, Oct 24](https://www.anews.com.tr/middle-east/2026/10/24/irans-revolutionary-guards-say-2-supertankers-hit-naval-mines-in-strait-of-hormuz)) *Tracker figures are third-party, as of Oct 18–24.*
- **Attacks and diplomacy.** Al Jazeera reported Oct 14 the worst spate of ship attacks since the war began in late February; the US-led maritime center rates the threat "severe." Iran's foreign minister says conditions for reopening are unchanged and its limited-ceasefire plan is still on the table, while Trump rejected a seven-day ceasefire plan. ([Al Jazeera, Oct 14](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/14/iran-steps-up-hormuz-surveillance-attacks-soar-oil-flows-crash-again)) *Diplomatic status as of mid-to-late October; I could not find a dated Oct 23 update on talks.*
- **Escalation rhetoric.** Netanyahu called Friday for the US and Israel to topple Iran's leadership, and the US maintains a naval blockade of Iranian oil exports. ([Fox News, Oct 23](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/us-iran-war-israel-netanyahu-oil-prices-10-23-26))

The White House said a Melania Trump-brokered ceasefire in Oleshky begins **Oct 29**. *Single-source search summaries; sanctions waiver details and date unconfirmed.* ([PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/trumps-decision-to-waive-russian-sanctions-outrages-congress-but-no-quick-counter-seen))
