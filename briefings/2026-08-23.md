# Morning Brief — Sunday, August 23, 2026

*Washington's pressure campaign hit two fronts at once this weekend: **50% tariffs on Canada** took effect after talks collapsed, and Treasury unveils what it calls the "toughest sanctions in history" on Iran tomorrow — and the resulting oil spike is already dragging cruise stocks, Royal Caribbean included, into a multi-day slide.*

## National

**Bottom line:** A US-Canada trade war went live overnight and a hawkish new Fed chair faces his first Jackson Hole test this week, with markets already unsettled.

- **US-Canada tariffs hit 50%.** Talks collapsed minutes before a Friday midnight deadline, triggering **50% US tariffs on $20–28 billion** of Canadian dairy, wine, cement, and other goods. PM Mark Carney called it "a miscalculation" and will retaliate "dollar for dollar" starting **Sept. 8** on US steel, dairy, and electronics; each side blames the other for the collapse. ([Washington Post, Aug 22](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/08/22/new-50-percent-tariffs-canada-go-into-effect-after-deal-with-us-falls-through/); [NPR, Aug 22](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/22/nx-s1-5941584/us-canada-tariffs))
- **Iran sanctions preview.** Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent holds a press conference **Monday, Aug 24** to detail sanctions he says will be **"the toughest in history,"** targeting Iran's remaining oil revenue and pressuring China to stop buying it. Iran's foreign ministry called the threat a path to "colonialism." (See Foreign Policy for the fuller Iran/Hormuz picture.) ([Reuters via Yahoo, Aug 21](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/us-treasury-secretary-bessent-hold-220931823.html))
- **Warsh's first Jackson Hole as chair.** Kevin Warsh, sworn in as Fed Chair in **May 2026** (Powell is out), has struck a hawkish tone and hinted at a possible **September hike** rather than a cut. He delivers his first Jackson Hole keynote **Friday, Aug 28** — a speech markets are watching closely since his Fed no longer telegraphs decisions the way Powell's did. ([Bloomberg, Aug 22](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-22/kevin-warsh-to-make-first-jackson-hole-speech-as-fed-chair))
- **Ravens WR Bateman arrested.** Baltimore's Rashod Bateman was arrested **June 3** on misdemeanor family-violence and property-damage charges after allegedly smashing a car windshield with his 3-month-old child inside; the arrest became public **Aug 21** and the NFL says the case is "under review." ([Baltimore Sun, Aug 21](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2026/08/21/ravens-rashod-bateman-arrest-charges-georgia/))

**Bottom line:** Iran's economic squeeze intensifies as Hormuz shipping collapses and gas prices climb, while Gaza's ceasefire stays nominal and Ukraine shows no sign of ending.

- **Hormuz Strait effectively closed.** A June truce briefly reopened safe passage, but Iran resumed attacks in July and the US reimposed its naval blockade. *As of Aug 22, unconfirmed since*: fewer than **10 commodity ships** transited the Strait on the most recently monitored day versus roughly **100** on a typical pre-war day. ([Hormuz Strait Monitor, Aug 22](https://hormuzstraitmonitor.com/crisis-timeline/))
- **Iran calls sanctions push a "declaration of war."** Ahead of Monday's Bessent announcement, Iran's Foreign Ministry framed the US sanctions campaign as extraterritorial overreach; a senior official close to the supreme leader said Tehran "will not submit." Trump separately floated bombing Iran "a little bit more." US gas prices are up nearly **$1/gallon year-over-year**. ([Al Jazeera, Aug 22](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/8/22/iran-war-live-trump-says-tehran-not-ready-to-make-right-deal-to-end-war); [CNBC, Aug 22](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/22/iran-criticizes-us-sanctions-extraterritorial-sovereignty.html))
- **Gaza ceasefire remains fragile.** Israeli strikes continued through the weekend, killing at least **2 Palestinians** in the last 48 hours. Kushner and Netanyahu agreed **Aug 17-18** that a US general should supervise Hamas's staged disarmament, but Netanyahu still won't fully withdraw IDF forces first. ([Al Jazeera, Aug 22](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/22/israeli-strikes-kill-two-and-injure-others-as-gaza-attacks-continue))
- **Ukraine: Russia offers only conditional openness.** Russian Deputy FM Sergey Ryabkov said Moscow is "ready to listen to any reasonable proposals" — but only ones matching Putin's terms on Donbas territory. **July was the war's deadliest month since May 2022** (376 Russian missiles launched vs. 288 in March). ([Al Jazeera, Aug 21](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/21/russia-ready-for-new-ideas-on-ukraine-war-that-match-putins-goals))
- **US-China tariff truce technically holds, quietly tightened.** USTR added a new **12.5% Section 301 tariff** on Chinese goods effective July 24, on top of the ~49% general rate from November's framework deal. The broader truce expires **Nov. 10** with no extension confirmed; *as of a May 20 statement, unconfirmed since*, Bessent said the administration was "not in a rush" to extend it. ([China Briefing tariff tracker](https://www.china-briefing.com/news/us-china-tariff-rates-2025/))
