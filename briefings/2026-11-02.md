# Morning Brief — Monday, November 2, 2026

*Election Day is tomorrow: the midterms lead because they set the policy and market backdrop for the rest of the year. Data caveat: many news sites were unreachable from this run, so several sections are thin and flagged as such.*

## National

**Bottom line:** Democrats enter the Nov. 3 midterms ahead in national polling, with the House leaning their way and the Senate closer.

- **Generic ballot.** RealClearPolling's average has Democrats up about **8 points** (51.5% to 43.1%), and an NPR/PBS/Marist poll shows a **10-point** edge among registered voters ([Forbes, Oct 29](https://www.forbes.com/sites/saradorn/2026/10/29/latest-polls-in-2026-midterms-democrats-lead-in-texas-maine-michigan-kansas-new-hampshire/)). Pollsters have understated Republicans in recent cycles, so the margin is not a forecast.
- **House map.** Cook Political Report rates **220 seats** as favoring Democrats, **17** toss-ups and 30 more competitive seats leaning GOP ([NPR, Oct 29](https://www.npr.org/2026/10/29/nx-s1-6020921/2026-house-map-democrats-flip-republicans-tossup-triage)).
- **Senate races.** Polls show the Senate tight. YouGov has Nebraska tied **49%-49%** (Ricketts vs. independent Osborn) and Kansas at **50%-49%** for the Democrat; Ohio polls show Brown ahead of Husted by 2 to 6 points ([Forbes, Oct 29](https://www.forbes.com/sites/saradorn/2026/10/29/latest-polls-in-2026-midterms-democrats-lead-in-texas-maine-michigan-kansas-new-hampshire/)).
- **Early vote.** More than **43 million** Americans had voted early as of the latest tally ([Al Jazeera, Nov 1](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/1/us-midterms-2-days-to-go-what-polls-say-as-trump-el-sayed-hit-the-trail)).
