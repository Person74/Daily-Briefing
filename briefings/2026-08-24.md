## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** Washington opens an "economic D-Day" sanctions campaign against Iran today, Russia's war on Ukraine grinds on with no ceasefire, and a US-Canada trade war erupted over the weekend after talks collapsed Friday — three live fronts with direct lines into oil prices and equity markets.

- **Iran faces "Economic D-Day":** Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent holds a 2 p.m. news conference today to unveil what officials call the toughest sanctions in history against Iran — targeting secondary sanctions on nations, banks and shipping channels still tied to Iranian oil, layered on the existing Strait of Hormuz naval posture. Iran's response has been defiant: a senior security official warned "not a single drop of oil will be exported" from the Persian Gulf if the pressure continues, and Tehran has threatened to detain or seize ships violating transit rules. The escalation comes as the US-Iran war, now in its sixth month, has missed its 60-day ceasefire window. *As of last week (unconfirmed since), the US Navy had been gaining ground patrolling Hormuz and the UAE had suspended all trade and financial transactions with Iran, though oil flows remain well below pre-war levels with neither side in full control.* ([CNN, Aug 23](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/23/world/live-news/iran-war-trump); [CNBC, Aug 24](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/24/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-bessent-economic-sanctions-.html); [US News, Aug 23](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-08-23/us-vows-economic-d-day-as-iran-threatens-to-halt-all-oil-exports))
- **US-Canada trade war erupts:** Talks collapsed late Friday, hours after Trump had said a deal was "pretty much" struck, and the US responded by imposing **50% tariffs** on an estimated **$20–28 billion** of Canadian goods — wine, hockey sticks, cement, dairy, electronics and building materials — stacking atop existing steel, lumber and auto tariffs. PM **Mark Carney** called the last-minute US demands "unacceptable," suspended negotiations, and says Canada will retaliate dollar-for-dollar starting **September 8**. Separately, the administration added new Section 232 tariffs on polysilicon and drones and Section 201 tariffs on quartz surfaces this past week, broadening the active US tariff regime well beyond this one dispute. ([Al Jazeera, Aug 22](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/22/us-imposes-50-tariffs-on-20bn-worth-of-canadian-goods-after-talks-fail); [CNBC, Aug 21](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/21/us-canada-fail-to-reach-a-tariff-deal-deepen-trade-war.html); [Axios, Aug 22](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/22/us-canada-tariffs-trade-trump-carney))

Separate reporting finds little progress on Phase 2 of Trump's 20-point plan (Hamas disarmament) in the past three months, with the humanitarian situation still dire. ([Middle East Monitor, Aug 23](https://www.middleeastmonitor.com/20260823-2-palestinians-including-child-killed-in-israeli-attacks-on-gaza-despite-ceasefire/))
