Rain chances run **70%** through this afternoon (mainly before 4 p.m.) and **60%** overnight, with mostly sunny skies between storm activity; wind stays light out of the east at 6-9 mph. Nothing severe expected. ([NWS Miami](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?zoneid=FLZ074))
- **Tropics: quiet.** No named storms are active in the Atlantic as of the National Hurricane Center's outlook issued 2 a.m. EDT today. Accumulated cyclone energy is running at levels typical of early-to-mid July — the slowest start since at least 1966 — with only five named storms all season and none reaching hurricane strength. One disorganized low in the central subtropical Atlantic is being watched (near-0% formation chance in 48 hours, ~20% in 7 days) but poses no threat to Florida. ([NHC Graphical Tropical Weather Outlook](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/gtwo.php); [Weather.com, Sep 10](https://weather.com/))
