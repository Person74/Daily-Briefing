# Morning Brief — Saturday, August 29, 2026

*Fed Chair Kevin Warsh used his first Jackson Hole address to explicitly wave off a rate cut just as July inflation came in hot and the Strait of Hormuz sat nearly shut to commercial traffic — three threads that converge on the same place: higher-for-longer rates and firmer oil, both of which flow straight into cruise-line fuel costs and market risk appetite heading into next week.*

## Markets

**Bottom line:** Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole speech met a stuck-at-3.7% inflation print Friday, pushing yields and the dollar higher and flipping the market's rate conversation from "when does the Fed cut" to "does it hike."

- **Warsh disappoints on rate cuts.** In his first Jackson Hole address as Fed Chair, Kevin Warsh said he's "impressed" by the economy's resilience but that underlying inflation trends "haven't meaningfully improved," and suggested rates may need to move *higher* in coming months rather than lower. He explicitly declined to signal a near-term cut, telling the room the Fed shouldn't be a crutch for traders' "next trade." (CNBC, Aug 28, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/28/kevin-warsh-jackson-hole-fed-inflation-rate-hike.html)
- **PCE stuck at 3.7%.** July headline PCE inflation held at **3.7%** year-over-year (vs. 3.6% consensus), core PCE flat at **3.3%** — the third straight month plateaued near those levels rather than cooling toward the Fed's 2% target. Personal income rose 0.4% ($115.1B); spending rose 0.2%, with services gains offsetting a pullback in goods. (CBS News, Aug 28, https://www.cbsnews.com/news/july-pce-inflation-index-federal-reserve/)
- **Stocks slip, yields climb.** The S&P 500 fell 0.25% to 7,711.76 and the Nasdaq dropped 0.52% Friday as investors digested Warsh's tone, though the S&P still closed the week higher. The 10-year Treasury yield rose to around **4.67%**, and the dollar index climbed to 99.47. CME FedWatch put the odds of a hold at the Sept. 16 FOMC meeting at 58.6% as of Aug 25, down sharply from 69.4% a week earlier — *rate-hike odds rising, not cut odds, as of Aug 25; unconfirmed since*. (Yahoo Finance/CNBC, Aug 28, https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-august-28-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-dip-fed-warsh-jackson-hole-speech-081514091.html)
- **Chip stocks volatile despite Nvidia beat.** Nvidia's Q2 print (Aug 26) beat on revenue ($96.22B) and EPS ($2.22), with Q3 guidance of $108B above Street estimates — but growth is decelerating to the mid-80% range year-over-year, and the broader semiconductor complex has swung hard this week on fears AI capex is peaking faster than expected. (Yahoo Finance, Aug 26, https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/nvidia-earnings-live-stock-moves-143102817.html)
- **Oil firms on Hormuz and Ukraine risk.** Brent crude traded near **$90.55/bbl** Friday morning, holding most of a recent rally as attention shifts from the Iran war toward Russia-Ukraine escalation — with the near-total closure of the Strait of Hormuz (see Foreign Policy) an underlying floor. (Fortune, Aug 28, https://fortune.com/article/price-of-oil-08-28-2026/)
- **On deck:** ISM Manufacturing PMI for August lands Tuesday, Sept. 1, and the August jobs report is due Friday, Sept. 4 — both tested against last month's shock 23,000 payroll drop and a Fed now leaning hawkish rather than dovish. (CNBC, Aug 7, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/07/jobs-report-july-2026.html)

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US-Israel war on Iran hit its six-month mark with no resolution, the Strait of Hormuz remains effectively closed to commercial shipping, and the Houthis have reopened a second front in the Red Sea — a geopolitical backdrop with direct implications for oil, insurance and shipping costs.

- **Iran war, six months in.** Trump launched joint US-Israeli combat operations against Iran on Feb. 28; six months later (Aug 28) the regime has survived, Trump's approval has slipped, and US munitions stocks are reportedly strained. The White House says it is *not* currently negotiating with Tehran, even as Iran says it's assembling conditions to reopen Hormuz. *Verified current as of Aug 28; whether talks resume is unconfirmed.* (Washington Post, Aug 28, https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/08/28/friday-marks-six-months-since-trump-launched-war-iran-now-he-stuck/)
- **Hormuz nearly shut.** Only about **3 ships** transited the Strait on Aug 23, versus a normal ~85 per day. Iran's IRGC says the blockade holds until the US "respects the terms" of the Islamabad MoU; a temporary shipping corridor floated with Oman remains unconfirmed. *Verified current as of Aug 28.* (Washington Times, Aug 26, https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/aug/26/irgc-strait-hormuz-closed-us-accepts-mou/)
- **Houthis escalate in the Red Sea.** A Houthi strike claimed against a Saudi-operated tanker off Yanbu on Aug 24 follows an Aug 11 attack on the cargo ship Tihamah that killed six crew — the first Red Sea shipping fatalities in over a year, reversing an earlier de-escalation. *Verified current as of Aug 24.* (Al Jazeera, Aug 24, https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/24/yemens-houthis-report-attack-on-saudi-ship)
- **Gaza ceasefire fraying.** Trump's Board of Peace envoy Nikolay Mladenov warned Aug 26-27 that ceasefire collapse would be a "point of no return." Gaza's Government Media Office counts **4,379** ceasefire violations by Israel and **1,288** Palestinians killed since the October 2025 truce. Netanyahu says Israeli forces stay put until Hamas fully disarms. *Nominally holding but fraying, as of Aug 27.* (NPR, Aug 27, https://www.npr.org/2026/08/27/g-s1-140400/israel-gaza-ceasefire)
- **US-China tariffs climbing ahead of Xi visit.** Washington is preparing a 7.5% "overcapacity" tariff on Chinese goods that would push total China tariffs to roughly **20%**, ahead of Xi Jinping's confirmed Sept. 24 White House visit — the first Chinese state visit to Washington in over a decade. *Tariff pending, not yet imposed, as of Aug 24.* (via US News/Bloomberg, Aug 24, https://www.usnews.com/news/top-news/articles/2026-08-24/us-eyes-china-overcapacity-tariffs-of-7-5-before-xi-trump-talks-bloomberg-news-reports)
