# Morning Brief — Monday, August 17, 2026

*The Strait of Hormuz has been effectively shut for nearly six months as the US-Iran war grinds on with no resolution — the single biggest wildcard for oil and shipping this morning — even as Wall Street opens the week near record highs on cooling inflation and a blowout Anthropic revenue report.*

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The Hormuz shutdown remains the dominant global risk with no end in sight, while Kushner presses both Hamas and Netanyahu on Gaza and a new 50% US tariff on Canada takes effect Wednesday.

- **Hormuz standoff drags on.** Nearly six months into the US-Iran war (which began Feb. 28), Tehran has kept the **Strait of Hormuz** effectively closed despite Trump's insistence it is "open and under US control"; traffic through the strait was just **17% of pre-conflict levels** over the past week, with only 151 ships transiting. A US carrier group is now heading to the region to replace the USS Lincoln. ([CNN, Aug 14](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/14/world/live-news/iran-war-trump)) ([Fortune, Aug 16](https://fortune.com/2026/08/16/iran-oil-exports-hormuz-us-naval-blockade-missiles-aircraft-carrier-lincoln/)) *as of Aug 16; fluid and worth checking daily.*
- **Kushner presses Hamas on disarmament.** Jared Kushner met Hamas chief **Khalil al-Hayya** in El Alamein, Egypt on Sunday — al-Hayya's first trip since taking over Hamas leadership in July — with Egyptian, Qatari and Turkish officials mediating. Kushner demanded "concrete, verifiable" disarmament steps; Hamas says it won't move until Netanyahu publicly commits to the Trump peace plan's terms. Kushner is due in Jerusalem today to meet Netanyahu, who rejected elements of the plan last week. ([Al Jazeera, Aug 16](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/kushner-meets-hamas-in-egypt-to-push-trumps-gaza-plan)) ([Axios, Aug 16](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/16/kushner-hamas-gaza-disarmament-egypt-israel))
- **Red Sea attacks resume with fatalities.** Houthi forces killed at least **six people** in a missile attack on the cargo ship Tihamah in the Bab al-Mandab Strait on Aug. 11 — the first shipping deaths there in this latest bout of fighting — and separately struck a Saudi oil refinery in Jazan and the port of Mokha, killing at least seven more. ([UPI, Aug 11](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/08/11/yemen-houthis-kill-6-in-attack/1181786477788/)) ([Fortune, Aug 11](https://fortune.com/2026/08/11/houthi-attacks-yemen-red-sea-iran-hormuz/))
- **New 50% US tariff on Canada takes effect Wednesday.** Trump's July 20 proclamations under Section 338 of the Tariff Act — the first-ever use of that authority — impose an additional **50% duty** on roughly **$20 billion** of Canadian dairy, alcohol and motor-vehicle imports, overriding USMCA preferences, effective 12:01 a.m. ET **Aug. 19** with no statutory expiration. ([White & Case, Aug](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/trump-administration-imposes-50-tariffs-certain-canadian-products-first-use-section))
- **Russia grinds forward, no ceasefire.** Russian forces advanced around Orekhov, Dobropolie and Kharkiv Oblast overnight; strikes killed **7** and wounded **51** across Ukraine, hitting a steel plant and targeting Kyiv, Kremenchuk and Kryvyi Rih. No active ceasefire is in place; talks stalled in June over Donbas territorial demands with no new session confirmed since. ([NPR, Aug 17](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/17/nx-s1-5930235/morning-news-brief))

## Markets
