# Morning Brief — Thursday, September 10, 2026

*U.S. and Iranian forces have been trading strikes near the Strait of Hormuz for over a week, sending Brent crude above $100 a barrel and pulling Wall Street lower for a third straight session — a combination that hits cruise-line fuel costs and portfolio positioning at once, and leads this morning's brief.*

## National

**Bottom line:** The U.S.-Iran war intensified this week with tanker strikes and a missile attack on a U.S. base in Jordan, oil topped $100 a barrel and pulled stocks lower for a third session, and the investigation into Sunday's fatal Miami cargo-plane crash is narrowing in on the final seconds before impact.

- **Iran war intensifies, oil spikes past $100.** U.S. forces destroyed five more Iranian oil tankers on Sept 8 (Kaviz, Charminar, Horizon 1, Riesco and Derya) — ten disabled or destroyed since Sept 1 — after Iran's IRGC twice targeted a U.S. warship. Iran retaliated with roughly **20 ballistic missiles** at Al Azraq air base in Jordan; Jordan says it intercepted 18, with no casualties. **Brent crude traded above $100/barrel** for the first time in about six weeks, and there's no active ceasefire — the prior U.S.-Iran memorandum of understanding lapsed in mid-August. ([Al Jazeera, Sep 9](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/9/us-destroys-five-iranian-tankers-iran-retaliates-with-attacks-on-jordan-base))

(See Markets, below.) ([TheStreet, Sep 9](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-sept-09-2026))
- **Miami plane-crash probe narrows.** Three days after an Amazon-contracted 21 Air Boeing 767 overran the runway at Miami International on Sept 6 and struck ground vehicles, killing five, officials on Sept 9 identified the victims; flight-data-recorder information indicates the crew set full engine power seconds before impact while attempting to abort an unsteady landing. NTSB says it's too early to rule mechanical failure, weather or crew performance in or out. ([AP/Anchorage Daily News, Sep 9](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2026/09/09/cargo-jet-crew-may-have-tried-to-abort-unsteady-landing-before-miami-crash-that-killed-5-flight-data-indicates/); [CNN, Sep 6](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/06/us/boeing-miami-plane-overruns-runway))
- **U.S.-Canada trade war escalates.** Canada's retaliatory tariffs — **15%, 25% and 50%** levies on roughly **$27.6 billion** of U.S. goods including steel, dairy, appliances and electronics — took effect Sept 8 in response to the U.S.'s 50% tariff on Canadian goods imposed Aug 22. Washington has moved to ban imports of Canadian dairy, motorcycles and alcohol starting **Sept 29** as the standoff deepens. ([Al Jazeera, Sep 8](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/8/canadas-retaliatory-tariffs-on-20bn-of-us-goods-take-effect))
