# Morning Brief — Thursday, November 5, 2026

*Democrats swept both chambers of Congress in Tuesday's midterms, and Wall Street rallied hard on it; that result frames the national, markets, Florida and foreign-policy stories below.*

## National

**Bottom line:** Democrats won the House and the Senate, ending unified Republican control in Washington; some races are still uncalled and Senate totals differ by source.

- **Democratic sweep.** The AP called the **House for Democrats** early Wednesday, completing a sweep of both chambers ([Variety, Nov 4](https://variety.com/2026/politics/news/midterm-election-results-democrats-win-house-senate-1236938473/); [Al Jazeera, Nov 4](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/democrats-win-control-of-house-and-senate-key-us-midterms-takeaways)). Democrats needed **51 Senate seats**; one tally has them at 54 to 44, but Alaska and Mississippi were unresolved and counts vary, so treat the margin as provisional ([Al Jazeera, Nov 4](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/democrats-win-control-of-house-and-senate-key-us-midterms-takeaways); [Yahoo News live, Nov 4](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/live/midterms-2026-live-updates-election-day-voters-head-to-the-polls-as-democrats-look-to-flip-gop-control-of-the-house-and-senate-110000715.html)).
- **Why voters swung.** Coverage points to high prices, the Iran war and corruption concerns; Democrats flipped Senate seats in **Texas, South Carolina, Iowa, Michigan and Kansas**, among others ([Al Jazeera, Nov 4](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/democrats-win-control-of-house-and-senate-key-us-midterms-takeaways)).
- **Trump response.** Trump reportedly said he would accept the results ([Al Jazeera, Nov 4](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/4/democrats-win-control-of-house-and-senate-key-us-midterms-takeaways)). Divided government makes major tax and spending legislation harder, which markets read as a positive (see Markets).
- **Economic backdrop.** Inflation remains the political and policy problem: PCE rose **3.6% year over year** ([CNBC, Oct 30](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/10/30/us-treasury-yields-fed-interest-rates.html)). Details in Markets.

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US-Iran war is in a tense negotiate-or-escalate window; talks are indirect and *as of Nov 2–3 unresolved*, with Hormuz still the central issue.

- **Iran talks.** Iran's foreign minister described "intensive" negotiations and said Tehran could reopen the **Strait of Hormuz within two days** if its demands are met; Washington had not accepted the conditions ([ABC Australia, Nov 3](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-03/iran-negotiations-us-intensive-war/107339208)). Iran called the latest US reply "unconvincing" and wants sanctions relief and an end to the **US naval blockade** ([Yahoo News, Nov](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/iran-says-us-reply-hormuz-043931688.html)).
- **Escalation risk.** US strikes occurred on the eve of the midterms, and Trump was reported to be leaning toward a larger attack after the vote but undecided ([CBS News, Nov 3](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-negotiations-midterms/)). *I could not confirm any development after Nov 3; the status may have changed overnight.*
- **Shipping.** Iran says **four oil tankers caught fire** after hitting mines near Hormuz ([Gulf News, Nov](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/middle-east-conflict-trump-warns-of-heavy-hit-as-iran-says-americans-voted-no-to-war-1.500735100)). Oil dropped 6–8% on Nov 2 on deal hopes; reports differ on size ([ABC Australia, Nov 3](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026/11/03/iran-negotiations-us-intensive-war/107339208)). Hormuz should be treated as *restricted, not reopened*.
- **US implications.** Vice President Vance said a deal needs a "verifiable commitment" that Iran will not rebuild its nuclear program ([CBS News, Nov](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-diplomacy-threat-resume-attacks/)). ## Florida and Miami

**Bottom line:** Voters passed Amendment 3's big homestead tax cut, and DeSantis will call a special session before leaving office to implement it; local governments face revenue pressure.

- **Amendment 3 passes.** It won roughly **62%** (needs 60%), lifting the homestead exemption from $50,000 to **$150,000 in 2027** and **$250,000 in 2028**; it applies to every taxing authority except school districts ([WUWF, Nov 4](https://www.wuwf.org/florida-news/2026-11-04/after-property-tax-cut-victory-desantis-eyes-special-session); [CBS Miami, Nov 4](https://www.cbsnews.com/miami/news/desantis-special-session-amendment-3-property-tax-implement-before-january/)).
- **Special session.** DeSantis said he will call it **before his term ends in early January**; no date set. He wants limits on local governments replacing lost revenue with fees and plans aid for affected localities ([MySuncoast, Nov 4](https://www.mysuncoast.com/2026/11/04/desantis-announces-news-conference-florida-capitol/)). *Miami-Dade-specific budget impact not found.*
- **Federal races.** Sen. **Ashley Moody** beat Angie Nixon; Democrat **Jared Moskowitz** won FL-25; **Carlos Gimenez** won FL-28; FL-27 (**Diaz-Balart**) was close at ~0.77% with 95%+ counted ([CBS Miami, Nov 4](https://www.cbsnews.com/miami/live-updates/florida-midterm-elections-2026/); [Decision Desk HQ, Nov 4](https://votes.decisiondeskhq.com/elections/2026-11-03/florida/miami-dade)). Results unofficial.
- **Local.** Democrats flipped state House District 106 (Lucia Báez-Geller over Fabian Basabe) and won open HD 113; **Vicki Lopez** won County Commission District 5 with ~60.6% ([NBC Miami, Nov 4](https://www.nbcmiami.com/elections/miami-dade-election-results-2026-midterms/3880240/)).

## Massachusetts

**Bottom line:** Healey won a second term decisively, Markey was re-elected, and voters kept legal cannabis and backed the 2024 gun law, with two ballot questions still close.

- **Governor.** **Maura Healey** beat Republican Mike Minogue with about **62%** at 95% reporting; Minogue self-funded **$19.5 million**, a statewide record ([NBC Boston, Nov 4](https://www.nbcboston.com/news/local/massachusetts-governor-race-healey-vs-minogue-ma-election-results/4043417/); [WBUR, Nov 3](https://www.wbur.org/news/2026/11/03/massachusetts-healey-governor-race-election-result)).
- **Senate.** **Ed Markey** won re-election over John Deaton per AP projections ([Dorchester Post, Nov 4](https://dorchesterpost.com/2026/11/04/massachusetts-2026-election-results-key-races-and-confirmed-winners/)).
- **Ballot questions.** Projected to pass: public-records expansion (Q1), public defender bargaining (Q2), Election Day registration (Q4), conservation fund (Q6) and the **2024 gun law (Q9)**. Projected to fail: party primaries (Q3) and the **cannabis rollback (Q8)**, so legalization stays. **Q5 (tax-revenue limit) and Q7 (single-family lot sizes) were too close to call** in the latest reports ([NBC Boston, Nov 4](https://www.nbcboston.com/news/local/massachusetts-election-results-ballot-questions-2026/4043419/)). Vote shares differ slightly between outlets.
