## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** Seven months into the Iran war, the fight has spread to global shipping — Houthi forces just seized a Red Sea island and port, and Saudi Arabia shut down a major oil pipeline in response.

- **Houthis seize Red Sea island.** Yemen's Iran-backed Houthi movement captured **Mayun (Perim) island** inside the Bab el-Mandeb Strait — the chokepoint carrying roughly **12% of world shipping** — along with the port of Mokha, opening a new front in the conflict. A Houthi spokesman said navigation remains "safe for all companies except for **Saudi vessels**." ([NPR, Sep 12](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/12/g-s1-143044/yemens-houthis-capture-red-sea-island))
- **Saudi Arabia shuts oil pipeline.** Riyadh shut down a major cross-country oil pipeline Friday as a "precautionary measure" following attacks the day before. *As of Sep 11-12; unconfirmed since.* ([NPR, Sep 11](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/11/g-s1-142822/houthis-red-sea-port))
- **Iran war grinds toward day 200.** As of Sept. 11 (Day 196 of the war that began when the **U.S. and Israel** struck Iran and killed Supreme Leader **Ali Khamenei** on Feb. 28), Iran's IRGC said it struck an unmanned vessel near the Strait of Hormuz, and a U.S. official said an earlier Iranian salvo damaged an A-10 and roughly eight F-15s at a Jordanian air base. ([GlobalSecurity.org, Sep 11](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/ops/iran-war-oprep.htm))
- **Ukraine drone war intensifies.** Russia launched **162 drones** at Ukraine overnight Sept. 10-11 while Ukrainian forces struck Russian fuel and logistics sites; neither side advanced Sept. 11. Zelensky and Canadian PM Mark Carney signed a **100-year partnership** including a joint drone-production deal Sept. 10. ([Kyiv Post/ISW, Sep 11](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/84312))
