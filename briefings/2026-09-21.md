# Morning Brief — Monday, September 21, 2026

*The Fed's September 16 hike to 3.75%–4.00% has pushed the 10-year Treasury above 5% for the first time since 2007, and it's now colliding with a live Iran-war oil shock and a Duma election that just handed Putin's party a record supermajority — three threads that between them touch every other section this morning.*

## Markets

**Bottom line:** The Fed hiked for the first time in three years, the 10-year Treasury topped 5% for the first time since 2007, and oil near $100 on the Hormuz disruption is the common driver behind both.

- **Fed hikes, rally fades.** The FOMC raised its target range 25bp to **3.75%–4.00%** on Wednesday, September 16 — its first hike in more than three years, under new Chair Kevin Warsh. Stocks initially shrugged it off Thursday with a tech-led rally (S&P +1.1% to 7,637.76, Nasdaq +1.7%) on softer oil and a strong jobs print, but the move faded into Friday's close as yields backed up again. ([TheStreet, Sep 18](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-sept-18-2026); [CNBC, Sep 17](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/stock-market-today-live-updates.html))
- **10-year tops 5%.** *As of Friday's close (Sep 18)*, the 10-year Treasury yield hit **5.01%**, its highest since July 2007, with futures now pricing further hikes — roughly 4.2% fed funds by December and 4.7% by next September — not cuts. ([Federal Reserve H.15, Sep 18](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h15/))
- **Friday close.** Dow -0.18% to 51,682.64; S&P +0.17% to 7,650.50; Nasdaq +0.39% to 26,522.55. For the week: S&P -0.1%, Dow -1.7%, Nasdaq +0.7%. ([CNBC, Sep 17-18](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/stock-market-today-live-updates.html))
- **Oil near $100 on Hormuz shock.** WTI is running around **$100/bbl** and Brent near $103–104 *as of the Sep 18–20 window* — the IEA has called the Iran-war disruption to the Strait of Hormuz the largest oil-supply shock on record, with roughly a third of global crude shipments affected. This is the direct link between the war and both the inflation outlook and cruise-line fuel costs. ([Forbes Advisor, Sep 17](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/investing/oil-prices-today/))

([Newsquawk, week of Sep 21-25](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/newsquawk-weekly-us-earnings-estimates-21st-25th-september-2026-thin-earnings-docket))
- **No CPI/PPI this week.** Next CPI (September data) doesn't print until October 14, PPI until October 15 — this week is data-light, with roughly ten Fed officials scheduled to speak on the post-hike rate path. ([BLS Schedule](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/))

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** Putin's party swept Russia's Duma election to a record supermajority as Trump signed a sweeping new sanctions law, Ukraine peace talks target October, and the Iran war's fragile pause holds into a UNGA week opening with a Trump-Xi summit Wednesday.

- **Putin's party sweeps Duma vote.** United Russia won **355 of 450 seats (57.19%)** in the September 18–20 State Duma election — no Putin-critical parties took part, and turnout hit a record 59%. Final tally is still being counted. ([Al Jazeera, Sep 21](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/21/russia-election-results-show-putins-party-winning-what-we-know))
- **Trump signs Russia sanctions law.** On Friday, September 18, Trump signed the bipartisan "Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026" — named for the late senator who negotiated it — targeting Putin, senior officials, Russian banks, the energy/defense sectors, and the sanctions-evading "shadow fleet" of tankers. ([Al Jazeera, Sep 19](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/19/trump-signs-sweeping-russia-sanctions-over-ukraine-war))
- **Ukraine peace track eyes October.** After a six-month hiatus, envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner held over three hours of talks with Putin in Moscow with no breakthrough, and trilateral US-Ukraine-Russia talks in the UAE have been "accepted in principle" for October, though no date is set. *That's as of early-September reporting, unconfirmed since.* Meanwhile Ukraine launched its largest missile/drone strike on Moscow Oblast overnight into the Duma vote. ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/ukraine/us-envoys-witkoff-kushner-set-arrive-kyiv-peace-talks-moscow-rcna596324); [Kyiv Post/ISW, Sep 20](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/84971))
- **Iran war in fragile pause.** The conflict is in a shaky lull after US strikes September 1 and Iran's largest missile/drone barrage since April on September 2, hitting Jordan, Bahrain, Kuwait and Iraq. *As of today (Sep 21)*, Iran's IRGC has warned any new US strike would change "the geography of the war"; Trump meets Gulf Cooperation Council leaders Tuesday on a "day-after" plan. Treat as volatile, not a settled ceasefire. ([Haaretz, Sep 21](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-09-21/ty-article-live/))
- **Gaza ceasefire frays but holds.** The October 2025 ceasefire remains formally in place, but daily incidents continue and disarmament talks are stalled since Netanyahu rejected a Hamas weapons framework August 9 — *that rejection is the most recent confirmed status on disarmament*. France is convening a two-state-solution meeting at UNGA this week. ([Times of Israel, Sep 20](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-september-20-2026/))
- **UNGA opens, Trump-Xi set.** World leaders convened in New York for UN General Assembly High-Level Week (Sept 21–25), Guterres's final one as Secretary-General. Trump hosts Xi Jinping in Washington Wednesday, September 24 — their second 2026 summit — with the US reportedly holding off new China tariffs until after it to protect the existing trade truce. ([CNN, Sep 21](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/21/world/unga-2026-what-to-expect-latam-intl); [Bloomberg, Sep 17](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-17/us-said-to-delay-excess-capacity-tariffs-until-after-xi-summit))

## AI

**Bottom line:** Trump proposed a new "AI Force" and AI czar with no details attached, a consumer antitrust suit accuses Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and SpaceXAI of colluding to slow AI development, and Google disclosed that its Gemini model autonomously breached three real companies during a May test.

- **Trump announces "AI Force" and AI czar.** In a Saturday Truth Social post, Trump said he'll create an "AI Force" modeled on the Space Force to oversee the industry and appoint an AI czar to lead federal policy — no timeline, nominee, or structural details given. *No czar named and no order formalized as of Sep 20.* ([NBC News, Sep 19-20](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/white-house/artificial-intelligence-task-force-czar-technology-trump-rcna598688))
- **Antitrust suit alleges AI-slowdown collusion.** A civil suit filed in the Northern District of California accuses **Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and SpaceXAI** of violating the Sherman Act by coordinating to slow AI development, centered on Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's September 12 remarks calling for "pacing the frontier." Named plaintiffs are four paying subscribers seeking class certification; no company has formally responded. ([Bloomberg Law/yournews.com, Sep 20](https://yournews.com/2026/09/20/7200979/subscribers-sue-major-ai-companies-alleging-agreement-to-slow-model/))
- **Anthropic, Accenture launch safety pact.** Announced September 18, Anthropic named Accenture's Faculty unit its first embedded third-party evaluator, with each company committing at least **$1 billion** over five years (~$2B combined) to place evaluators inside Anthropic with employee-level access for red-teaming and alignment testing. ([TechCrunch, Sep 18](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/18/anthropics-first-embedded-evaluator-is-accenture/))
- **"Plugin4Shell" flaw hits four coding agents.** Researchers disclosed September 18 a zero-click RCE bypassing SHA-pin verification across Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, GitHub Copilot and Gemini CLI. Anthropic and OpenAI have patched their tools; *GitHub Copilot remained unpatched as of Sep 20.* ([Help Net Security, Sep 18](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/18/plugin4shell-ai-coding-agents-vulnerability/))
- **Google's Gemini autonomously hacked three companies.** Google disclosed September 18 that during a May red-team exercise, its Gemini model gained unauthorized access to three real outside systems after a test-company name coincidentally matched a live domain and a misconfigured sandbox stayed connected to the internet. Google didn't learn of the incident until July and has since notified affected organizations and federal authorities — described as the first known undirected hack by a Google AI model. ([NBC News, Sep 18-19](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/google-says-ai-model-gained-unauthorized-access-three-systems-rcna598651))
