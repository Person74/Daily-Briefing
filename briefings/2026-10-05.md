## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US–Iran war is unresolved: Trump rejected Tehran's seven-day Hormuz proposal, but both sides say talks continue, and the strait remains effectively closed. *Status as of Oct 4; unconfirmed since.*

- **Hormuz deal stalled.** Iran offered to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and restart nuclear talks within seven days if the US lifts its naval blockade, waives oil sanctions and observes a ceasefire including Lebanon. **Trump rejected it** ("not the deal that I want to make") but told Axios he expects talks to resume this week. ([CBS News, Sep 27](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-strait-of-hormuz-7-day-proposal/); [Axios, Sep 27](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/27/trump-iran-war-hormuz-blockade-negotiations))
- **Military posture.** Senior officials met at Camp David and the **USS Theodore Roosevelt** is heading to CENTCOM; Iran's foreign minister says Tehran is "fully prepared" for war to resume. Reports as of Oct 3–4 say Iran has not answered a US counterproposal. ([Fox News, Oct 4](https://foxnews.com/live-news/us-iran-war-hormuz-strait-peace-talks-donald-trump-october-4); [Iran War Live, Oct 3](https://iranwarlive.com/recaps/day-218)) Day counts and details vary by tracker; treat as indicative.
- **Ukraine talks.** The US proposed **trilateral technical talks by end of October**, possibly in the UAE; Zelensky says Kyiv is receptive and no date is set. Russia has vowed intensified strikes after a drone attack damaged Kyiv's Northern Bridge. ([France 24, Oct 4](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20261004-live-germany-s-merz-in-kyiv-for-talks-with-zelensky); [CNBC, Oct 5](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/05/ukraine-war-russia-kyiv-putin-zelenskyy-trump.html))
