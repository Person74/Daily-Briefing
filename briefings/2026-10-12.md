## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The Iran war is not winding down: Hormuz shipping is contested, diplomacy is stalled, and Washington says it will not resume strikes before the midterms.

- **Hormuz contested.** Tehran and Washington are both claiming control of the strait and running competing blockades. Kpler data put crude flows for the week ended Oct 7 roughly **40% below normal**, and CNN counted just **seven tankers** transiting on one recent day. The IRGC said a supertanker hit a mine on a route Tehran had not approved, and the U.S. military disabled a cargo vessel it said tried to run the blockade of Iran's ports. ([Al Jazeera, Oct 11](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/11/hormuz-attacks-intensify-as-efforts-to-end-war-on-iran-stall); [CNN, Oct 11](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/11/middleeast/iran-hormuz-oil-analysis-intl))

Treasury has sanctioned **17 more vessels** in Iran's shadow fleet. ([Al Jazeera, Oct 11](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/11/hormuz-attacks-intensify-as-efforts-to-end-war-on-iran-stall); [CBS News live updates, Oct 11](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-nuclear-donald-trump-vance-rubio-strait-of-hormuz/))

Separately, Houthi attacks are keeping a Saudi airport closed and Trump is reportedly weighing strikes in Yemen. ([CBS News live updates, Oct 11](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-nuclear-donald-trump-vance-rubio-strait-of-hormuz/)) *Pledge reported via live-updates page; check for any walk-back.*
- **EU–China talks.** Trade Commissioner Šefčovič and Commerce Minister Wang Wentao met in Beijing **Oct 8–9**, agreeing on possible tariff cuts on some goods under WTO rules, with more talks set for **January and March 2027**. ([Forbes, Oct 6](https://www.forbes.com/sites/marktemnycky/2026/10/06/eu-china-trade-feud-reaches-defense-sector-ahead-of-beijing-talks/); [EU Insider](https://www.euinsider.eu/news/eu-china-mid-october-deadline-tariffs-unavoidable-2026))

## Markets

**Bottom line:** Stocks sit near records despite a 5%+ 10-year yield and $100+ oil, because soft jobs data cooled Fed-hike bets; Wednesday's CPI is the next test.

- **Friday's close.** The S&P 500 rose about 0.6% to **7,811.54**, just shy of its record set three days earlier. The Dow gained about 0.8% to **51,654.95** and the Nasdaq about 0.6% to **27,366.17**. For the week the S&P was up about 1.2%, while the Russell 2000 fell about 0.9%. The 10-year Treasury yield rose to **5.24%**. ([AP via La Nación, Oct 9](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/usa/how-major-us-stock-indexes-fared-friday-1092026-nid09102026/))

- **Fed.** Under Chair Kevin Warsh the FOMC voted 12–0 on Sept 16 to hike 25 bp to **3.75%–4.00%**. Minutes released Oct 7 show officials see another hike coming but gave no timing. Market odds for an Oct 28 hike have fallen to roughly **17%** per one prediction-market tracker. ([CNBC, Oct 7](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/07/fed-officials-see-another-hike-coming-but-no-sign-as-to-when-minutes-show.html); [DeFiRate, Oct](https://defirate.com/prediction-markets/fed-decision-odds/)) *Odds are from a secondary source and move daily.*

([CNBC, Oct 1](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/01/stock-market-today-live-updates-.html); [Al Jazeera, Oct 11](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/11/hormuz-attacks-intensify-as-efforts-to-end-war-on-iran-stall))
