## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** Washington opened a new tariff front against 14 economies, and the Gulf energy war is spreading to Saudi infrastructure. Neither the Iran nor the Ukraine track showed a breakthrough in the latest available reporting.

- **New Section 301 tariffs.** On Nov 25, USTR announced "structural excess capacity" findings and **proposed new tariffs on 14 economies**:
  - China: up to 20%, or up to 40% combined if talks fall short.
  - Vietnam: up to 35%.
  - India: up to 25%.
  - EU, Japan, South Korea, Switzerland and Taiwan: up to 15%, consistent with existing deal caps.
  - Several Southeast Asian economies: about 18–20%.

  Yale Budget Lab estimates the average statutory rate would rise to **13.9%** if the proposal takes effect. The action relies on Section 301 because the Supreme Court struck down the earlier tariffs in February. Comments are due **Jan 5** and hearings begin **Jan 25**. USTR Greer said nothing would take effect for "a couple months." Outlets disagree on whether China's headline rate is 20% or 40%, so check the USTR notice for exact rates. ([Baker McKenzie, Nov 25](https://globalimportblog.bakermckenzie.com/2026/11/25/us-ustr-proposes-section-301-tariffs-on-14-economies-over-structural-excess-capacity/); [The Boston Report, Nov 25](https://thebostonreport.com/2026/11/25/ustr-section-301-tariffs-14-countries-china-20-percent))
- **Beijing and Brussels push back.** China's Commerce Ministry said today it "firmly opposes" the plan as a WTO violation and **reserves the right to respond**, but it announced no countermeasures. The EU rejected the overcapacity premise and said it expects Washington to honor the agreed **15% cap**. ([France 24, Nov 26](https://www.france24.com/en/americas/20261126-unacceptable-major-economies-push-back-against-new-us-tariffs))
- **Houthis hit Saudi energy.** On Nov 25, the Houthis claimed a drone attack on **Ras Tanura**, Saudi Arabia's largest refinery. The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted four drones there. A Reuters review of NASA imagery showed a large smoke plume, but Riyadh has not confirmed damage to the refinery. The group also claimed a strike on a Riyadh financial-district tower, where Saudi Civil Defence reported **11 wounded**. That was the second hit on the same tower in under a week, following a Nov 22 claim against the Rabigh refinery. ([US News/Reuters, Nov 25](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-11-25/yemens-houthis-say-they-attacked-saudi-oil-refinery-and-riyadh-financial-district); [Al-Monitor, Nov 25](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/yemens-houthis-claim-attack-saudi-arabias-largest-refinery))
- **Strait of Hormuz.** *As of Nov 25, the strait was rated "Restricted," not reopened.* UKMTO counted transits **about 65% below pre-war levels** in the week to Nov 18. It reported five vessels struck by projectiles on Nov 22 and 106 incidents since the war began Feb 28. Most traffic uses a US-facilitated southern corridor. The US blockade of Iranian ports, in place since Jul 14, remains in effect. *Status is from tracker and UKMTO data through Nov 25 and is unconfirmed since.* ([Iran International, Nov 21](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611215184); [Hormuz.now, Nov 25](https://hormuz.now/faq))
- **Iran diplomacy.** *As of Nov 21, there was no deal.* A Qatari delegation held two hours of talks in Tehran on Nov 20. Iran said these were **not yet formal negotiations** and repeated that its enriched uranium must stay in the country and the US blockade must end. Witkoff and Kushner met Qatari mediators in Berlin on Nov 19. *Unconfirmed since Nov 21.* ([Al Jazeera, Nov 21](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/iran-says-held-talks-with-qatari-mediators-urges-us-to-accept-conditions); [Axios, Nov 20](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/20/witkoff-kushner-meet-qatari-mediators-iran))
- **Ukraine.** The Nov 19 Coalition of the Willing summit in Berlin, held days after three-way Trump–Putin–Xi talks in China, reaffirmed about **$80B** in military aid. It also urged Russia to accept a **winter energy-strike truce**. Russia has not agreed to European participation in the next trilateral round, and no date has been set. *As of Nov 20; unconfirmed since.* ([The Local, Nov 19](https://www.thelocal.de/20261119/ukraine-allies-meet-in-berlin-following-trump-putin-xi-talks); [US News, Nov 20](https://www.usnews.com/news/national-news/articles/2026-11-20/european-leaders-reaffirm-support-for-ukraine))
