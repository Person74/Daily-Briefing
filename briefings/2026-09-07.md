## National

**Bottom line:** Record Labor Day gas and diesel prices tied to the widening US-Iran conflict, a pending Supreme Court fight over mail-ballot rules, and a hotter-than-expected jobs report reshaping Fed odds dominate a midterm-heavy week.

Iran's parliament speaker warned Sunday that the "era of proportionate responses is over," while Trump called the conflict "small potatoes" and claimed the US has "essentially" taken over Iran. ([Washington Post, Sep 7](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/09/07/labor-day-record-high-gas-prices/8ed13114-aa71-11f1-b498-8697f35a6743_story.html); [ABC News, Sep 7](https://abcnews.com/International/live-updates/iran-live-updates-centcom-targeted-iranian-forces-posed/?id=136080582))
- **Supreme Court weighs mail-ballot rule.** The Trump administration filed a third emergency appeal Sunday seeking to enforce USPS rules requiring states to submit voter-citizenship data before mailing ballots, after a Massachusetts federal judge blocked the policy Friday as unconstitutional. The Court ordered responses by **Wednesday**, with a ruling expected that afternoon as states are already mailing midterm ballots. ([CNN, Sep 6](https://us.cnn.com/2026/09/06/politics/trump-administration-again-asks-supreme-court-to-allow-mail-ballot-rule-to-take-effect))
- **Jobs report flips Fed odds.** August payrolls rose **162,000** versus **53,000** expected, unemployment held at **4.1%**, and June-July payrolls were revised up a combined 55,000 — pushing odds of a September Fed rate *hike*, not cut, to roughly 50-58% in futures markets. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq slipped Friday on the news, with August CPI due Sept. 11 ahead of the Sept. 15-16 FOMC meeting. ([CNBC, Sep 4](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/jobs-report-august-2026.html))

([NBC News, Sep 6](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/weekend-rundown-september-6-rcna596242))
- **Government funded through Dec. 11.** Trump signed a stopgap spending bill (H.R. 6500) Sept. 2 after it passed 370-48 in the House and 90-6 in the Senate, averting a pre-midterm shutdown and setting the next funding fight for December. ([NAGGL, Sep 2](https://www.naggl.org/president-signs-continuing-resolution-through-december-11/))

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US and Iran are exchanging strikes over the Strait of Hormuz for a second consecutive week, Russia's Donetsk offensive has stalled even as the drone war intensifies, and Germany's far-right AfD just scored a historic regional breakout.

- **Hormuz clashes escalate.** The two sides traded strikes over the weekend, with Iran's IRGC firing ballistic missiles before dawn Sept. 5 at a US carrier and destroyer (CENTCOM says both were "successfully evaded"); Iran also announced a new shipping route through the strait while Trump insisted the US controls the waterway. Iran struck at least **13 commercial vessels** in August, and the strait has seen shipping largely blocked since a June ceasefire framework collapsed in July. *As of Sep 7; strikes ongoing.* ([NPR, Sep 7](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/07/nx-s1-5959706/strikes-between-u-s-and-iran-continue-as-they-vie-for-control-of-strait-of-hormuz))

Overnight Sept. 5-6 Russia launched **108 drones** at Ukraine while Ukraine kept striking Russian oil infrastructure; Putin has reiterated he won't make "meaningful compromises" in talks. *As of Sep 6.* ([Kyiv Post/ISW, Sep 6](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/83903))
- **Panama Canal cuts transits again.** Citing a **44% drop** in watershed inflows, the ACP is capping daily transits at 34 vessels from Sept. 4, falling to 32 from Sept. 15, with max draft trimmed to 14.48m "until further notice" amid an expected prolonged El Niño. *As of Sep 2; restrictions take effect this week.* ([DTN, Sep 2](https://www.dtnpf.com/agriculture/web/ag/columns/cash-market-moves/article/2026/09/02/panama-canal-authority-adopts-new))
- **Germany's AfD breaks through.** Exit polls show the far-right Alternative for Germany more than doubled its vote share to take a commanding lead in Sunday's Saxony-Anhalt state election, though it remains unclear whether AfD can form Germany's first far-right state government since WWII. ([NPR, Sep 6](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/06/nx-s1-5955677/german-exit-polls-suggest-far-right-party-big-lead-in-regional-election))
- **New sanctions hit Cuba, Iran.** Washington sanctioned several Cuban state companies and Fidel Castro's grandson, while Treasury/OFAC designated nearly 60 entities tied to Iran's military procurement and petroleum revenue under "Operation Economic Outcast," with a wind-down deadline of Sept. 8 for related licenses. ([Al Jazeera, Sep 4](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/4/us-continues-to-squeeze-cuban-economy-with-new-round-of-sanctions))
