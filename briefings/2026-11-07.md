# Morning Brief — Saturday, November 7, 2026

*Wall Street ended a brutal week where the AI trade broke: Thursday's Nasdaq correction followed Democrats taking Congress, and a hot jobs report on Friday pushed December Fed-hike odds back above 70%. That leads because it drives rates, fuel costs and cruise-equity valuations all at once.*

## Markets

**Bottom line:** Stocks bounced Friday after Thursday's AI-led rout, but the S&P 500 and Nasdaq both posted their worst weeks since April 2025 and a strong jobs print kept a December rate hike more likely than not.

- **Thursday's rout.** On Nov 5 the **Nasdaq fell about 5.5% into correction**, the S&P 500 about 4.4% and the Dow roughly 1,830 points (3.4%), the worst Nasdaq day since April 2025. The stated driver was fear that a Democratic Congress, including wins in data-center-heavy states like Texas and Ohio (governorships), could slow or regulate AI infrastructure spending; semiconductors fell about 9% and the Magnificent 7 lost about **$1 trillion** in value. ([Axios, Nov 5](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/05/nasdaq-plummets-correction); [CNBC, Nov 5](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/05/stock-market-selloff-ai-fears.html)) Outlets differ slightly on exact percentages.
- **Friday's bounce.** The **S&P 500 rose 0.3% to 7,376.86**, the Nasdaq gained 1.1% to 24,579.42 and the Dow slipped about 0.5% to 51,285.73 (one outlet has the Dow at -0.4%). For the week the S&P is down about 2.7% and the Nasdaq about 3.4%. Chips led the rebound, with AMD up roughly 10.8% and Nvidia up 3.9% per one recap. ([MyMotherLode/AP-style table, Nov 6](https://mymotherlode.com/news/national/11287401/how-major-us-stock-indexes-fared-friday-11-6-2026.html); [Yahoo Finance, Nov 6](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-november-6-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-jobs-report-earnings-135953863.html))
- **Jobs report beat.** October payrolls rose **165,000 versus roughly 65,000 expected**, unemployment fell to **4.1%** from 4.2%, and average hourly earnings rose 0.4% m/m and 3.1% y/y. September was revised up to 60,000 from 29,000. Long-term unemployment duration is rising even as headline hiring is strong. ([Bloomberg, Nov 6](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/us-adds-165-000-jobs-above-all-forecasts-as-unemployment-falls); [CNBC, Nov 6](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/long-term-unemployment-october-jobs-report.html))
- **Fed hike odds.** The Fed held at **3.75%–4%** on Oct 28 on a 10-2 vote, with Cook and Hammack dissenting in favor of a hike. CME FedWatch showed about **72.8%** odds of a December hike on Nov 6 (down from about 80.7% on Nov 5; prediction markets were lower, near 66%). Treasury 10-year yield was about 4.86%. ([KuCoin/CME FedWatch, Nov 6](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/fed-s-december-rate-hike-probability-at-72-8-before-nonfarm-payrolls-release); [KuCoin, Nov 6](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/us-democrats-win-congress-fed-rate-hike-odds)) *Odds move daily and differ by source.*
- **Oil slump.** Brent slid about **15% on the week to roughly $99**, the biggest weekly drop since 2022, on hopes of a US-Iran deal. This is a tailwind for cruise fuel costs; see Foreign Policy for why it is fragile. ([Rigzone, Nov 6](https://www.rigzone.com/news/wire/oil_posts_biggest_weekly_drop_since_2022-06-nov-2026-185035-article/))
- **On deck.** **October CPI is due Tuesday, Nov 10 at 8:30am ET**; the next FOMC decision is **Dec 9**. ([KuCoin calendar summary, Nov 6](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/us-democrats-win-congress-fed-rate-hike-odds)) I did not confirm a consensus CPI figure.
