# Morning Brief — Wednesday, November 11, 2026

*A hotter-than-expected October CPI has pushed the market to fully price a **December Fed rate hike**, with a Fed governor floating a half-point move. That reprices borrowing costs for every leveraged balance sheet, cruise lines included, so it leads. The Iran standoff is the second story: Washington says the war is "effectively over," but the Strait of Hormuz is still not open.*

## Markets

**Bottom line:** Hot inflation sent the 2-year yield up the most in 19 months and stocks lower, and with the bond market shut for Veterans Day, Thursday is the next chance for rates to reprice.

- **Stocks fell on CPI.** The **S&P 500 fell 0.38% to 7,520.93**, the Dow lost 0.30% to 52,231.89, the Nasdaq dropped 0.74% to 25,065.88, and the Russell 2000 slid 0.68% to 2,821.38. The S&P is about 4.1% below its record ([Phemex, Nov 10](https://phemex.com/news/article/us-stocks-close-lower-as-all-three-major-indexes-fall-103094); [Eurasia Business News, Nov 11](https://eurasiabusinessnews.com/2026/11/11/stock-market-today-dow-jones-nasdaq-sp-500-fall-on-november-10-2026-oil-and-gold-retreat/)). Tuesday gave back part of Monday's rally, when the Dow rose 2.2% and the S&P gained 2.4% ([Yahoo Finance, Nov 10](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-news-nov-10-094800346.html)).
- **CPI beat consensus.** Headline October CPI rose **0.4% m/m against 0.3% expected**. Core rose **0.3% against 0.2%**, its third straight 0.3% month ([FXStreet, Nov 10](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/us-cpi-data-set-to-ease-in-october-as-markets-reassess-fed-rate-hike-expectations-202611100930); [Mitrade, Nov 11](https://www.mitrade.com/insights/more/inflation/core-cpi-third-straight-20261111J1108)). Consensus going in varied by poll: Dow Jones had 0.3%/0.2%, while FactSet had 0.2% for headline ([CNBC, Nov 9](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/october-cpi-report-2026-inflation.html)). BLS published **no year-over-year rate**, because October 2025 data was never collected during the shutdown.
- **Fed hawks got louder.** Governor Waller said he backs a December hike and that a **50 bp move** should be considered if core keeps running hot. Dallas Fed President Logan called for "at least two more" 25 bp hikes, and St. Louis Fed President Musalem said more firming is needed "soon." After Waller spoke, swaps **fully priced a December hike**, up from about 85% before ([CNBC, Nov 10](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/fed-governor-waller-sees-december-hike-likely.html); [Korea Herald, Nov 10](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10933368)). The FOMC meets **Dec 8–9**.
- **Rates spiked, oil eased.** The **2-year yield jumped 17 bp to 4.73%**, its biggest one-day rise since April 2025. The 10-year rose 10 bp to 4.98% and briefly traded above 5% ([Reuters via Investing.com, Nov 10](https://investing.com/news/economy-news/bonds-reel-as-hot-inflation-fuels-jumbo-fed-hike-fears-5017511)). Brent fell 1.17% to **$91.49** and WTI fell 0.93% to $86.04, both the lowest settlements since Aug 31, as US officials played down further Iran strikes. The dollar firmed ([Eurasia Business News, Nov 11](https://eurasiabusinessnews.com/2026/11/11/stock-market-today-dow-jones-nasdaq-sp-500-fall-on-november-10-2026-oil-and-gold-retreat/)).
- **Nvidia's governance probe.** Nvidia fell **about 5.5% to roughly $168.89** after its independent directors hired outside counsel to investigate reports that CEO Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. Microsoft passed it in market value ([Motley Fool, Nov 10](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/10/stock-market-today-nov-10-nvidia-stock-plunges-5-5-as-ceo-scandal-overshadows-its-upcoming-earnings-report/); [Yahoo Finance, Nov 10](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/microsoft-tops-nvidia-market-cap-193114512.html)). A report that $7.5B in chips reached Chinese firms through that relationship is *unverified; Reuters could not confirm it*. Nvidia reports earnings **Nov 17**.
- **On deck.** Corteva and CAE report after today's close. **Cisco and Applied Materials** report Thursday after the close ([Kiplinger, Nov 9–13 calendar](https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/stocks/17494/next-week-earnings-calendar-stocks); [MarketScreener, this week](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/weekly-earnings-agenda-softbank-coreweave-nebiusa-ai-looking-for-reassurance-ce785fd2d08ef422)). Stocks trade today, but **bond markets are closed** for Veterans Day ([Eurasia Business News, Nov 11](https://eurasiabusinessnews.com/2026/11/11/stock-market-today-dow-jones-nasdaq-sp-500-fall-on-november-10-2026-oil-and-gold-retreat/)).

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** No US-Iran deal has been announced, Hormuz is still effectively shut by Iran's account, and the first Russia-Ukraine talks in nine months wrapped up today without a reported breakthrough.

- **Iran: war "effectively over."** After meeting Qatar's prime minister on Monday, Trump said "Iran militarily is effectively over. We're just keeping it the status quo." He also said the **naval blockade stays in place**, and he gave no sign of a troop drawdown. US forces carried out limited strikes on Iranian air defenses at least twice last week ([AJC/AP, Nov 9](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/11/trump-after-meeting-with-qatari-pm-says-he-believes-war-with-iran-is-militarily-effectively-over/)).
- **Qatar's $8B interim plan.** Qatar brought an Iran-approved proposal to the White House. Iran would stop attacking Gulf shipping; in exchange, the US would lift the blockade and release **$8 billion** in Iranian assets held in Qatar. *Neither government has confirmed the terms*, and Rubio, Bessent, Lutnick and Hegseth reportedly oppose it ([Iran International, Nov 9](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611094583)). Rubio said the talks have made **no progress** and called Iran's demands "outrageous" ([The Tribune, Nov 9](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/usa-news/unacceptable-outrageous-ridiculous-demands-marco-rubio-says-no-breakthrough-in-us-iran-peace-talks)). Qatar reports "progress."
- **Hormuz status.** *As of Nov 11*, senior Iranian official Mohsen Rezaei says the Strait **will not reopen** until Tehran's seven conditions are met, and he called reports of ongoing talks "a pure lie" ([Newscord roundup, Nov 11](https://newscord.org/article/irans-mohsen-rezaei-rejects-us-talks-says-hormuz-stays-shut-until-seven-conditio--Story_20261111_IrandeniestalkswithU48818a5b)). Hegseth claims US control of the Strait. Ships are transiting at sharply reduced volumes, and *no independent count newer than Nov 7 was found*. US implication: oil has fallen on de-escalation hopes, but that rests on talks Iran publicly denies.
- **Russia-Ukraine in Abu Dhabi.** The two-day talks, mediated by Kushner and Witkoff, **ended today after a final session of less than two hours** ([Moscow Times, Nov 11](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/11/11/russia-and-ukraine-wrap-up-final-day-of-abu-dhabi-peace-talks-a94227)). Day one ran nearly four hours. Zelenskyy called it "constructive" and Kushner said he saw ways to "bridge" differences ([PBS NewsHour, Nov 10](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/russia-and-ukraine-hold-nearly-4-hours-of-u-s-mediated-talks-in-abu-dhabi)). **No agreement has been reported.** Ukraine's team was due to meet European officials afterward; the Kremlin said it would not.
- **Israel's new Knesset.** The **26th Knesset was sworn in**, and Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar party called off coalition talks with Likud after what it called a "divisive speech" by Netanyahu ([Times of Israel, Nov 10](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-november-10-2026/)). Government formation is unresolved.
- **Europe security notes.** Poland arrested two Ukrainian men suspected of spying for Russia, and the UK committed **£90 million** to Royal Navy anti-submarine technology ([Democracy Now, Nov 10](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/10/headlines)).

## Massachusetts

**Bottom line:** A federal jury convicted former Stoughton officer Matthew Farwell in the Sandra Birchmore killing, and Boston plans to loosen its affordable-housing rules.

- **Farwell convicted.** A federal jury in South Boston found **former Stoughton police officer Matthew Farwell guilty on both counts**: killing Sandra Birchmore and her unborn child. Jurors accepted the prosecution's argument that he staged her death to look like a suicide ([CBS Boston, Nov 10](https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/matthew-farwell-trial-jury-verdict-sandra-birchmore/); [NBC Boston, Nov 10](https://www.nbcboston.com/news/local/court-updates-sandra-birchmore-matthew-farwell/4045630/)). Prosecutors did not seek the death penalty, and *no sentencing date has been reported*.
- **Boston eases housing rules.** The Wu administration proposes raising the inclusionary-zoning threshold from **7 to 10 units** and giving developers more time to pay into the city's affordable-housing fund ([Boston Globe, Nov 9](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/11/09/business/boston-affordable-housing/)). The BPDA board is scheduled to take it up **this week**, followed by the Zoning Commission and the City Council. The goal is to restart stalled residential construction.
