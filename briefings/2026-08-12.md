Strait transits have "effectively collapsed" since fighting broke out in February — just 8-15 vessel transits Aug 4-6 versus ~130 before the conflict — and the EIA doesn't expect Middle East output to normalize until early 2027. AAA's national gas average hit **$4.10/gallon** on Aug 3, up 30% year-over-year. ([CNBC, Aug 10](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-hormuz-trump-iran.html)) ([Al Jazeera, Aug 12](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/12/oil-prices-rise-as-attacks-dent-hopes-for-strait-of-hormuz-reopening))

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** Four active fronts — Iran, Ukraine, Gaza, and the Red Sea — remain unresolved as of this week, with the Hormuz standoff now the one most likely to move markets in the next few days.

- **Strait of Hormuz still closed.** Commercial shipping through Hormuz has been effectively shut since the conflict escalated in late February. *As of Aug 12*, Iran, Oman, and the US are described as close to an interim deal — separate shipping lanes, demining, a 60-day ceasefire clock — but nothing is signed; Iran wants a 5-7% cargo fee versus Oman's 3% offer, and Iran's IRGC says the Strait reopens only on its terms. ([CNN, Aug 10](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/10/world/live-news/iran-war-trump))
- **Ukraine: no ceasefire, fresh escalation.** Russia struck Zaporizhzhia with **North Korean ballistic missiles** overnight Aug 10-11, killing at least 6 and wounding 19+; a North Korean missile unit reportedly deployed to western Russia with roughly 120 ballistic missiles. Zelensky said Putin "is preparing for escalation, not peace" and is pushing for more sanctions; a bipartisan US House bill targeting Russian energy and banks was introduced this week. ([CNBC, Aug 11](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/ukraine-war-zelenskyy-russia-putin-north-korea.html))
- **Gaza ceasefire fragile.** The formal ceasefire dating to October 2025 technically holds, but Israel intensified operations in early August around a disputed "phase two" disarmament plan; Hamas has agreed to phased disarmament, but Israel's government cites "serious security concerns." *As of Aug 10-11*, post-ceasefire Israeli strikes are reported to have killed over 1,259 Palestinians per Gaza officials. ([Al Jazeera, Aug 10](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/8/10/iran-war-live-us-semi-negotiating-with-tehran-israel-rejects-gaza-plan))
- **Red Sea blockade of Saudi Arabia continues.** Houthi forces declared a maritime blockade of Saudi Arabia on July 20 and have since struck eight Saudi-linked vessels; on Aug 5 they threatened tankers in the northern Red Sea as a new escalation zone. Weekly transits fell from 354 to roughly 266 following the blockade announcement. ([Bloomberg, Aug 5](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/yemen-s-houthis-say-they-ll-target-tankers-in-northern-red-sea))
- **US-China trade truce holds, loosely.** *Reported Aug 11 in secondary coverage (not independently confirmed against a primary wire release)*, Trump extended by 90 days a pause on raising the reciprocal tariff to 125%; Section 301 tariffs remain on roughly $300B of Chinese goods, with a new 12.5% forced-labor tariff added July 24. Treat the truce as continuing but unconfirmed in its exact terms. ([Tax Foundation tracker, accessed Aug 11](https://taxfoundation.org/research/all/federal/trump-tariffs-trade-war/))
- **Panama Canal operational, tighter drafts ahead.** The Canal is running normally at full draft with about 36 transits a day, but the authority has announced maximum draft at the Neopanamax locks will drop to 48 feet on Aug 26 and 47.5 feet on Sep 3 as a developing El Niño lowers Gatun Lake levels. ([Panama Canal Authority, 2026](https://pancanal.com/en/panama-canal-reports-higher-transits-and-tonnage-while-preparing-to-meet-demand-amid-expected-el-nino-conditions/))
