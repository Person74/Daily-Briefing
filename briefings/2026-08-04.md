## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** Trump pulled back from a threatened strike on Iran in favor of talks over the Strait of Hormuz, while Gaza's ceasefire remains fragile, Russia-Ukraine grinds on, and Houthi attacks keep Red Sea shipping disrupted.

([globalsecurity.org, Aug 3](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/world/war/russo-ukraine-2026-08-01.htm))
