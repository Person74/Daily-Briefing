## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** No breakthroughs anywhere. The Trump–Xi–Putin summit produced a declaration and nothing binding, the US–Iran talks are stuck while Iran prepares to test the US oil blockade, and the Ukraine diplomacy is still about security guarantees rather than a ceasefire.

- **Iran: blockade test looming.** *As of Nov 20–21:* satellite and tracking data show **14–20 tankers** gathered at Kharg Island, most with transponders off. FM Araghchi said Friday that Iran would break the blockade. CENTCOM has not commented, and a Kpler analyst says it is "too early" to call this a serious breakout attempt. The US reimposed the blockade on Jul 14 ([Yahoo/Reuters, Nov 20](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/iran-readies-tanker-convoy-kharg-100649908.html); [Iran International, Nov 20](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611204994)). - **Hormuz still dangerous.** UKMTO counted **31 attacks in October** and about 19 so far in November, the most since the war began in late February ([ABC News, Nov 21](https://abcnews.com/International/strait-hormuz-attacks-increased-highest-level-iran-war/story?id=138304456)). - **Trade.** Trump announced a **US–Philippines trade deal** on Nov 18. It replaces the 10% levy imposed after the Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs in February ([Philstar, Nov 18](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2026/11/18/2570994/trump-says-us-philippines-sealed-biggest-trade-deal)). - **Nintendo: Ocarina and Black Friday.** The Switch 2 Ocarina of Time remake sold **5.2M in four days**, making it Nintendo's fastest-selling first-party Switch 2 game and the console's No. 2 seller behind Mario Kart World ([Game Informer, Nov 11](https://gameinformer.com/2026/11/11/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-remake-is-nintendos-fastest-selling-switch-2-game)). The US Black Friday deal is the **Switch 2 + Mario Kart World bundle at $499.99**, $50 off, from Nov 22 to Dec 5, alongside an eShop sale ([Digital Citizen, Nov 19](https://www.digitalcitizen.life/nintendo-black-friday-2026-switch-2-mario-kart-world-bundle-499-eshop-sale-november-19/)). eShop gifting went live around Nov 17, but only within a Nintendo Account family group; patch notes and outlets disagree on Nov 16 vs. Nov 17 ([Nintendo Everything, Nov](https://nintendoeverything.com/?p=929302)). No Direct was announced this week.
- **GTA VI launches.** It came out Nov 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with no PC version. Rockstar claims **$3.79B in first-day retail sales**, while Sensor Tower estimates about **$2.36B on 24.6M units**. Rockstar's figure isn't independently verified ([WECT/AP, Nov 21](https://www.wect.com/2026/11/21/grand-theft-auto-vi-sets-record-with-379b-first-day-retail-sales/); [VGC, Nov](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/gta-6-sold-around-25-million-copies-and-made-2-36-billion-in-its-first-day-estimates-suggest/)).
- **Labor.** About **1,600 Rockstar UK staff** are voting on union recognition through **Nov 26**. A yes vote would create the UK industry's largest recognized union ([Fortune, Nov 20](https://fortune.com/2026/11/20/rockstar-games-union-vote-gta-vi/)). Ubisoft is cutting **166 jobs** in Montreal and Quebec ([Gematsu, Nov 19](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/ubisoft-lays-off-166-staff-at-montreal-and-quebec-studios)).
- **PlayStation and Xbox.** Sony puts PS5 sell-in **above 100M** ([PlayStation Blog, Nov 20](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/20/official-playstation-podcast-episode-553-peak-gaming)). Xbox's Black Friday sale started Nov 19 with about 2,000 games, following its 25th-anniversary showcase, which announced Wolfenstein 3 and gave a Project Helix update ([Xbox Wire, Nov 12](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/12/xbox-25th-anniversary-news-recap/)).

## Weather and Disruptions

**Bottom line:** Normal weekend: low 80s, a 30–40% chance of afternoon showers today, no tropical cyclones, but **PortMiami has a 10-ship day Sunday**.

- **Miami forecast.** Low pressure crossing the Gulf brings **afternoon showers today**; NBC6 puts the chance at about **30–40%**. Highs are in the low 80s. It clears Sunday and Monday, and showers return Tuesday (about 40%) and Wednesday (about 30%). Thanksgiving looks dry; outlets disagree on how cool it will be ([CBS Miami, Nov 20](https://www.cbsnews.com/miami/news/south-florida-weather-forecast-miami-fort-lauderdale-miami-dade-broward-november-20-2026/); [Caplin News, Nov 20](https://caplinnews.fiu.edu/weather-heavy-rainfall-flooding-broward-thanksgiving/)).
- **Tropics.** **No Atlantic tropical cyclones.** The NHC is watching a low along a front off the Southeast coast that could become subtropical as it moves north. It's a surf, rip-current and coastal-flooding risk for the Southeast and mid-Atlantic into early next week, not a Miami threat. Hurricane season ends Nov 30 ([NHC, Nov 20](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/gtwo.php)). Rough seas may cause more Bahamas skipped calls like this week's.
- **Port, rail and air.** **PortMiami has 10 ships in port Sunday, Nov 22**, so expect heavy traffic on Port Boulevard and the MacArthur Causeway ([Cruise Industry News, Nov](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/portmiami-closes-fy-2026-with-record-10-5m-cruise-guests/)). Brightline's flood suspension was only between Orlando and West Palm; full service was set to resume today, and Miami–West Palm trains ran normally ([Spectrum News 13, Nov 19](https://mynews13.com/fl/orlando/news/2026/11/19/brightline-suspends-service-between-orlando-and-west-palm-beach-on-friday)). I found no reports of MIA disruptions since the Nov 18 thunderstorm ground stop, which lasted 8:57–10:15am ([Terminair, Nov 18](https://terminair.com/story/147253-miami-airport-ground-stop-on-18-november-2026)).
- **Roads and water.** No Brickell or downtown closures have been posted for this weekend; I-395/SR 836 work continues ([I-395 project, Nov 20](https://i395-miami.com/construction-updates/)). **North Miami** issued a precautionary boil-water notice on Nov 18 for the area around NE 3rd Ct and 131st–135th St. *No lift has been reported as of Nov 20* ([WSVN, Nov 18](https://wsvn.com/news/local/miami-dade/north-miami-issues-water-boil-advisory-following-water-main-break/)). **Art Week (Nov 30–Dec 6)** will bring Miami Beach traffic; road-closure plans haven't been published yet ([City of Miami Beach, Nov 9](https://www.miamibeachfl.gov/miami-beach-once-again-becomes-center-of-global-art-world/)).
