## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US–Iran standoff over Hormuz is escalating at the margins with no deal in sight, while Thursday's Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen is the week's main diplomatic event.

- **Hormuz: no deal, blockade on.** As of Nov 16–17, the **US naval blockade of Iran remains in place** and there is **no agreement to reopen the strait**. Trump said on Nov 12 that "we don't need a deal with Iran" and pulled VP Vance off the talks ([CNBC, Nov 12](https://cnbc.com/2026/11/12/trump-iran-deal-hormuz-strait.html)). Iran has since escalated with drone attacks on US assets in Bahrain. Iran claims it destroyed a radar there; CENTCOM says it shot the drones down without damage. Trump has signaled he will wait Iran out ([CNN, Nov 15](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/15/middleeast/iran-bahrain-attacks-trump-intl)). *Reports conflict on whether any formal ceasefire is still in effect.* One report says Trump terminated it after US strikes, while other coverage describes diplomacy as still alive. Treat the ceasefire's status as unconfirmed ([CBS News live, Nov 16–17](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-nuclear-oil-strait-of-hormuz/)).
- **New mine claims; Oman talks.** Today the IRGC claimed that a **supertanker and an LPG carrier struck mines** in the strait's southern lane. There has been no independent confirmation. Iranian FM **Araghchi** is in Muscat for maritime-security talks with Oman ([The National, Nov 17](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/gulf/2026/11/17/iran-and-oman-discuss-maritime-security-amid-rising-attacks-in-strait-of-hormuz/)). - **Trump's Asia swing.** In Manila, Trump attends the **ASEAN summit**, is set to sign a **trade deal with Thailand**, holds the first second-term **Quad** meeting with Japan, India and Australia, and has a state visit with President Marcos on Wednesday. He then goes to APEC in Shenzhen for a **Thursday trilateral with Xi and Putin** focused on nuclear arms, plus a one-on-one with Putin ([PBS/AP, Nov 17](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/trumps-asia-reset-deals-in-manila-and-shenzhen-then-a-rare-nuclear-summit-with-xi-and-putin); [CBS News, Nov 17](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-asia-trip-trilateral-summit-putin-xi/)). Trump says he will raise Ukraine, but US officials reportedly briefed that it would not be on the agenda. He said **Kim Jong Un "may come"** to Shenzhen ([News4Jax/AP, Nov 16](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2026/11/16/the-latest-trump-says-he-plans-to-talk-about-war-in-ukraine-during-meeting-with-xi-and-putin/)).

The Shenzhen meeting is the next point where US pressure on Moscow could shift. *No ceasefire framework has been confirmed as of Nov 16.*

## Cruise Industry

**Bottom line:** There was no major Royal Caribbean corporate news in the past 48 hours. The industry story to watch today is whether MSC signs for control of Meyer Werft, one of the few yards that can build the largest cruise ships.

*Business / industry*

- **MSC–Meyer Werft.** Der Spiegel reported that Germany and Lower Saxony are close to giving **MSC the right to buy about 80% of Meyer Werft**, with a signing expected **"next Tuesday", which would be today**. The Meyer family would keep the other 20% under a joint arrangement ([Manila Times/Reuters, Nov 16](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/16/business/foreign-business/swiss-group-eyes-stake-in-german-shipbuilder/2470360); [gCaptain, Nov 16](https://gcaptain.com/msc-nears-deal-to-buy-majority-stake-in-german-shipyard-meyer-werft/)). *Lower Saxony reportedly denied a signing this week. That denial appears in only one source, and nothing is confirmed signed.* If the deal closes, a direct competitor would control one of the few yards able to build large cruise ships, which raises questions about slot access and pricing for other lines' future orders, including Royal Caribbean's.
- **Cruise stocks vs. macro.** Falling oil (Brent near $92) helps fuel costs, but the broad risk-off tape weighed on Monday (see Markets). This week's consumer read-through comes from **retail sales today and Walmart Thursday**.
