## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** Three active conflicts — Ukraine, Gaza, and Iran/Strait of Hormuz — all intensified over the weekend even as Washington notched a rare trade win with Beijing.

- **US-China cut tariffs in "30-for-30" deal.** Following last week's Trump-Xi summit, the US and China agreed to slash reciprocal tariffs on a combined roughly **$60 billion** in goods: Trump cut the China tariff rate to **47% from 57%**, while Beijing resumes US soybean purchases, pauses new rare-earth export restrictions, and commits to **10 million metric tons** of US coal imports in both 2027 and 2028. It's the most concrete de-escalation in the trade war since tariffs peaked near 145%/125% last year. ([CNN Business, Sep 28](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/28/business/us-china-tariff-cuts-60-billion-intl))
- **Russian strikes on Ukraine intensify.** Russia launched a mass drone/missile barrage overnight into Sept 27-28 — **170+ Shahed drones**, strikes on a Kyiv data center and Kyivstar HQ, and a guided bomb on a Kharkiv apartment block that injured at least 22 (outlets' overnight death tolls range 6-14, so treat as disputed). Zelensky says Trump made a "final decision" to license Ukrainian production of Patriot interceptors, but Trump has since declined to confirm it publicly, and any domestic production would take over a year — not addressing Ukraine's immediate air-defense shortage. ([Al Jazeera, Sep 28](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/28/russian-strikes-kill-eight-injure-dozens-across-ukraine))
- **Gaza ceasefire fragile, casualty toll disputed.** Nearly a year after the October 2025 deal, Gaza's Health Ministry says **more than 1,300** Palestinians have been killed since the truce took effect, while Israel continues near-daily strikes it says target Hamas/Islamic Jihad operatives violating it. Israel controls roughly **54%** of Gaza to Hamas's 46%, and four Israeli soldiers have died in incidents since the ceasefire began. ([Times of Israel, Sep 28](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-september-28-2026/))
- **Iran rejects US terms; Hormuz still effectively closed.** Iran's FM Araghchi proposed a seven-day roadmap — ending the US naval blockade, unfreezing assets, waiving oil sanctions — in exchange for reopening the Strait of Hormuz only on the final day; Trump rejected it Sept 26, though indirect talks reportedly remain open this week. Hormuz vessel transits remain near a standstill (as low as nine), a direct risk to global oil flows and cruise-line fuel costs. ([CBS News, Sep 27-28](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-strait-of-hormuz-7-day-proposal/))
- **New Russia/Iran sanctions law phases in by Oct 18.** The "Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026," signed Sept 18, authorizes tariffs up to **100%** on buyers of Russian oil/gas and extends Iran Sanctions Act authority to 2031; most measures take effect within three weeks. *As of signing, implementation was still pending — worth rechecking closer to Oct 18.* ([GlobalSecurity.org/RFE-RL, Sep 19](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/russia/2026/09/russia-260919-rferl02.htm))
- **Venezuela: opposition presses for elections.** Hundreds rallied in Caracas and other cities over the weekend demanding María Corina Machado's return and new presidential elections, months after Maduro's January capture (Operation Absolute Resolve) — the political transition remains unresolved. ([Al Jazeera, Sep 28](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/28/venezuelans-rally-for-opposition-leaders-return-and-presidential-elections))
