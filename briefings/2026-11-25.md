## Weather and Disruptions

**Bottom line:** Expect a washout in Miami today. Allow extra time for every trip, watch for street flooding near the coast and in Brickell at high tide, and plan for MIA/FLL delays. Thanksgiving Day clears out.

- **Flood watch through tonight.** The NWS flood watch covers coastal and metro **Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach**. It took effect at 7 p.m. Tuesday and runs to **1 a.m. Thursday**, with rain arriving in two waves today ([WLRN, Nov 24](https://www.wlrn.org/weather/2026-11-24/flood-watch-in-effect-for-the-east-coast-of-south-florida)). A CBS Miami live blog says the watch was extended to 7 a.m. Thursday for Miami-Dade and Broward, so check the latest NWS statement ([CBS Miami, Nov 24](https://www.cbsnews.com/miami/live-updates/south-florida-flooding-live-updates-heavy-rain-broward-miami-dade/)). Flood advisories were already up early this morning in parts of Miami-Dade and Palm Beach ([Local 10, Nov 25](https://www.local10.com/weather/2026/11/25/flood-advisory-issued-for-parts-of-south-florida-as-heavy-rain-continues/)).
- **Rain totals and severe risk.** Local 10 expects **3–6 inches**, with isolated totals near 10 inches possible through this evening. There is a **slight (Level 2 of 4) risk** of isolated tornadoes ([Local 10, Nov 25](https://www.local10.com/weather/2026/11/25/nov-25-south-florida-faces-another-significant-flood-threat/)). WLRN's forecast is lower, at 2–3 inches with a worst case of 4–6. The ground is already saturated after weeks of rain running 400–600% of normal in parts of Broward and Palm Beach ([WLRN, Nov 24](https://www.wlrn.org/weather/2026-11-24/flood-watch-in-effect-for-the-east-coast-of-south-florida)). WSVN also flags waterspouts and gusts that could reach 60 mph ([WSVN, Nov 24](https://wsvn.com/weather-blog/on-storm-watch-2)).
- **King tides make it worse.** This is the year's last king tide cycle, running Nov 23–26 in Miami-Dade. Peak levels fall in the **Tuesday–Wednesday high-tide cycles**, so rain and tidal flooding can overlap and leave deeper water that lasts longer ([WUSF, Nov 24](https://www.wusf.org/weather/2026-11-24/flood-risk-rises-across-florida-as-heavy-rain-threatens-ahead-of-thanksgiving)). Expect low-lying streets in Brickell, downtown and Miami Beach to take water around high tide. *I found no specific road-closure, Metromover or Brightline advisories as of this morning.*
- **Thursday improves.** The system moves into the Atlantic tonight, and northwest winds dry things out for Thanksgiving Day ([Local 10, Nov 25](https://www.local10.com/weather/2026/11/25/flood-advisory-issued-for-parts-of-south-florida-as-heavy-rain-continues/)). Coastal flooding may still return around Thursday's high tides ([WUSF, Nov 24](https://www.wusf.org/weather/2026-11-24/flood-risk-rises-across-florida-as-heavy-rain-threatens-ahead-of-thanksgiving)).
- **Air travel.** About **2 million passengers** are expected through MIA alone this week ([WSVN, Nov 24](https://wsvn.com/news/local/miami-dade/mia-fll-thanksgiving-travel-rush)). Forecasters see the East Coast's biggest weather-delay risk in the **Southeast today**, moving into the Northeast on Thursday ([Latin Times, Nov 24](https://www.latintimes.com/aaa-thanksgiving-2026-travel-forecast-601731)).
- **Tropics are quiet.** The NHC's early Wednesday outlook expects **no tropical cyclone formation in the next 7 days**, and no storms are active. Hurricane season ends Nov 30 ([NHC, Nov 25](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/gtwo.php)).
