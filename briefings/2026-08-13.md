# Morning Brief — Thursday, August 13, 2026

*The US-Iran war's Strait of Hormuz standoff is now the thread running through everything this morning — oil near five-session highs, an unusually hawkish Fed debate, and a Red Sea shipping squeeze that's rerouting global trade.*

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US-Iran war has entered a sixth month with the Strait of Hormuz the live flashpoint, and the resulting oil-price pressure is now the thread connecting markets, inflation, and shipping.

- **Hormuz standoff escalates.** *As of Aug 13,* Trump claims "total control" over the Strait of Hormuz via a naval blockade he calls a "wall of steel," but Iran disputes it; transit traffic sits near three-month lows and the **IEA warned this week** that reopening the waterway is becoming urgent as global oil stockpiles deplete. A June interim peace framework collapsed after the US was accused of violating it within 48 hours, and Iran's foreign minister has warned of a "bigger miscalculation" ahead. ([CNBC, Aug 13](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/13/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-irgc.html))
- **Russia-Ukraine grinds on.** Russia struck Ukraine with **133 drones** overnight into Aug 13 and killed two railway workers in the Odesa region; Moscow says it's "ready to consider" resuming talks but wants Washington to first clarify the disputed Anchorage summit outcome, which Secretary of State Rubio called "dead" in late July. The US Senate passed sweeping new bipartisan sanctions on Russia this week. *No negotiation breakthrough as of Aug 13.* ([RBC-Ukraine, Aug 13](https://newsukraine.rbc.ua/news/russia-says-it-is-ready-to-discuss-peace-1785166775.html))
- **Gaza ceasefire fraying.** The October 2025 ceasefire remains formally intact but is violated almost daily — an Israeli strike killed one in Beit Lahiya on Aug 12, and the IDF conducted a targeted drone strike on a Hamas commander despite pressure to halt such strikes. Hamas has dissolved its Gaza government while refusing to disarm under Trump's July 31 "Board of Peace" roadmap. ([Al Jazeera, Aug 12](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/12/israeli-air-strike-kills-one-in-northern-gaza))
- **Houthis blockade Red Sea.** Yemen's Houthis carried out their **first deadly Red Sea attack in over a year** on Aug 12, killing six aboard a cargo ship, continuing their July 20 blockade of Saudi-linked shipping; weekly Suez-area transits have fallen to **269 from 354**, and a shipper this week paid **$4 million** to skip a 10-day Panama Canal queue as vessels reroute around Africa. ([Semafor, Aug 12](https://www.semafor.com/article/08/12/2026/houthis-attack-red-sea-cargo-ship-in-first-deadly-strike-in-a-year))
- **US-China tariff standoff.** A new **12.5% Section 301 tariff** on Chinese goods took effect July 24 over forced-labor enforcement; Beijing retaliated Aug 5 with sanctions on seven US entities and new drone-export controls. The broader tariff truce runs through **Nov 10, 2026**, with no renewal talks confirmed. ([NBC News, Aug 5](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/china/china-announces-countermeasures-washington-sanctions-drone-exports-us-rcna590924))
