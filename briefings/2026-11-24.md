## Weather and Disruptions

**Bottom line:** Expect a wet commute home tonight and a flood-prone Wednesday. A flood watch covers Miami-Dade from Tuesday evening into early Thursday, with **1–3 inches** of rain likely and **4–6+** possible, plus king-tide coastal flooding.

- **Flood watch tonight.** NWS Miami issued a flood watch for coastal and metro Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach at 4:16 a.m. today. It runs from about **7 p.m. Tuesday to 1 a.m. Thursday**. The office expects 1–3 inches, with a reasonable worst case of 4–6 inches, mostly along the east coast. Sources disagree on the exact end time: the alert listing says 1 a.m. Nov 26, and earlier coverage said "early Thursday" ([GovOneStop/NWS, Nov 24](https://govonestop.com/nws/florida-severe-flood-watch-effective-11242026-0116); [Hastings Tribune/AP, Nov 23](https://www.hastingstribune.com/ap/national/severe-weather-could-hit-south-florida-as-flood-watch-goes-into-effect-tuesday/article_487867eb-6f54-50e3-a3c8-256a04e1957a.html)).
- **Saturated ground, king tides.** Rain over the past 30 days has run **200–300% of normal** across parts of Miami-Dade and 400–600% in parts of Broward and Palm Beach. Forecasters also warn of king-tide coastal flooding from today through the end of the week. Low-lying Brickell and downtown streets are the usual trouble spots ([CiberCuba, Nov 24](https://en.cibercuba.com/noticias/2026-11-24-u1-e135253-s27061-nid346491-nueva-vigilancia-inundaciones-miami-dade-broward)).
- **Why it matters: last week's flood.** The Nov 18 storm dumped nearly 12 inches near Plantation, closed Miami-Dade and Broward public schools and led DeSantis to add Miami-Dade to a state executive order. NWS says the risk of severe storms rises if the Gulf low moves inland ([CBS Miami, Nov 18](https://www.cbsnews.com/miami/live-updates/south-florida-flooding-live-updates-heavy-rain-broward-miami-dade/); [NBC Miami, Nov 19](https://www.nbcmiami.com/weather/stories-weather/desantis-expands-executive-order-rains-flooding/3890641/)).
- **Front, then a cooler holiday.** A cold front should clear the rain for a drier, cooler Thanksgiving. Outlets disagree on timing: one has the front arriving Wednesday, another Thursday ([Yahoo/Palm Beach Post, Nov 23](https://www.yahoo.com/news/weather-news/articles/another-storm-system-tracked-thanksgiving-232954408.html)).
- **Tropics quiet.** The NHC's Atlantic outlook today says **no tropical cyclone formation is expected in the next 7 days**. Hurricane season ends Nov 30 ([NHC, Nov 24](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/gtwo.php?basin=atlc&fdays=7)).
- **Other disruptions.** No confirmed reports were found of Metromover or Brightline problems, PortMiami operational issues, or boil-water notices. *Check the county's alerts before the evening commute, since flooding on surface streets is the main risk.*

## National

**Bottom line:** The White House is setting up fights with the Democratic Congress that takes over in January, and federal funding runs out **Dec 11** with no deal yet. Meanwhile the President's intervention at Nvidia moved trillions in market value.

- **Huang back at Nvidia.** Nvidia's board voted **unanimously** on Nov 23 to reinstate Jensen Huang, 11 days after he resigned. He left amid an investigation into an affair with a woman US intelligence suspects of spying for China. In an 8-K, the board said it had input from "senior levels of the United States government" and is confident Huang poses no national security risk. The independent directors' investigation continues ([SEC 8-K, Nov 23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm); [Axios, Nov 23](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-board-jensen-huang-as-ceo)). Trump confirmed he personally lobbied the board, directly and indirectly ([NBC News, Nov 23](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-rcna610864)). Governance critics point out that the board had no chair and no succession plan before the ouster ([CNN, Nov 23](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/23/tech/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-corporate-governance)).
- **Order limits congressional probes.** Trump signed an executive order requiring government contractors to get **White House approval** before complying with congressional investigations. It is a preemptive move against the incoming Democratic majorities ([CNN, Nov 23](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/23/politics/trump-democrats-investigations-congress); [NPR, Nov 23](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/23/nx-s1-6050940/if-you-can-keep-it-congressional-oversight-part-2)). Speaker-designate Hakeem Jeffries said on CBS that health care could be an area for cooperation ([Everything Briefing, Nov 23](https://everythingbriefing.substack.com/p/todays-news-history-and-calendar-9f1)).

The National Flood Insurance Program also lapses with funding, which matters for Florida ([Argentum, Nov 23](https://www.argentum.org/congress-faces-year-end-deadlines-as-long-term-care-scrutiny-builds/)). *Status as of Nov 23. ## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The Iran war is still causing damage in the Gulf. Hormuz traffic remains severely restricted, Iran has resumed strikes on Bahrain, and Washington has put major new strikes off until January. That keeps oil, and with it inflation and Fed risk, elevated.

- **Hormuz still restricted.** For the week ending Nov 18, UKMTO counted **263 transits, about 65% below pre-war levels**, and six attacks on tankers. The Joint Maritime Information Center rates the threat as severe ([Iran International, Nov 21](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611215184)). A live tracker listed the strait as "severely restricted" as of Nov 23, 18:59 UTC ([hormuz.now, Nov 23](https://hormuz.now/faq)). *No reopening deal has been announced as of Nov 23.* Counts vary by method: one daily brief cites IMF PortWatch data showing far fewer transits on individual days ([Straits.live, Nov 21](https://straits.live/briefs/2026-11-21)). The US blockade of Iranian ports, in place since **Jul 14**, was still being enforced *as of Nov 21; unconfirmed since*.
- **Strikes delayed to 2027.** Trump told Reuters he would decide on major new Iran strikes "after the beginning of the year," despite Netanyahu pressing for a short joint campaign. His advisers are split between more strikes and continuing the economic blockade ([Iran International, Nov 23](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611231814); [Ynet, Nov 23](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/sjz00i2xkmx)).
- **Iran hits Bahrain again.** Bahrain says an Iranian strike hit a civilian industrial facility. It was about the **fourth attempted attack in a week**, after a lull of about two months, set off by the US strike on an IRGC missile site on Qeshm Island on Nov 17. Treasury issued new Iran-related sanctions on Nov 23. Separately, the Houthis say a Saudi strike killed 17 people ([Khaleej Times, Nov 23](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/mena/us-israel-iran-saudi-arabia-yemen-war-live-updates-november-23-2026)). Israeli strikes in Lebanon continue despite a fragile ceasefire, *as of Nov 23* (same source).
- **Ukraine: no ceasefire.** The Kremlin has not responded to Zelensky's Nov 14 proposal for a localized ceasefire along the Dnipro. It is instead pushing an arrangement near Kostyantynivka that ISW calls infeasible. Russia launched **134 drones** overnight, and Western intelligence reportedly fears Russia could launch a nuclear-armed satellite within weeks ([Kyiv Post/ISW, Nov 23](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/90994)). *As of Nov 23, the US-brokered process has produced no announced breakthrough.*
- **Israel coalition talks.** Eisenkot and Bennett are reportedly split over setting Likud a deadline in unity-government talks, and Likud is demanding that Bennett's party be excluded ([Times of Israel, Nov 23](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-november-23-2026/)).

## AI

**Bottom line:** This week's AI news is about markets and policy rather than new models. Nvidia's CEO reversal and the post-IPO swings in Anthropic stock are driving the sector, and governors are pushing back against federal preemption of state AI rules.

- **Nvidia leadership restored.** The world's key AI-chip supplier has its founder back as CEO (see National and Markets). The market treated his ouster as a risk to the whole AI buildout.
- **Governors want a say.** The National Governors Association's bipartisan AI council released principles on Nov 22. They call for independent audits or evaluations of frontier models, reporting of serious safety incidents, and no broad congressional override of state AI laws. A draft federal framework reportedly would preempt state laws for **three years** ([PYMNTS, Nov 23](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/governors-want-states-to-have-a-voice-in-regulating-ai/)).
