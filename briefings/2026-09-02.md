# Morning Brief — Wednesday, September 02, 2026

*Iran struck US-linked bases in Jordan and Bahrain overnight in retaliation for Tuesday's US strikes on Iranian targets near the Strait of Hormuz, sending oil toward $95/barrel and knocking down equities and cruise stocks alike — the story that touches nearly every section below.*

## National

**Bottom line:** Iran retaliated against US bases in Jordan and Bahrain overnight, escalating the conflict Washington opened Tuesday, even as Congress separately cleared a stopgap bill that avoids a government shutdown through December.

- **Iran hits US bases.** Iran launched missile and drone strikes on US-linked sites in Jordan (Camp Titin, near the Gulf of Aqaba) and Bahrain (near the US Navy's 5th Fleet headquarters in Manama), with reported strikes toward Kuwait as well, retaliating for Tuesday's US strikes on IRGC targets near the Strait of Hormuz. Jordanian air defenses reportedly intercepted **10 of 13** incoming missiles and two US officials said there were no American casualties in Jordan; Trump had warned Iran would be hit "at a much harder and higher level" if it retaliated. ([CBS News, Sep 2](https://www.cbsnews.com))
- **Shutdown averted.** The House passed a stopgap funding bill **370-48** on Tuesday, funding the government through **December 11** and pushing the fight past the midterms; the Senate had already passed it and it now heads to Trump's desk. ([Bloomberg, Sep 1](https://www.bloomberg.com))
- **Markets slide on the conflict.** The Dow fell **419 points** to 52,766.88, the S&P 500 dropped to 7,631.47, and the Nasdaq lost 271 points to 26,099.77 on Tuesday as attention shifted from earnings to the Middle East; Brent crude jumped roughly **5% toward $95/barrel**, its highest since late July. ([Yahoo Finance/CNBC, Sep 1](https://www.cnbc.com))
- **Fed calendar looms.** Under new Chair Kevin Warsh, the Fed holds its rate at **3.50%–3.75%** ahead of Friday's (Sept 4) August jobs report (consensus ~**55,000** added), CPI on Sept 11, and the FOMC decision Sept 15–16. ([Bloomberg, Aug 29](https://www.bloomberg.com))
- **Army Secretary resigns.** Dan Driscoll, the youngest-ever Army secretary, resigned Monday after months of friction with Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth over general officer firings and blocked promotions — added Pentagon churn as the Iran conflict continues. ([NPR, Sep 1](https://www.npr.org))
- **Red Sox stay in the hunt.** Boston beat Seattle 9-8 in 10 innings on a Trevor Story walk-off Monday, its 34th comeback win of the season, before losing 9-6 to the Mariners Tuesday; Boston sits **75-64**, in wild-card position as September begins. ([Boston Globe, Sep 1](https://www.bostonglobe.com))

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** Every major conflict front — Iran, Ukraine, Gaza, and US-China trade — is active and unresolved, with the Iran-US exchange the most dangerous escalation of the group.

- **Iran-US ceasefire in tatters.** Beyond the overnight strikes on Jordan and Bahrain, Kuwait and Bahrain separately reported Iranian drone activity; Iran's president said Tuesday Tehran would return to the June ceasefire/MOU if Washington does too. Core disputes — Iran's nuclear program, Strait of Hormuz access, sanctions relief — remain unresolved *as of Sept 1; status fluid.* ([Times of Israel, Sep 1](https://www.timesofisrael.com))
- **Russia escalates on Kyiv.** A Russian ballistic-missile strike hit a Kyiv rail depot overnight Sept 1, killing at least **12** (including 6 rail workers) in the sixth straight day of attacks, followed by **174 drones** and Iskander missiles into Sept 2. Zelensky warned Russian airspace will be "de facto closed" to foreign airlines. *Peace talks are stalled as of Aug 27–Sept 1: the Kremlin says no new proposals are on the table; unconfirmed since.* ([Kyiv Independent / PBS, Sep 1](https://kyivindependent.com))
- **Gaza ceasefire still fraying.** The truce in effect since Oct 2025 remains "neither war nor peace" — the IDF struck a Hamas commander in Jabalia this week, calling it a response to a "systematic violation." *As of Sept 1; no Phase 2 progress on the 20-point plan reported.* ([Times of Israel liveblog, Sep 1](https://www.timesofisrael.com))
- **New China tariffs ahead of Xi visit.** Washington is set to add a **7.5%** tariff on Chinese goods over alleged manufacturing overcapacity, pushing the effective China tariff rate back to roughly **20%**, layered on the 12.5% forced-labor tariff in effect since July. This comes just ahead of Xi Jinping's planned **Sept 24–25** White House visit, which the US has scheduled but Beijing hasn't formally confirmed. ([Bloomberg, Aug 24](https://www.bloomberg.com))
- **Red Sea risk resurfaces.** A mid-August Houthi attack in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait killed six seafarers — the first Red Sea shipping fatalities in over a year — reviving concern for the corridor carrying **12–15%** of global trade, compounding Strait of Hormuz risk from the wider Iran-US conflict. *As of Aug 12–24; unconfirmed since.* ([UN News / CNBC, Aug](https://news.un.org))
