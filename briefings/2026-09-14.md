## AI

**Bottom line:** Anthropic's Amodei, OpenAI's Altman and Elon Musk jointly called this weekend for the AI industry to slow down — an unusual alignment among rivals that the White House rejected within a day, citing the race with China.

- **Amodei calls for pacing the frontier.** Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a roughly 3,800-word essay Saturday, Sept 12, warning that within **6-12 months** a swarm of AI agents could become capable of causing hundreds of billions of dollars in damage across the internet, and proposing independent, publishing-empowered safety reviewers inside frontier labs plus shared standards among democratic nations. ([Washington Post, Sep 12](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/09/12/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-calls-ai-industry-slow-down/); [Axios, Sep 12](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/12/anthropic-ai-amodei-pacing))

The Trump administration rejected the call Sunday, arguing a slowdown would cede ground to China in the AI race. ([Bloomberg, Sep 14](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-14/ai-bosses-risk-clash-with-wall-street-and-trump-over-safety-call))

([Bloomberg, Sep 11](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/anthropic-says-us-adversaries-aimed-claude-at-weapons-research); [Axios, Sep 12](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/12/anthropic-ai-threat-report-russia-iran-china))
