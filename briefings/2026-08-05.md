# Morning Brief — Wednesday, August 05, 2026

*Wall Street closed at fresh records Tuesday — the Dow above 54,000 for the first time ever — on a mix of strong earnings and easing Middle East tension, while Royal Caribbean's raised full-year guidance keeps the cruise sector's own rally intact even as Perfect Day Mexico stalls and rivals trim outlooks.*

## Markets

**Bottom line:** All three major indices closed at or near records Tuesday on strong earnings and Strait of Hormuz optimism, but oil ticked back up in early Wednesday trade on a fresh shipping threat and Friday's jobs report is the next big catalyst.

- **Record close across the board.** The **S&P 500 rose 1.79% to 7,737** — its first record in two months — while the **Dow surged 1.71% to close above 54,000** for the first time and the **Nasdaq jumped 2.59% to 26,585**. Drivers included strong corporate earnings, a tech rebound (**Palantir +29%** on an AI-driven revenue surge, its second-best day on record), and optimism over a possible Strait of Hormuz reopening deal; Amazon's market cap topped **$3 trillion** for the first time. ([CNBC, Aug 4](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/stock-market-today-live-updates.html))
- **Oil, dollar swing on Hormuz talk.** WTI crude slid roughly **$4.57 to $75.77** Tuesday as Qatar reportedly drafted an interim proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, with Washington and Tehran signaling progress — but a fresh threat against Middle East shipping in Wednesday premarket trade pared some of that optimism. The **dollar index fell to 99.84**, on pace for its worst week in three months, after softer JOLTS data and a surprise 0.3% drop in factory orders. ([Bloomberg, Aug 5](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/latest-oil-market-and-analysis-for-aug-5); [Vantage Markets, Aug 5](https://www.vantagemarkets.com/market-analysis/us-dollar-index-dxy-forecast-worst-week-august-3-7-2026/))
- **AMD beats, falls; Caterpillar jumps.** **AMD posted Q2 EPS of $1.66** on **$11.5B revenue**, beating consensus, and guided Q3 revenue above expectations at **$12.7–13.3B** — yet shares still fell more than **8%**. Caterpillar rose **5.6%** on record quarterly revenue. ([Yahoo Finance, Aug 4](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/amd-to-report-q2-earnings-as-chip-stocks-continue-to-waver-110000620.html))
- **Disney, McDonald's report today.** **Disney reports fiscal Q3 results before Wednesday's open**, with analysts expecting adjusted EPS of $1.88 (+16.8% y/y) and revenue up over 7% to $25.4B — its strongest top-line growth in three-plus years; McDonald's, Eli Lilly, Novo Nordisk, Shopify and Uber also report around Aug 5–6. SpaceX shares fell 10% in its first post-IPO quarterly report on capex jumping sixfold to $18.4B. ([Yahoo Finance, Aug 5](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/walt-disney-dis-reports-next-140022992.html))
- **Fed, jobs report ahead.** The **10-year Treasury yield sits at 4.63%**, down from last week's 4.74% high, with markets pricing roughly **65% odds of a September rate hike** after the Fed held rates unchanged in July with three dissents. The **BLS July jobs report lands Friday, Aug 7 at 8:30am ET** — the next major catalyst for rates and the dollar. ([Penn Mutual Asset Management, Aug 3](https://www.pennmutualam.com/market-insights-news/blogs/monday-morning-perspectives/2026-08-03-us-treasury-yields-setting-fresh-2026-highs))

## Cruise Industry

**Bottom line:** Royal Caribbean beat and raised guidance again even as Perfect Day Mexico stalls indefinitely, while NCLH and Carnival both trimmed their outlooks — a split picture heading into the back half of the year.

- **Royal Caribbean beats, raises again.** Royal Caribbean Group's **Q2 adjusted EPS of $4.21 beat estimates of $3.93** on strong close-in demand and lower costs; the company **raised full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $17.73–$17.87** (~14% growth), backed by **6.6% capacity growth**, though it flagged a modest booking impact on select itineraries from prolonged geopolitical activity. ([PR Newswire, Jul 28](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/royal-caribbean-group-reports-second-quarter-results-above-expectations-and-raises-full-year-guidance-302836372.html))
- **Perfect Day Mexico stalls.** CEO **Jason Liberty confirmed on the Q2 call** that Perfect Day Mexico (Mahahual) will **miss its planned 2027 opening**, after Mexico's Environmental Agency ruled against the project and President Claudia Sheinbaum's government objected on ecological grounds. No new opening date was given; Liberty said Royal Caribbean still wants a negotiated compromise. ([Cruise Critic, Jul 28](https://www.cruisecritic.com/news/royal-caribbean-group-discovery-class-silversea-plans-perfect-day-mexico-delay-celebrity-river))
- **NCLH and Carnival trim outlooks.** Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings beat its own Q2 guidance but **lowered its full-year outlook**, with the beat driven by cost cuts — it identified another **$100M in annualized savings**, bringing total identified savings past **$500M** over three years; Freedom Broker cut its price target to $21. Separately, Carnival's *most recent guidance (late June, still standing as of early August)* trimmed net yield growth to **roughly 1.75–2.25%**, a full point below prior guidance, on weaker close-in Mediterranean bookings tied to Middle East disruption. ([GlobeNewswire, Jul 30](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/30/3335864/24500/en/Norwegian-Cruise-Line-Holdings-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results.html); [TravelersToday, Jun 24](https://www.travelerstoday.com/articles/60295/20260624/carnival-posts-record-67b-q2-revenue-ccl-stock-falls-iran-war-clips-yield-outlook.htm))
- **Explora III sets sail.** MSC's luxury brand **Explora Journeys named its third ship, Explora III**, in Barcelona and departed on its maiden seven-night voyage to Lisbon via Ibiza, Puerto Banús, Tangier and Portimão. It's the brand's first LNG-powered ship and marks the halfway point toward a planned six-ship fleet. ([Cruise Industry News, Aug 3](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/08/explora-iii-sets-sail-on-maiden-voyage/))
- **Celebrity hikes gratuities.** Celebrity Cruises, a Royal Caribbean Group brand, **raised automatic daily gratuities by $1.50** with no advance notice — standard staterooms to **$19.50/day**, suites to $24.50 — while Royal Caribbean's flagship brand has held its rate since October 2024; the move is being watched as a possible bellwether for a group-wide increase. Carnival separately began piloting an autonomous hull-cleaning robot aboard Carnival Pride to cut fuel use. ([Royal Caribbean Blog, Jul 29](https://www.royalcaribbeanblog.com/2026/07/29/celebrity-cruise-line-increases-gratuities); [Marine Log, Aug 4](https://www.marinelog.com/technology/carnival-cruise-line-pilots-jotun-hull-skater-on-carnival-pride/))

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The Strait of Hormuz diplomacy driving Tuesday's market rally remains unconfirmed as a deal, Gaza's ceasefire is fraying over a disarmament dispute Netanyahu now says Israel never agreed to, and a new 50% Canada tariff is set to hit August 19.

- **Hormuz talk still unconfirmed.** Market optimism about a Strait of Hormuz reopening rests on reports that **Qatar drafted an interim proposal** and that Washington and Tehran signaled progress — but no deal has been announced, and a fresh shipping threat surfaced in Wednesday premarket trade, pushing oil back up. *Treat as an unconfirmed, developing state, not a done deal.* ([Bloomberg, Aug 5](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/latest-oil-market-and-analysis-for-aug-5))
- **Gaza ceasefire fraying.** **Netanyahu said for the first time** that Israel has **not agreed** to the Hamas-disarmament plan the Board of Peace announced July 31 and that Hamas said it accepted; Israel says it won't withdraw from current lines until Hamas is "completely disarmed." The October 2025 ceasefire nominally holds, but Israel has expanded territorial control from **53% to over 60%** of Gaza, and strikes have killed **1,000+ Palestinians** since the truce began. *As of Aug 4.* ([Times of Israel, Aug 4](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-august-4-2026/))
- **Russia-Ukraine strikes escalate.** Long-range missile and drone attacks killed **at least nine people** in the 24 hours to Aug 4 (five in Russia, four in Ukraine); Russia set a wartime record in July with **381 missiles launched** (~12/day), while its spring-summer offensive has failed to produce significant territorial gains after two and a half months. Peace talks remain stalled, with Putin still demanding full control of Donbas as a precondition for any leaders' meeting. ([Al Jazeera, Aug 4](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/nine-killed-in-escalating-long-range-strikes-between-russia-and-ukraine))
- **New Canada tariff looms.** A **50% tariff on certain Canadian goods**, invoked under Section 338 of the Tariff Act, is set to **take effect August 19** — a sharp escalation beyond the 35% IEEPA tariff that had applied to non-USMCA-compliant goods. *Not yet in effect.* Separately, the **US-China tariff truce** (holding bilateral tariffs down from 125% toward 10%) remains the operative framework through its **November 10 expiration**, with no reporting of an earlier collapse *as of early August*. ([Wiley, Jul 2026](https://www.wiley.law/alert-President-Trump-Imposes-New-50-Tariffs-on-Certain-Canadian-Imports))
- **EU's 21st Russia sanctions package.** The EU's 21st sanctions package, adopted July 23, begins staggered enforcement this month: **33 additional Russian banks** join the financial transaction ban from **Aug 13**, alongside new crypto-provider transaction bans and asset freezes on 18 oil-sector entities. ([Cooley, Aug 4](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-04-the-eu-21st-russian-sanctions-package-is-here-business-implications))

## Florida and Miami

**Bottom line:** A judge ordered DeSantis's property-tax ballot amendment rewritten as misleading, with sheriffs and firefighters now warning it could cost local governments up to $12B a year.

- **Amendment 3 ballot language rejected.** Leon County Circuit Judge **David Frank ruled** the ballot title for DeSantis's property-tax constitutional amendment — "Save Our Homes From Excessive Property Taxes" — reads more like "a political slogan" than a neutral description, and ordered it rewritten. **AG James Uthmeier has 10 days** to revise the language before counties need it for printing ahead of the Sept. 24–Oct. 1 vote-by-mail window; the amendment itself stays on the November ballot. ([Florida Phoenix, Aug 3](https://floridaphoenix.com/2026/08/03/florida-sheriffs-association-is-significantly-concerned-about-amendment-3/))
- **Marlins reshape roster at deadline.** Miami traded catcher **Liam Hicks** to Tampa Bay, sent lefty **Braxton Garrett** to the Cubs, and dealt **Connor Norby** to Colorado — all for prospects — while holding onto outfielder Kyle Stowers despite Astros interest, continuing a prospect-focused rebuild. - **Miami-Dade District 2 race.** Incumbent **Marleine Bastien** faces two challengers for the Miami-Dade Commission's District 2 seat (Miami, North Miami, Opa-locka, Hialeah); early voting runs **Aug 3–16** with Election Day **Aug 18**. ([Local10, Aug 3](https://www.local10.com/news/local/2026/08/03/trio-vies-for-commission-seat-in-miami-dades-district-2/))
