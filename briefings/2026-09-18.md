# Morning Brief — Friday, September 18, 2026

*The Fed hiked rates 25bp to 3.75%–4.00% Wednesday — its first increase since 2023 — and markets shrugged it off with a two-day rally, but a Houthi seizure of Yemen's Red Sea coast has just threatened the alternate oil route that was supposed to cushion the Iran-war risk premium. Oil, not the Fed, is now the wildcard for inflation, cruise fuel costs, and the October rate decision alike.*

## Markets

**Bottom line:** The Fed's first hike since 2023 briefly spooked markets Wednesday, but easing oil and yields drove the best stock rally since early August on Thursday — watch three Fed speakers and housing data today, then PCE on Sept 30.

- **Fed hikes to 3.75%–4.00%.** FOMC voted 12-0 to raise the target range a quarter point, citing inflation still running above target amid oil-driven price pressure from the Iran war; Chair Kevin Warsh said inflation remains "too high" even as the labor market holds up. ([Federal Reserve, Sept 16](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a.htm))
- **Stocks stage sharp rebound.** S&P 500 closed at **7,637.76 (+1.14%)** and the Nasdaq **+1.61%** Thursday — their best day since early August — on dip-buying in AI/semis (Intel, Micron, AMD) as falling oil and Treasury yields eased inflation anxiety; futures were higher again Friday morning. ([TheStreet, Sept 17](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-sept-17-2026))
- **Yields, dollar ease off post-Fed spike.** The 10-year Treasury yield hit a **19-year high above 5.01%** right after the hike Wednesday before easing to **4.94%** by Thursday's close; the dollar index broke above 100 for the first time in seven weeks and is holding just above that level. ([MacroRadar](https://www.macroradar.io/10-year-treasury-yield); [TradingEconomics](https://tradingeconomics.com/dxy:cur))
- **Oil retreats on Saudi pipeline-repair news.** WTI fell to **$101.21/bbl** and Brent to **$103.61** Thursday as Saudi Arabia moves to restore its East-West pipeline — an alternate route around Hormuz — though crude remains up roughly **62% year-over-year** on the broader Iran-conflict risk premium (see Foreign Policy). ([CNBC, Sept 7](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/oil-prices-rise-to-6-week-high-after-iran-and-u-s-trade-blows-saudi-aramco-facilities-reportedly-hit.html); [Fortune, Sept 16](https://fortune.com/article/price-of-oil-09-16-2026/))
- **Lennar misses, cuts guidance.** Q3 adjusted EPS of **$1.23** missed the **$2.00** year-ago figure by a wide margin on a 6% revenue decline; Lennar trimmed full-year delivery guidance to **80,000–81,000 homes**, the first housing-sector read on this week's rate move. ([Lennar IR, Sept 16](https://investors.lennar.com/press-releases/2026/09-16-2026-213038087))
- **On deck:** Housing starts/permits and three Fed speakers (Daly, Harker, Bostic) land today; markets price roughly **50/50 odds** of another hike at the Oct 28 FOMC meeting; August PCE data (Sept 30) lands the same day as Micron earnings, with Nike reporting Sept 24. ([BLS calendar](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm); [centralbank.watch](https://centralbank.watch/federal-reserve/))

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** Houthi forces have seized Yemen's Red Sea coast and the Bab-el-Mandeb chokepoint in their biggest land grab in years, forcing a Saudi pipeline shutdown, while Iran, Gaza and Ukraine all remain unresolved conflicts heading into next week's Trump-Xi summit.

- **Houthis seize Red Sea coast.** Yemen's Houthi rebels captured the country's Red Sea coastline, including Mayun island at the Bab-el-Mandeb Strait entrance, forcing the shutdown of a critical Saudi oil pipeline and reigniting the 12-year civil war — a direct threat to the alternate export route markets were counting on. ([NPR, Sept 18](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/18/nx-s1-5973810/houthi-attacks-saudi-oil-world-markets))
- **Gaza ceasefire nominal, violations continue.** The October 2025 ceasefire technically holds, but Gaza's Health Ministry says Israel has violated it for **343 consecutive days**; a Sept 17 building collapse killed 21, underscoring the toll nearly a year in. ([AP/Click2Houston, Sept 18](https://www.click2houston.com/news/world/2026/09/18/fatal-building-collapse-highlights-dangers-plaguing-gaza-nearly-a-year-after-ceasefire/))
- **Iran war unresolved, IAEA locked out.** No durable ceasefire has held since a July 8 collapse; Iran continues denying inspectors access to bombed nuclear sites despite a Sept 9 IAEA resolution, with roughly **440kg** of enriched uranium unaccounted for. *As of Sept 16; unconfirmed since.* ([GlobalSecurity.org, Sept 16](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/ops/iran-war-oprep.htm))
- **Ukraine war active, peace push stalled.** Russian strikes hit Dnipropetrovsk and Odesa overnight; a Kremlin meeting between Putin and US envoys Witkoff and Kushner produced no breakthrough, and Russia held parliamentary elections today in occupied Ukrainian territory that Kyiv called a sham. ([Sunday Guardian, Sept 18](https://sundayguardianlive.com/world/russia-ukraine-war-latest-news-ukraine-says-russian-parliamentary-elections-bear-no-resemblance-to-democracy-as-cross-border-attacks-continue-287562/))
- **Trump-Xi summit set for Sept 23–25.** Xi will visit Washington for a follow-up to their May Beijing meeting; the US and China are discussing cutting tariffs on energy and agricultural goods beforehand. ([Bloomberg, Sept 18](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-18/traders-eye-xi-trump-meet-for-clues-on-ai-rivalry-yuan-outlook))
- **Canada trade war at an impasse.** Canada's retaliatory tariffs (15%–50%) on **C$27.6B** of US goods took effect Sept 8 with no talks scheduled; PM Mark Carney says Canada is effectively "at war" with the US on trade. *As of Sept 8–15; unconfirmed since.* ([Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/world/us-politics/article-canada-us-trade-talks-reach-an-impasse/))
