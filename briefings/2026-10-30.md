## Cruise Industry

**Bottom line:** Both Royal Caribbean and Norwegian beat and raised this week, but fuel near five-month highs is the swing factor for cruise stocks.

- **Royal Caribbean raises.** Q3 adjusted EPS was **$6.57** against ~$6.38–6.45 expected; full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance rose to **$17.85–$17.95** from $17.73–$17.87, with Q4 guided to **$3.48–$3.58**. Yields ran 60 bps above guidance on close-in demand in Europe and the Caribbean, and management reiterated the path to a **~$20 EPS in 2027**. Revenue (~$5.6B) was marginally light, and part of the EPS beat (~$0.26 vs. midpoint) was cost timing. ([Cruise Industry News, Oct 27](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/royal-caribbean-group-reports-q3-2026-results-raises-full-year-guidance/); [Globe and Mail/PR Newswire, Oct 27](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/RCL/pressreleases/8470953/royal-caribbean-group-reports-third-quarter-results-above-expectations-and-raises-full-year-guidance/))

([Skift, Oct 29](https://skift.com/2026/10/29/norwegian-cruise-lines-third-quarter-earnings-turnaround-progress); [NCLH 8-K, Oct 29](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001513761/000117184326006957/exh_991.htm))
