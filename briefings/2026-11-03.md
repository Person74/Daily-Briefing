## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US-Iran conflict is unresolved: diplomatic signals Monday drove oil down, but a Nov 3 report of US strikes in the Strait of Hormuz leaves the direction unclear.

- **Hormuz, newest.** A US official said American forces **struck Iranian targets in the strait**; what comes next is unclear. The US naval blockade of Iranian ports continues, and mediators (Qatar, Pakistan, Oman) are pushing a framework swapping an end to Iranian attacks for lifting the blockade. ([CP24, Nov 3](https://www.cp24.com/news/world/2026/11/03/us-strikes-iranian-targets-in-the-strait-of-hormuz-official-says-leaving-unclear-what-comes-next/))
- **Iran's position.** Iran cites a "seven-step proposal," wants the blockade lifted, sanctions eased and frozen assets released, and says ships must use routes it authorizes; it reportedly denies direct talks with the US. Four tankers reportedly hit mines. The June ceasefire collapsed July 8. Shipping remains far below the roughly 130 daily transits before the war. ([CBS News live updates](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-strait-of-hormuz-sanctions/); [Gulf News, Nov 2](https://gulfnews.com/business/energy/oil-plunges-8-as-us-iran-peace-hopes-ease-hormuz-fears-1.500733408))
- **US implications.** Fuel costs are a headwind for cruise lines (RCL cited higher fuel in its Q3 guidance) and drive inflation and Fed expectations.
- **Ukraine.** *As of Oct 19, latest I could verify:* Russia struck Kyiv, Ukraine and Russia were finalizing an evacuation from Oleshky, and Trump said Zelenskyy never wanted to end the war. No fresh Nov 1-3 developments found; no peace deal confirmed. ([Ukrainska Pravda, Oct 19](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/main/date_19102026/))
