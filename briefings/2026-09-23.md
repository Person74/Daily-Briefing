## Cruise Industry

**Bottom line:** Royal Caribbean Group's $3B Sandals joint venture — its biggest deal ever — dominates the sector today, with Carnival's Q3 earnings and a possible MSC-Meyer Werft shipyard deal the next catalysts to watch.

- **RCL's $3B Sandals JV.** Royal Caribbean Group agreed to pay roughly **$3 billion** for a **50% stake in Sandals Resorts International** (Sandals & Beaches), valuing the all-inclusive operator at about $6B and creating a 50-50 joint venture across **20 resorts** in Jamaica, the Bahamas, Turks and Caicos, and elsewhere in the Caribbean. ## Foreign Policy

([CNN, Sep 21](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/21/middleeast/yemen-houthis-offensive-intl))
