## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The Gulf situation got worse over the weekend: Iran claimed more tanker mine strikes and a drone attack on Bahrain while Hormuz traffic stays far below normal. Trump, Xi and Putin are set to meet Thursday in Shenzhen.

- **Hormuz: effectively closed.** As of Nov 14–15, commercial transits through the Strait are about **65% below pre-war levels**, according to UK Maritime Trade Operations. The UN reports that October had the most shipping incidents of any month since the conflict began ([The National, Nov 15](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/gulf/2026/11/15/bahrain-issues-first-nationwide-alert-in-months-as-iran-claims-attack-on-base-hosting-us-helicopters/); [UN News, Nov 2026](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/11/1168804)). Iran's IRGC said tankers hit mines and caught fire Saturday. Reports put the number at **two, three or four vessels**, and maritime authorities have not confirmed any of the strikes or identified the ships ([Yahoo News, Nov 14](https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/iran-says-four-tankers-hit-074043264.html)). Tehran ties any reopening to an end of the **U.S. naval blockade** of its ports. *The April 7 ceasefire is nominally in place but under heavy strain; status as of Nov 15.*
- **Bahrain attacked.** Bahrain issued its **first nationwide alert in months** and said its air defenses destroyed incoming projectiles after Iran claimed an attack on a base that hosts U.S. helicopters. CENTCOM said two one-way attack drones were shot down and denied Iran's claim about hitting a radar site ([The National, Nov 15](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/gulf/2026/11/15/bahrain-issues-first-nationwide-alert-in-months-as-iran-claims-attack-on-base-hosting-us-helicopters/)). The UN Security Council met at Bahrain's request, and Gulf states asked it to hold Tehran accountable ([UN News, Nov 2026](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/11/1168804)). Trump has said he may hold off on new strikes if there are no further provocations. That could change quickly after Saturday.
- **Trump–Xi–Putin, Nov 19.** The Kremlin and the White House both confirm a first-ever **trilateral meeting in Shenzhen on Thursday**, on the sidelines of APEC (Nov 18–19) ([Anadolu, Nov 14](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/eurasia/kremlin-says-putin-xi-trump-to-hold-trilateral-meeting-in-shenzhen-on-nov-19/4125290); [Washington Post, Nov 13](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/11/13/trump-xi-putin-summit-china/3558fd80-dfbc-11f1-912c-4fbb80d73f7f_story.html)). Trump plans to raise **nuclear arms reduction** and Iran, and reportedly to steer away from Ukraine. U.S. officials call it a "first step" and don't expect an agreement. Beijing has previously rejected joining trilateral disarmament talks ([Al Jazeera, Nov 14](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/14/trump-xi-putin-trilateral-meeting-at-apec-what-to-expect)).
- **Israel: no government yet.** As of Nov 14, Gadi Eisenkot still had **no coalition deal**. His party and Likud are negotiating, but Netanyahu wants a unity government he would lead and Eisenkot insists the mandate is his ([Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/h17p1i80zg); [Times of Israel, Nov 14](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-november-14-2026/)). Reports differ on his deadline: 28 days plus a possible two-week extension, versus six weeks.
- **Ukraine talks.** The latest round of direct Russia–Ukraine talks, brokered by the U.S. (Witkoff and Kushner) in Abu Dhabi on **Nov 10–11**, focused on humanitarian issues and prisoner swaps. Zelenskyy rejects a U.S. suggestion that Ukraine cede parts of Donbas ([Washington Post, Nov 10](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/11/10/russia-ukraine-begin-us-backed-peace-talks-abu-dhabi/)). *As of Nov 11; no ceasefire confirmed since, and fighting continues.*
