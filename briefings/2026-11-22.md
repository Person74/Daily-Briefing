## Markets

**Bottom line:** An AI-led crash left the S&P 500 more than 10% below its record and the Nasdaq negative for 2026. PCE and GDP data on Wednesday now meet a market already pricing a December rate hike.

- **Friday's rout.** The S&P 500 fell **3.7% to 6,910.39**, the Nasdaq dropped **5.3%** and the Dow lost 949.73 points (−1.9%) to 48,147.33. For the week the S&P shed 8.6%, the Nasdaq 10.0% and the Russell 2000 7.5% ([Spectrum News/AP, Nov 20](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/us/snplus/business/2026/11/20/wall-street-nov-20-2026)). The S&P closed roughly 11.6% below its Oct 6 record, which is correction territory. The Nasdaq is now down for the year ([Spectrum News/AP, Nov 20](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/us/snplus/business/2026/11/20/wall-street-nov-20-2026); [CNBC, Nov 20](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/sp-500-correction-market-sell-off.html)). - **Nvidia is the epicenter.** Nvidia was already down more than 6% in a broad AI selloff when Reuters reported that **SEC enforcement staff are examining potential accounting issues** at the company. The stock finished down about **14%**, its worst day since the DeepSeek shock, and logged a **seventh straight weekly loss**, its longest losing streak since 2008 ([CNBC, Nov 20](https://cnbc.com/2026/11/20/nvidias-7-week-losing-streak-is-longest-since-2008-financial-crisis.html); [Motley Fool, Nov 20](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/20/nvidia-stock-plunge-wipes-out-nearly-usd500-billion-in-one-day-as-sec-reportedly-probes-its-accounting/)). Reports of the size of the drop range from 12% intraday to 14% at the close, depending on timing ([24/7 Wall St., Nov 20](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/20/nvidia-stock-plunges-12-after-reuters-reports-an-sec-probe-of-its-accounting-its-nvidias-worst-day-since-deepseek-and-it-has-lost-2-7-trillion-since-october/)). Nvidia is also in a leadership vacuum: **Jensen Huang resigned Nov 12**, with CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO ([Nvidia 8-K via SEC, Nov 12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm)). *No permanent successor had been reported as of Nov 20.*

One outlet put the close near 4.99%, so treat the exact level as approximate ([Spectrum News/AP, Nov 20](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/us/snplus/business/2026/11/20/wall-street-nov-20-2026)). WTI settled near **$84** and Brent near $92.57. Prices remain elevated by the Hormuz disruption (see Foreign Policy). Fed Governor Waller has floated a half-point move, while New York Fed President Williams ruled out an outsized hike ([CNBC, Nov 18](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/federal-reserve-governor-christopher-waller.html); [Axios, Nov 18](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/18/interest-rates-new-york-fed)). *These odds predate Friday's flight to safety and may have shifted since.* The inflation backdrop: October CPI rose **0.4%** (consensus 0.3%) and core rose 0.3% (consensus 0.2%).
