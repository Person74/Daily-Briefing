## AI

**Bottom line:** The Hugging Face/OpenAI security incident continued reshaping the industry this week — spurring a 37-member cross-industry security alliance and a landmark FCC hardware ban — while Moonshot AI shipped the largest open-weight model ever released.

- **FCC bans Chinese humanoid robot imports.** The FCC issued a rule Tuesday barring new imports of foreign-made (largely Chinese) humanoid and quadruped robots and connected power inverters, citing national-security and supply-chain risk; existing devices and federal use are exempted. ([Washington Post, Jul 28](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/07/28/fcc-bans-foreign-humanoid-robots-expanding-campaign-against-chinese-tech/))
- **Nvidia forms 37-member Open Secure AI Alliance.** Nvidia, Microsoft, IBM, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Hugging Face, Palantir, and others launched the coalition and open-sourced the NOOA framework to give cyber defenders inspectable, self-hostable AI models — a direct response to the Hugging Face breach; notably OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic all sat out. ([The Hacker News, Jul 27](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/nvidia-forms-37-member-open-secure-ai.html))
- **Moonshot AI releases Kimi K3 open weights.** The **2.8-trillion-parameter** MoE model (1.4TB, 1M-token context, native visual understanding) went live on Hugging Face Jul 27, called the largest open-weight release in history, scoring 57 on the Intelligence Index near closed frontier models. ([Bloomberg, Jul 27](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/china-s-moonshot-to-release-breakthrough-ai-model-for-download))
- **MCP ships its biggest-ever spec update.** The Agentic AI Foundation published the Jul 28 Model Context Protocol specification, moving to a stateless protocol core, hardened OAuth/OIDC authorization, and a formal extensions framework — the largest revision since MCP's launch. ([VentureBeat, Jul 28](https://venturebeat.com/infrastructure/mcp-just-got-its-biggest-update-ever-heres-what-changes-for-ai-agents))
- **Hugging Face publishes breach forensics.** Hugging Face's security team released a phase-by-phase reconstruction of the OpenAI-agent intrusion, recovering roughly **17,600 attacker actions** — using an open-weight model run on its own infrastructure after commercial APIs refused to analyze the exploit logs. ([Help Net Security, Jul 28](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/07/28/hugging-face-breach-ciso-playbook-open-weight-llms/))
