# Morning Brief — Wednesday, July 29, 2026

*The Fed decides at 2:00 p.m. ET with roughly a third of the market pricing an actual rate hike — the first serious hike risk in years, driven by oil-fueled inflation from the Iran conflict. It leads because everything else this morning, from Royal Caribbean's fuel line to Miami condo absorption, runs downstream of it.*

## Markets

**Bottom line:** A hawkish Fed under Kevin Warsh meets a market already in revolt over AI capital spending, and both resolve today — rate decision at 2:00 p.m. ET, Microsoft and Meta after the close.

- The FOMC announces at 2:00 p.m. ET today, with Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference at 2:30 p.m. Consensus still says hold at 3.50%–3.75% — which would be the fifth straight pause — but the distribution has moved hard. CME FedWatch put hike odds at 10.7% on July 15, 34.7% by July 22, and roughly 38% by late last week, as crude topping $100 during the Iran escalation pushed headline inflation to about 3.7% ([Forbes, Jul 23](https://www.forbes.com/sites/simonmoore/2026/07/23/markets-see-chance-fed-hikes-next-week-at-july-meeting/); [HNGN, Jul 24](https://www.hngn.com/articles/272326/20260724/fed-rate-hike-odds-surge-38-ahead-july-meeting-oil-prices-fuel-inflation-fears.htm)). Warsh has told Congress the committee has "no tolerance for persistently elevated inflation." No Summary of Economic Projections accompanies this meeting, so the statement language and the presser carry the entire signal.
- Tuesday's tape was a rotation, not a rally: the Dow rose 537.24 points (+1.03%) to 52,747.32, the S&P 500 added 0.21% to 7,428.78, and the Nasdaq Composite slipped 0.22% to 24,876.91. Semis were the funding source — the VanEck Semiconductor ETF fell more than 3% for a fourth consecutive down day, with Micron and AMD each off more than 8% — while Sherwin-Williams (+8% on a Q2 beat) and Coca-Cola (+5% on a beat and a raised outlook) led the industrials-and-staples bid. Apple briefly touched a $5 trillion market cap, a day after passing Nvidia as the most valuable public company ([TheStreet, Jul 28](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-july-28-2026); [Yahoo Finance, Jul 28](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-tuesday-july-28-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-chip-stocks-082832371.html)).
- Microsoft and Meta both report after today's close, into a tape that has stopped rewarding AI capex. Street consensus for Microsoft is roughly $4.24 adjusted EPS on about $87.6 billion in revenue (+14.6% y/y), with Azure growth and AI monetization the whole story; Meta is expected near $7.18–$7.24 EPS on about $60.2 billion (+27%), after raising 2026 capital spending guidance to $125–145 billion from $115–135 billion ([TipRanks, Jul 27](https://www.tipranks.com/news/big-tech-earnings-showdown-meta-and-microsoft-in-focus-ahead-of-july-29-earnings-report)). The cautionary template is Alphabet, which beat on revenue (+24% to $119.8 billion) but posted negative free cash flow of $5.9 billion — its first negative quarter in roughly two decades — as quarterly capex doubled to $44.9 billion and full-year capex guidance went to $195–205 billion ([Investing.com, Jul 24](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-alphabet-beats-q2-2026-estimates-shares-fall-on-capex-surge-93CH-4807140); [Fortune, Jul 26](https://fortune.com/2026/07/26/big-tech-earnings-meta-microsoft-apple-amazon-market-revolt-ai-spending/)).
- Oil gave back its war premium. WTI fell 4.03% to $79.28 and Brent slipped 0.94% to $87.53 on Tuesday as the Hormuz de-escalation removed geopolitical risk from the curve ([Trading Economics, Jul 28](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/crude-oil); [Vantage Markets, Jul 28](https://www.vantagemarkets.com/market-analysis/crude-oil-prices-today-brent-wti-july-28-2026/)). That is the single most important variable for the Fed's inflation path — and the reason a hike today would look like fighting the last quarter's war.
- Consumer confidence released yesterday came in soft: the Conference Board index fell 1.4 points to 90.8 from an upwardly revised 92.2 in June. The Present Situation Index dropped 3.6 points to 114.9, its third straight monthly decline, while the Expectations Index was flat at 74.7. Chief economist Dana Peterson noted appraisals of current business conditions and, to a lesser extent, the current labor market both softened; the survey ran July 1–22, fully inside the Middle East conflict window ([The Conference Board, Jul 28](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/us-consumer-confidence-edged-down-in-july-302836484.html)).
- On deck Thursday at 8:30 a.m. ET: advance Q2 GDP, June PCE, personal income, consumer spending and jobless claims all at once. Q1 finished at 2.1% annualized; the Atlanta Fed's GDPNow was tracking Q2 at 1.7% as of July 17 against a consensus nearer 2.0%. June PCE follows May's hot 4.1% headline / 3.4% core — the highest since 2023 — which is the number that will either validate or embarrass whatever the Fed does today ([Letters to a Young Investor, Jul 27](https://letterstoayounginvestor.substack.com/p/weekly-economic-calendar-july-27)).

## Cruise Industry

**Bottom line:** Royal Caribbean beat and raised full-year EPS guidance yesterday, but quietly trimmed its revenue growth target on Middle East itinerary softness — strong pricing, weaker geography.

- **Business — Royal Caribbean Group.** RCL reported Q2 EPS of $4.20 and Adjusted EPS of $4.21, above guidance and above the ~$3.97–$3.98 consensus, on revenue of $4.83 billion. The company carried 2.4 million guests, up 6% y/y, at a 110% load factor, and introduced Legend of the Seas in the quarter. Full-year Adjusted EPS guidance went up to $17.73–$17.87 from $17.10–$17.50, driven by strong close-in demand, lower costs and favorable joint-venture performance ([Royal Caribbean Group, Jul 28](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/royal-caribbean-group-reports-second-quarter-results-above-expectations-and-raises-full-year-guidance-302836372.html); [Cruise Industry News, Jul 28](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/07/royal-caribbean-2026-q2-results-strong-company-raises-full-year-guidance/)). Sources differ slightly on the revenue growth rate — the release and most coverage put it at 6%, Zacks-sourced write-ups at 6.5% — a rounding difference, not a discrepancy in the underlying number.
- **The catch in the print.** Even while raising EPS, RCL pulled its full-year revenue growth target back to roughly 9% from roughly 10%, citing softening demand on select sailings tied to prolonged geopolitical activity — which is why the pre-market reaction was muted despite a clean beat ([Quartz, Jul 28](https://qz.com/royal-caribbean-earnings-revenue-forecast-geopolitical-bookings-072826); [Skift, Jul 28](https://skift.com/2026/07/28/royal-caribbean-earnings-impact-iran-war/)). Fuel was the other pressure point, running 27% above last year in the quarter. Management's offsetting message: the book is still at record prices with volumes above last year, and 2027 booking trends are running ahead of historical levels — including on the very itineraries geopolitics dented this year.
- **Capital deployment.** RCL returned more than $600 million to shareholders in the quarter — $199 million of buybacks and $404 million of dividends — with $805 million remaining on the repurchase authorization ([Royal Caribbean Group, Jul 28](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/royal-caribbean-group-reports-second-quarter-results-above-expectations-and-raises-full-year-guidance-302836372.html)).
- **Perfect Day Mexico stays in limbo.** CEO Jason Liberty barely touched the project on the call, saying only that "our commitment to Mexico and the destinations we visit is stronger than ever" and that the company remains in constructive dialogue with community leaders and public officials in Mahahual ([Seatrade Cruise News, Jul 28](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/ports-destinations/royal-caribbean-in-constructive-dialogue-with-mahahual-leaders)). Background: Mexican officials confirmed in May that the 230-acre Costa Maya destination — bought as a $292 million port acquisition in October 2024 and once slated for 2027 — would not be approved on environmental grounds tied to the Mesoamerican Reef ([Cruise Critic, May 2026](https://www.cruisecritic.com/news/mexican-environmental-agency-halts-perfect-day-mexico)). The private-destination portfolio is still guided to expand from three to eight by 2028 across the Perfect Day and Royal Beach Club collections. Executives also flagged Celebrity River as an emerging growth engine, citing premium pricing and crossover from the ocean brands ([Cruise Industry News, Jul 2026](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/07/royal-caribbean-big-ambitions-for-celebrity-river-entry/)).
- **Business — competitors.** Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings reports Q2 tomorrow, July 30, at 6:30 a.m. ET with a call at 8:30 a.m. ([Seatrade Cruise News, Jul 2026](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/finance-legal-regulatory/nclh-sets-july-30-for-q2-earnings-report)). It is the most consequential cruise print of the week: consensus is 39 cents adjusted, down 23.5% y/y, on $2.63 billion. NCLH carries roughly $15.2 billion of debt at about 5.3x net leverage, is down ~19% year-to-date, and already cut full-year guidance to $1.45–$1.79 from as high as $2.38, blaming fuel and soft European bookings. Carnival, by contrast, has raised its FY2026 outlook toward roughly $2.22 adjusted EPS and about $7.11 billion adjusted EBITDA, and marked the first anniversary of Celebration Key on Grand Bahama at 2.4 million guests ([Travel And Tour World, Jul 2026](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/xdz339lsw3g3/)).
- **Ecosystem — PortMiami.** The port has opened a cluster of infrastructure procurements worth roughly $4.19 million covering portwide fencing, passenger boarding-bridge runway surfaces at Cruise Terminal E, and roof, concrete and electrical repairs at Cruise Terminal C, with a bid deadline of July 30. Separately, the $345 million Cruise Terminal G — built for RCG's largest ships at roughly 7,000 passengers per call — remains on track for late 2027. PortMiami handled a record 8,564,225 cruise passengers last fiscal year, up 4.02% ([Travel And Tour World, Jul 2026](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/ijmgx7hxifdd/)).
- **Consumer-facing.** Yesterday's deployment news: Princess Cruises opened sales for its 2028 Alaska season with 185 departures across eight ships; Virgin Voyages announced it will sail Northern Europe in summer 2028; United Waterways and its ocean division Ocean Advice advanced their newbuild program including a new expedition ship for Poseidon; and Oceania rolled out Wellness Discovery Tours ([Vacations To Go, Jul 28](https://www.vacationstogonewsletters.com/current/cruise_newsletter.cfm)). ## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** Trump held back-to-back White House meetings with Netanyahu and Zelenskyy yesterday, and the Hormuz reopening is progressing faster than expected — the reason oil fell and the reason cruise itinerary demand may recover into 2027.

- Netanyahu met Trump at the White House Tuesday for their first face-to-face since the Iran war began — closed to press, with Trump departing from custom by not opening it even briefly. Agenda: the Iran conflict, a peace framework for Lebanon, expansion of the Abraham Accords, and Iran's alleged expansion of the Pickaxe Mountain nuclear site. Netanyahu called it one of the best sit-downs the two have had; the leaders reaffirmed an "ironclad" commitment to keeping Iran from nuclear weapons. No final agreements were reached ([UPI, Jul 28](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/US/2026/07/28/trump-meets-with-netanyahu/9321785265918/); [Fox News, Jul 28](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/trump-netanyahu-meeting-iran-war-white-house-july-28-2026)).
- Zelenskyy met Trump earlier the same morning at 9:30 a.m. ET for just over an hour, focused on licenses for domestic Patriot interceptor production — Trump agreed earlier this month to allow joint production — and on reviving diplomacy to end a war now past its fourth year. Zelenskyy called it a "good meeting," the latest marker in a relationship that has warmed considerably ([Al Jazeera, Jul 28](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/28/ukraines-zelenskyy-hails-good-meeting-with-trump-at-white-house); [CNBC, Jul 28](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/trump-zelenskyy-ukraine-russia-iran-war.html)). Both leaders were in Washington for Sen. Lindsey Graham's funeral.
- The Strait of Hormuz — which carried roughly one-fifth of global oil supply before the February 28 US and Israeli strikes on Iran — is reopening ahead of schedule after a US-Iran memorandum of understanding and indirect talks in Qatar. Iran and Oman spent the weekend negotiating the terms, with Oman proposing a regional consortium to oversee maritime security and Iran insisting on retaining control of the waterway ([Al Jazeera, Jul 2](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/2/with-hormuz-reopened-has-the-oil-shortage-turned-into-a-glut); [Just Security, Jul 28](https://www.justsecurity.org/150079/early-edition-july-28-2026/)). Trump has said there is "a good chance" the new diplomatic round yields a breakthrough while warning he would resume airstrikes if it does not. The market's read: as flows resume, the risk has flipped from shortage to glut, with China cutting imports.
- Brazil requested WTO consultations with the US over tariffs announced this month, and separately agreed with South Korea to accelerate talks on a Korea–Mercosur trade deal ([Just Security, Jul 28](https://www.justsecurity.org/150079/early-edition-july-28-2026/)). Colombian president-elect Abelardo de la Espriella will halt plans to open an embassy to the Palestinian Authority in Ramallah, a signal of warming ties with Israel and Washington.

The Big Grass Fire near Jordan Valley has grown to nearly 200,000 acres at 5% containment; the Akawa Butte Fire is at 27,000-plus acres in Deschutes and Jefferson counties at 6% containment. The Oregon National Guard activated four helicopters and roughly 20 servicemembers Tuesday for the Hagen Fire in Eastern Oregon. Hot, dry weather and strong winds have driven the run since the weekend, and homes have been lost ([KLCC, Jul 28](https://www.klcc.org/disasters-accidents/2026-07-28/thousands-under-go-now-evacuation-orders-oregon-wildfires-burn-1-1-million-acres); [OPB, Jul 27](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/07/27/wildfire-oregon-weather-public-safety/)).

## Florida and Miami

**Bottom line:** Haitian TPS lapsed Monday night with roughly 150,000 affected people in Florida concentrated in the metro's healthcare and tourism workforces — the most economically disruptive local story of the week.

- Temporary Protected Status for Haitians terminated effective July 27 at 11:59 p.m., ending work authorization and deportation protection for roughly 350,000 people nationally after the Supreme Court backed the administration's termination on June 25. An estimated 150,000–158,000 live in Florida, more than 45% of the US Haitian immigrant population, and they are concentrated in the state's two largest industries — healthcare and tourism. ICE is reported to be preparing increased arrests of Haitian nationals as early as this week. Holders with separate status or independent work authorization are not immediately affected; those relying solely on TPS are ([WFLX, Jul 28](https://www.wflx.com/2026/07/28/floridas-haitian-community-braces-job-losses-deportations-tps-ends/); [WSVN, Jul 28](https://wsvn.com/news/us-world/south-floridas-haitian-community-braces-for-expected-start-of-deportations-after-end-of-court-issued-tps-extension/); [USCIS](https://www.uscis.gov/humanitarian/temporary-protected-status/temporary-protected-status-designated-country-haiti)). For anyone staffing hotel, port, hospital or shipboard-adjacent operations in Miami-Dade and Broward, this is a near-term labor availability problem, not an abstract one.
- Leon County Circuit Judge David Frank ruled Monday, in a 25-page order issued just before 5 p.m., that Republican gubernatorial candidate James Fishback stays on the August 18 primary ballot. Lt. Gov. Jay Collins had sued to disqualify him, arguing Fishback forfeited Florida residency by voting in Washington, D.C. in 2020; the judge found Collins had not overcome Florida's presumptions favoring continued residency and ballot access, and declined to "decide the race." A FiftyPlusOne poll released Tuesday still has Fishback second to Trump-endorsed Rep. Byron Donalds ([Florida Phoenix, Jul 27](https://floridaphoenix.com/2026/07/27/florida-judge-refuses-to-boot-fishback-from-the-ballot-wont-decide-the-race/); [WLRN, Jul 27](https://www.wlrn.org/government-politics/2026-07-27/fishback-florida-ballot-governor-collins-lawsuit-ruling); [WFLX, Jul 28](https://www.wflx.com/2026/07/28/fishback-demands-collins-resign-after-judge-keeps-him-floridas-republican-primary-ballot/)).

## Massachusetts

**Bottom line:** A stalled, fall-strength storm is dropping a month's worth of rain on southern New England today and tonight with flash flooding likely — the commute is the story, not the politics.

## AI

Conspicuously absent: OpenAI, Google and Anthropic — the three largest closed-model developers — which makes the alliance as much a competitive alignment as a safety initiative ([Tech Startups, Jul 27](https://techstartups.com/2026/07/27/top-tech-news-today-july-27-2026-anthropic-monday-com-moonshot-ai-nvidia-openai-more/)).

## Gaming

**Bottom line:** Thin news cycle — Nintendo's Switch 2 continues to run ahead of the PS5's lifecycle pace with Xenoblade Chronicles 2 arriving tomorrow, while the industry still absorbs Microsoft's July restructuring.

- Xenoblade Chronicles 2 — Nintendo Switch 2 Edition releases tomorrow, July 30, with 60fps and 4K output ([Nintendo Life, Jul 2026](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/upcoming-nintendo-switch-2-games-and-accessories-for-july-and-august-2026); [Game Rant, Jul 2026](https://gamerant.com/ns2-games-coming-out-soon-list-july-2026/)). Splatoon Raiders, the co-op campaign spin-off, held the top spot on the Switch 2 eShop charts as of the July 26 reading, with the Final Fantasy X/X-2 HD Remaster entering at #6 ([Nintendo Everything, Jul 26](https://nintendoeverything.com/nintendo-switch-2-eshop-charts-july-26-2026/)).
- The hardware context: Switch 2 shipped 19.86 million units through the fiscal year ended March 31 — the fastest ramp of any Nintendo console — with 2.49 million in the January–March quarter alone, outselling PS5 by roughly a million units that quarter and running about 8 million ahead of the PS5's pace at the equivalent lifecycle point. Nintendo guides to 16 million more consoles and 60 million games by March 2027 ([This Week In Video Games, 2026](https://thisweekinvideogames.com/news/nintendo-sells-19-86-million-switch-2-consoles-expects-to-move-16-million-more-in-12-months/)). A US price increase to $499.99 from $449.99 takes effect September 1 — worth noting as a forward catalyst for pull-forward demand this quarter.
- Business layer, still reverberating: Xbox CEO Asha Sharma announced layoffs, management restructuring and studio portfolio changes on July 6, affecting five studios and thousands of corporate roles — the largest gaming layoff in years. Ninja Theory was told on June 15 it was closing; Compulsion Games and Double Fine are in closure or buyout negotiations; Arkane Lyon reportedly faces closure alongside cancellation of Marvel's Blade. Xbox will finish fiscal 2026 at roughly a 3% profitability margin, having spent more than $20 billion on content over five years — excluding the $68.7 billion Activision Blizzard deal — while annual revenue shrank by nearly $500 million ([Tech Times, Jul 6](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319765/20260706/xbox-fires-thousands-shuts-five-studios-largest-gaming-layoff-years.htm)).
- Sony has set an end to physical disc production for all new PlayStation releases in January 2028, and disclosed roughly $765 million in write-downs against Bungie's asset value tied to Destiny 2 underperformance and Marathon's failed March launch ([Business Standard, Jul 7](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/microsoft-xbox-layoff-restructuring-consoles-sony-nintendo-gaming-evolution-gaming-industry-126070700695_1.html)).
