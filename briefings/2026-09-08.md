# Morning Brief — Tuesday, September 8, 2026

*Miami International is still running on half its runways after Sunday's fatal Amazon cargo crash, and an escalating US-Iran war has pushed oil to seven-week highs right as a hawkish Fed pivot puts a rate **hike** — not a cut — back on the table for next week's FOMC meeting.*

## Weather and Disruptions

**Bottom line:** MIA remains significantly disrupted after Sunday's fatal crash even as the forecast itself is routine and the tropics stay unusually quiet.

- **MIA still on half capacity.** An Amazon Air Boeing 767-300 (Flight 7598) overran a runway on landing Sunday afternoon, Sept. 6, killing **5** people on the ground and injuring 5 more; NTSB has recovered both black boxes and is investigating. As of Monday, only **2 of 4 runways** were open, FlightAware logged roughly 129 delays and 65 cancellations, and the FAA's ground delay program averaged **2 hours 31 minutes** — disruptions are expected to continue into today, so confirm flight status directly with your airline. ([Local10, Sep 7](https://www.local10.com/news/local/2026/09/07/delays-cancellations-continue-at-mia-following-amazon-cargo-plane-crash-that-killed-5-people/))
- **Miami forecast: hot, stormy, routine.** Highs near **90°F** under partly sunny skies with scattered afternoon thunderstorms driven by tropical moisture and light easterly winds — a pattern that holds into midweek with no severe weather flagged. ([NWS Miami-South Florida](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lon=-80.31639&lat=25.79056))
- **Tropics historically quiet.** NHC's latest outlook shows no development expected across the Atlantic, Caribbean, or Gulf over the next 7 days despite three tracked tropical waves. The 2026 season has produced five named storms but **zero hurricanes** so far, putting it on pace to challenge the satellite-era record for latest first hurricane if none forms by Sept. 11. ([WTOK, Sep 6](https://www.wtok.com/2026/09/06/were-heading-toward-peak-atlantic-hurricane-season-all-remains-quiet-now/))
- **PortMiami tunnel overnight closures.** Scheduled maintenance closures run **9 p.m.–5 a.m.** nightly from tonight through Thursday; daytime cruise operations are unaffected. ([PortMiami operations notice](https://www.miamidade.gov/portmiami/home.page))

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The Iran war escalated sharply over the weekend and Ukraine's brief ceasefire lapsed with no deal, while trade truces with China and the EU hold for now.

- **Iran war escalates toward Hormuz.** CENTCOM struck and disabled Iranian oil tankers after Iran's IRGC fired ballistic missiles at a US carrier and destroyer Friday (both evaded, no casualties); Iran-backed Houthis then struck Saudi cities including Abha and Najran. Iran's Supreme National Security Council said Monday it plans to declare a maritime **"exclusion zone"** near the Strait of Hormuz, and Qatar said Tuesday it's working with China to revive diplomacy. Brent crude hit **~$99/barrel**, a seven-week high. ([CBS News, Sep 8](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-strait-of-hormuz-oil-gas-price-strikes/); [Al Jazeera, Sep 7](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/9/7/iran-war-live-tehran-to-announce-new-hormuz-shipping-route-in-coming-days))
- **Ukraine ceasefire pause expires.** A limited three-day halt in frontline fighting tied to a US envoy push (Witkoff and Kushner met Putin, then Zelensky) lapsed at end-of-day yesterday with no breakthrough; Russian strikes on Kyiv reportedly killed at least two people as the pause ended. Zelensky said he expects the war to continue, with Russia still demanding full Donbas cession. ([Al Jazeera, Sep 8](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/8/russia-strikes-kyiv-as-three-day-pause-during-us-envoy-visits-ends))
- **Gaza ceasefire strained, not collapsed.** *As of the most recent detailed assessment (early September, unconfirmed since):* the October 2025 US-brokered ceasefire remains technically in force — Israel controls roughly **54%** of Gaza, Hamas dissolved its civil administration in July — but over 700 Palestinians and 4 Israelis have died in violations since, and 77% of Gazans face acute food insecurity. No reports of collapse have surfaced this week. ([J Street assessment](https://jstreet.org/six-months-in-assessing-the-status-of-the-gaza-ceasefire/))
- **US-China truce holds, friction persists.** The May Trump-Xi summit truce (China buying $17B/year in US agricultural goods through 2028) remains in place and both leaders are slated to meet again in the US this month, but USTR added a fresh **12.5%** tariff on all Chinese goods in July — analysts call it a "tactical truce, not a strategic shift." ([China Briefing](https://www.china-briefing.com/news/trump-xi-meeting-outcomes-and-implications/))
- **EU tariff framework in force.** The US-EU deal took effect July 1, with the US moving several EU export categories (generic pharma, cork, aircraft parts) to duty-free treatment as of Sept. 1; steel/aluminum derivatives still sit at a 50% tariff, and the EU has an automatic snapback if US derivative duties aren't cut to 15% by year-end. ([Consilium](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/20/eu-us-trade-council-and-parliament-strike-a-deal-to-implement-the-tariff-elements-of-the-joint-statement/))

## Markets

**Bottom line:** A blowout jobs report and a hawkish Fed pivot have markets pricing rate-**hike** odds for next week, and Monday's Iran-driven oil spike is compounding the inflation worry.

- **Jobs report reignites hike bets.** Friday's August payrolls smashed expectations at **+162,000** versus a consensus of just 53,000, with unemployment steady at 4.1% and wages up 3.1% y/y. Because inflation is already running hot, the strong print pushed odds of a **25bp hike** at the Sept. 15–16 FOMC meeting to roughly **58–63%** — a reversal from earlier-2026 rate-cut expectations. ([CNBC, Sep 4](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/jobs-report-august-2026.html))

([CNBC, Aug 28](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/28/kevin-warsh-jackson-hole-federal-reserve-inflation.html))
- **Oil surges on Iran conflict.** Brent (+2.2% to **$99.16**) and WTI (+3.3% to **$94.46**) hit six-week highs Tuesday after the weekend's tanker strikes and missile exchange; Goldman Sachs raised its Brent/WTI forecasts by $5 citing prolonged Hormuz disruption risk into 2027. ([CNBC, Sep 8](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-hormuz-iran-war.html))
- **Tuesday session: tech leads losses, dollar firms.** The S&P 500 fell roughly **0.15%** intraday with Apple, Alphabet, and Microsoft among the biggest drags, while the dollar index rebounded to **99.3** on both hike bets and a safe-haven bid from the Iran conflict. ([Yahoo Finance/TheStreet, Sep 8](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-tuesday-september-8-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-080440338.html))
- **What's ahead: CPI Friday, Oracle earnings.** August CPI lands **Friday, Sept. 11** — a pivotal print given the hawkish pivot (prior core reading was +0.2% m/m / +2.5% y/y). Oracle reports fiscal Q1 earnings Sept. 10, with AI-driven data-center capex in focus after 39% cloud revenue growth last quarter. ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm))
