## Markets

**Bottom line:** The S&P 500 and Nasdaq closed at records Friday as a shockingly weak July jobs report cut September rate-hike odds nearly in half; oil is climbing on unresolved Hormuz tensions — a direct fuel-cost risk for cruise lines, Carnival most of all — with CPI and PPI due this week to confirm or contradict the dovish read.

- **Records on a weak print.** The **S&P 500 rose 0.62%** to a record **7,757.64** and the **Nasdaq jumped 1.30%** to 26,690.62 Friday; for the week the S&P gained **3.6%**, its best since April, reversing a 450-point Dow selloff earlier in the week tied to oil prices. ([Yahoo Finance/TheStreet, Aug 7](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-august-7-nasdaq-dow-sp-500-july-jobs-report-surprises-100009572.html))
- **July payrolls fell 23,000** versus consensus of +80,000-86,000, with May and June revised down a combined 103,000; unemployment ticked down to **4.1%** on people leaving the workforce, not hiring strength, and wage growth slowed to 3.2% y/y, the weakest since May 2021. Because 2026 markets have spent the year fearing a Fed *hike* on the oil-driven inflation shock rather than a cut, weak jobs data read as bullish — the opposite of the usual dynamic. ([BLS, Aug 7](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm); [CNBC, Aug 7](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/07/odds-the-fed-hikes-in-september-tumble-following-big-july-jobs-miss.html))
- **Hike odds tumbled.** The 10-year yield fell 7bp to roughly 4.6%, and odds of a September Fed hike dropped from about 58% to **40-42%**. The next FOMC decision lands **September 16**. ([Federal Reserve H.15, Aug 7](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h15/); [CNBC, Aug 7](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/07/odds-the-fed-hikes-in-september-tumble-following-big-july-jobs-miss.html))
- **Oil climbs on Hormuz risk — direct cruise exposure.** Brent crude rose to **$84.18/bbl** Monday, its third straight gain, as Iran-Oman shipping talks remain unresolved. Analysts estimate a 10% change in fuel costs cuts **Carnival's 2026 net income by $156 million**, versus **$57 million for Royal Caribbean** — Carnival is by far the most fuel-exposed major line. ([Trading Economics, Aug 10](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/brent-crude-oil); [AOL, cruise fuel cost analysis](https://www.aol.com/articles/cruise-lines-face-fuel-cost-221640717.html))

## AI

**Bottom line:** OpenAI paused work on its next frontier model after it neared "critical" cyber-capability thresholds, Anthropic is making Claude Code's autonomous mode the default this week, and Google DeepMind lost several top scientists — including Jeff Dean — to a new startup automating scientific discovery.

- **OpenAI pauses Astra over cyber risk.** OpenAI disclosed it paused internal work on its next major model, Astra, after safety evaluations couldn't rule out **"critical"** cyber capabilities — including identifying and developing functional exploits against hardened systems. - **Google DeepMind leadership shakeup.** Demis Hassabis is stepping back from day-to-day DeepMind control to become Alphabet's Chief Scientist, while Chief Scientist **Jeff Dean**, along with Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le, are leaving Google after 27 years to found Discovery Loop, aimed at automating the scientific method; Google is a founding investor.
