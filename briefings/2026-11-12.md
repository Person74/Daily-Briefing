## AI

**Bottom line:** The most valuable AI hardware company may lose its founder-CEO within days, and the AI infrastructure trade is otherwise strong on CoreWeave's numbers.

- **Nvidia leadership crisis.** The WSJ reported, and Reuters confirmed, that **Nvidia's board met late Wednesday** on whether to remove Huang ([NBC News, Nov 11](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/nvidia-shares-drop-hours-reports-meeting-ceo-jensen-huangs-future-rcna608906); [Al Jazeera, Nov 12](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/12/nvidia-shares-plunge-amid-reports-board-to-discuss-ceo-huangs-future)). Two issues are driving it. Huang earlier admitted an affair with a person US intelligence reportedly linked to Beijing, which China denies, and the NYT has since reported a relationship with an Nvidia VP who reports to him. **CFO Colette Kress** is reportedly favored by some directors as interim CEO ([Semafor, Nov 11](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/11/2026/nvidia-board-preparing-to-oust-ceo-huang-wsj)), and Bloomberg reports an outside search firm is identifying successors ([Irish Times/Bloomberg, Nov 12](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/11/12/nvidia-board-weighs-huangs-future-as-shares-tumble/)). *As of this morning no decision has been announced* ([CNBC, Nov 12](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-nvda-jensen-huang-second-affair.html)).
- **Board probe background.** The board's **Nov 8 statement** opened an independent investigation with outside counsel. At that point it said it had no reason to believe secrets or national security were compromised and expressed confidence in Huang ([NVIDIA IR, Nov 8](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/Statement-from-NVIDIA-Board-of-Directors/default.aspx)). That confidence now appears to have eroded. Separately, three House GOP chairs have requested a closed-door briefing from Huang on **Nov 18** ([Lets Data Science, Nov 9](https://letsdatascience.com/news/nvidia-board-opens-investigation-into-reported-foreign-intel-c5b93236)).
- **CoreWeave and AI capex.** CoreWeave's **$132.5B backlog** and raised guidance (see Markets) signal that AI compute demand is still outrunning supply. Capex reached roughly **$14.5B** in the quarter, keeping questions about financing and margins alive ([TradingKey, Nov 11](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262299527-coreweave-earnings-report-q3-revenue-growth-backlog-capital-expenditures-net-loss-tradingkey)).
- **New York's RAISE Act goes live.** New York opened its frontier-AI developer registration portal on **Nov 10**. Labs with more than $500M in revenue must file by **Jan 1, 2027**, and a new state office will enforce the law ([AI Weekly, Nov 10](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/new-york-opens-ai-portal-openai-anthropic-must-file-by-jan-1)). This is the first major state frontier-model rule to start operating, against federal pressure to preempt state AI laws.

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** The US–Iran standoff is unresolved and Hormuz traffic is still a fraction of normal. Ukraine talks produced a prisoner-swap deal but no ceasefire, and US envoys see Putin today.

- **Iran: no talks, blockade stays.** Iran's top security official said on **Nov 10** that no negotiations with Washington are underway, and that Tehran is waiting for a "clear and explicit" US answer to its conditions ([US News/Reuters, Nov 10](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-11-10/irans-top-security-official-says-no-talks-underway-with-us)). Those conditions include lifting the US naval blockade of Iran's southern ports and sanctions relief, roughly a return to the June Islamabad MOU ([Al Jazeera, Nov 9](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/9/iran-stands-by-demands-to-end-the-war-as-us-ramps-up-military-pressure)). Sources disagree on whether Qatar-mediated contacts continue. After Trump's Nov 11 "total victory" remarks, Iran's parliament speaker said Tehran may end the "no war, no peace" limbo through deterrence rather than negotiation ([National Herald/IANS, Nov 11](https://www.nationalheraldindia.com/international/iran-stands-firm-on-nuclear-stockpile-as-trump-seeks-total-victory)). Iranian commanders separately threatened US targets worldwide ([Iran International, Nov 11](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611111877)).
- **Strait of Hormuz: still restricted.** *As of 02:47 UTC Nov 12*, a ship-tracking monitor puts AIS-visible commercial crossings at **~6.9 per day over the past week, about 8% of the pre-war norm** ([hormuz.now, Nov 12](https://hormuz.now/)). CENTCOM says it is keeping the strait open for commercial shipping while enforcing the blockade on Iran ([Newsquawk/CENTCOM](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/us-forces-continue-to-work-around-the-clock-keeping-the-strait-of-hormuz-open-for-commercial-shipping-strictly-enforcing-the-ironclad-naval-blockade-against-iran)). Iran treats the strait as closed to "hostile" shipping. The two sides describe its status in opposite terms.
- **Ukraine: swap deal, no ceasefire.** The first trilateral US–Russia–Ukraine talks since February ended in Abu Dhabi on **Nov 11** with an in-principle agreement on a **large POW exchange**, possibly within 2–3 weeks. There was **no breakthrough on a ceasefire or territory** ([PBS/AP, Nov 11](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/russia-and-ukraine-agree-to-a-large-prisoner-exchange-as-talks-end-with-no-major-breakthrough)). US envoys Kushner and Witkoff are scheduled to meet **Putin today** and **Zelenskyy Friday** ([NPR, Nov 12](https://npr.org/2026/11/12/nx-s1-6037558/russia-ukraine-talks)).
- **China backs Cuba against US sanctions.** On **Nov 11**, FM Wang Yi met Cuba's foreign minister in Beijing and pledged support against US sanctions. Washington recently sanctioned Chinese electronics firms it accuses of supplying Cuba's military, and Beijing says it will take "all necessary measures" in response ([China-Global South Project, Nov 11](https://chinaglobalsouth.com/2026/11/11/china-cuba-us-sanctions-wang-yi-beijing)). This opens another front in US–China friction, close to Florida.
- **Spain strike before snap election.** UGT and CCOO held Spain's **first 24-hour general strike since 2012** on **Nov 11**, over housing costs and wages ([Catalan News](https://www.catalannews.com/housing-crisis/item/unions-call-general-strike-for-november-11-over-housing-and-wages)). Turnout was large by early reports ([Yahoo News, Nov 11](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/today-top-stories-november-11-003000137.html)). The campaign for the **Nov 29** general election starts Friday ([Idealista, Oct 9](https://www.idealista.com/en/news/legal-advice-in-spain/2026/10/09/917678-spain-general-strike-over-housing-crisis-set-for-11-november)).
