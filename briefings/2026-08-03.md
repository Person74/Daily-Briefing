# Morning Brief — Monday, August 03, 2026

*The Fed's July 29 hold came with a hawkish twist — three dissenters, a Chair calling it "the beginning of a story, not the end," and futures now pricing hikes instead of cuts — just as an OPEC+ supply increase and a shaky new round of Iran-Hormuz talks crushed oil back into the low $80s, a direct tailwind for cruise fuel costs even as Amazon and Apple pulled Wall Street's AI-capex story in opposite directions.*

## Markets

**Bottom line:** Stocks closed July at fresh highs on an Amazon-fueled rally, but the backdrop turned more hawkish over the weekend as the Fed held rates while signaling possible hikes ahead, sending Treasury yields to multi-year highs even as oil tumbled on OPEC+ supply increases and Iran de-escalation hopes.

- **Fed holds, tilts hawkish.** The FOMC voted 9-3 on July 29 to hold the federal funds rate at 3.50%–3.75%, its fifth straight pause, with three dissenters pushing for an immediate hike. New Fed Chair **Kevin Warsh** called it "the beginning of a story, not the end," and futures markets flipped to pricing **two 25bp hikes** — September and December — rather than cuts. ([CNBC, Jul 29](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/fed-rate-decision-july-2026.html); [Federal Reserve, Jul 29](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260729a.htm))
- **Yields hit multi-year highs.** The 10-year Treasury yield finished Friday at **4.75%** and the 30-year hit **5.23%**, its highest in 19 years, after several Fed officials said inflation risks are skewed to the upside. Rising yields are a headwind for capital-intensive, debt-heavy sectors like cruise operators. ([CNBC, Jul 31](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/treasury-yields-inflation-interest-rates-federal-reserve.html))
- **Amazon and Apple split the tape.** The S&P 500 rose to 7,489.72 and the Nasdaq to 25,373.85 Friday as **Amazon surged as much as 15%** on AWS revenue up **37% y/y** to $42.2 billion — its fastest cloud growth in four years — while **Apple fell roughly 6-8%** on weak guidance tied to a memory-chip supply squeeze, with scrutiny that a one-time tariff refund had flattered its EPS beat. ([CNBC, Jul 30](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/30/amazon-amzn-q2-earnings-report-2026.html); [CNBC, Jul 30](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/30/apple-earnings-live-updates.html))
- **Oil sinks on supply and de-escalation.** Brent slid from a July high near $91 to about **$84** and WTI to roughly **$81** — both down more than 13% from peak — after Trump eased fears of imminent Iran escalation and OPEC+ agreed to another September output increase. Lower crude is a direct tailwind for cruise line fuel costs. ([Benzinga, Aug 2](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/commodities/26/08/60862086/crude-oil-price-dives-as-opec-agrees-to-another-supply-hike))
- **Core PCE keeps running hot.** June's headline PCE eased to 3.7% from 4.1%, but **core PCE accelerated to 3.3%**, extending a five-year run above target and reinforcing the Fed's new hawkish tone rather than reviving cut hopes. ([CNN, Jul 30](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/30/economy/us-pce-inflation-consumer-spending-june))
- **Heavy week ahead.** ISM Manufacturing PMI prints today; Palantir, McDonald's, Disney and Uber report through the week, building to Friday's July jobs report — the marquee release given the Fed's inflation-first stance. New Section 232 tariffs of 100% on patented pharmaceutical imports also took effect Friday. ([TipRanks, Aug 2](https://www.tipranks.com/news/earnings-to-watch-this-week-august-3-7-2026); [Crowell & Moring, Jul 31](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/trump-administration-imposes-section-232-tariffs-on-patented-pharmaceutical-imports-tiered-rate-structure-takes-effect-beginning-july-31-2026))

## AI

**Bottom line:** The past 48 hours brought a genuine capability marker — an unreleased OpenAI model producing verified proofs for ten open math problems — alongside the first day the EU and California's AI transparency laws became simultaneously binding, plus a documented case of autonomous cyberattacks run via DeepSeek.

- **OpenAI's Astra solves open math problems.** An internal, unreleased version of OpenAI's next major model, Astra, produced machine-checkable Lean proofs for ten previously unsolved problems spanning group theory, geometry and combinatorics, including a new sphere-packing density bound — the first improvement since 1978. OpenAI says the total compute cost was roughly **$2,000**; independent mathematicians still need to verify novelty, and Astra itself remains unreleased. ([Tech Times, Aug 2](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/322710/20260802/openais-astra-solves-ten-decade-old-math-problems-machine-checkable-lean-proofs.htm))
- **EU and California transparency rules go live together.** On **August 2**, the EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency obligations took effect, requiring chatbots to disclose they're AI and deepfakes to carry disclosure labels; the same day, California's AI Transparency Act became operative, requiring large generative-AI providers to offer detection tools and embed provenance disclosures. ([European Commission, Aug 2](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august))
- **DeepSeek used for autonomous cyberattacks.** Palo Alto Networks disclosed that a Chinese-speaking threat actor wired **DeepSeek** into an agent framework, directing it via a single command to autonomously attack roughly **460 internet-facing systems**, confirming three successful compromises; OpenAI's and Anthropic's safety controls blocked comparable requests. ([Tech Times, Aug 1](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/322582/20260801/deepseek-ran-autonomous-cyberattacks-that-claude-openai-safety-controls-blocked.htm))
- **Google scraps standalone AI Studio app.** Google canceled its planned standalone AI Studio mobile app — despite roughly 800,000 preorders since May — folding its features into the main Gemini app instead. ([Dataconomy, Aug 3](https://dataconomy.com/2026/08/03/google-cancels-ai-studio-app-moves-creation-gemini/))
