# Morning Brief — Thursday, September 17, 2026

*The Fed raised rates for the first time in three years to fight an Iran-war oil shock, sparking a one-day market selloff that's already reversing this morning — and a public clash between Chair Kevin Warsh and President Trump that will hang over markets for weeks.*

## National

**Bottom line:** The Federal Reserve raised interest rates for the first time since 2023, rattling markets and reigniting a public clash with President Trump, while House Republicans cut short their session to dodge a surprise impeachment vote against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth.

- **Fed hikes rates, breaks three-year pause.** The FOMC voted **12-0** on Wednesday to raise the federal funds rate a quarter point to a **3.75%-4%** target range — the first increase since 2023 — with Chair **Kevin Warsh** saying "inflation is too high and has been for too long" and signaling openness to further hikes. Stocks sold off on the news: the **Dow fell about 631 points (1.2%)** to 51,461.90 and the S&P 500 dropped 0.45%, while the 10-year Treasury yield jumped above 5%. ([CNBC, Sep 16](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-september-2026.html)) ([Yahoo Finance, Sep 16](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-wednesday-september-16-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-fed-meeting-decision-080356525.html))
- **Trump blasts "hostile" Fed board.** Reacting to the hike, Trump said the FOMC is "very political" and accused it of trying to make him "do as bad as they possibly can" ahead of the midterms, while still voicing confidence in Warsh — whom he nominated earlier this year — and demanding rates be cut to **1% or lower**. ([CNBC, Sep 16](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/trump-fed-interest-rate-warsh.html))
- **House recesses early to sidestep Hegseth impeachment vote.** Speaker Mike Johnson sent the House home a day early on Wednesday, preventing a floor vote on Rep. Thomas Massie's privileged resolution to impeach Hegseth over his conduct of the six-month Iran war, including allegations of unauthorized troop deployments. Johnson called the effort "ridiculous"; the House won't return until **Nov. 9**, pushing the fight past Election Day. ([CNN, Sep 16](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/16/politics/house-early-recess-hegseth-impeachment)) ([NBC News, Sep 16](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/house-leave-early-punting-hegseth-impeachment-vote-november-elections-rcna598147))
- **Corporate movers amid the rate-driven selloff.** Generac surged roughly 30% after-hours on an **$8 billion** long-term supply deal with Amazon; Intel gained on reported talks with SK Hynix about U.S.-based memory-chip manufacturing; J.B. Hunt shares sank as much as 12.7% after an intra-quarter profit warning dragged down trucking stocks broadly. ([Yahoo Finance, Sep 16](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-wednesday-september-16-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-fed-meeting-decision-080356525.html))
- **NFL Week 1 wraps, Thursday night on deck.** Kansas City and Denver combined for 41 points in their season-opening Monday night matchup, tying a Super Bowl-era record for Week 1 scoring; newly acquired Chiefs back Kenneth Walker III rushed for 173 yards including a 60-yard touchdown. Detroit visits Buffalo on Thursday Night Football tonight, with Josh Allen and the Bills favored. ([NBC Sports, Sep 16](https://www.nbcsports.com/nfl/profootballtalk/rumor-mill))

## Markets

**Bottom line:** Stocks are clawing back Wednesday's Fed-hike selloff this morning as investors decide the quarter-point increase was already priced in, even as the Iran-war oil shock that forced the Fed's hand remains the underlying inflation threat to watch.

- **Oil shock forced the Fed's hand.** The driver behind Wednesday's hike is the widening Iran conflict choking transit through the **Strait of Hormuz**, which pushed Brent above $105 and WTI above $100 for the first time since May — reigniting inflation fears and putting Warsh, Trump's own pick who'd been expected to cut, at odds with the White House. ([Bloomberg, Sep 16](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-16/fed-raises-rates-as-warsh-bucks-trump-to-contain-inflation))
- **Wednesday's selloff, by the numbers.** The **Dow dropped 1.21% to 51,461.90** (down over 800 points intraday before paring losses), the **S&P 500 fell 0.45% to 7,551.81**, and the **Nasdaq Composite slipped 0.01% to 25,978.42**, as rate-sensitive financials and homebuilders led losses and bond yields rose. ([Yahoo Finance, Sep 16](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-wednesday-september-16-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-fed-meeting-decision-080356525.html))
- **Thursday rebound underway.** As of midday, the S&P 500 was up 0.4% to 7,617.88 and the Nasdaq climbed 0.8% to 26,198.18, with Dow futures also higher, as investors judged the hike had been fully anticipated and shifted focus to whether one more increase lands before year-end. ([TheStreet, Sep 17](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-sept-17-2026))
- **Oil eases, dollar firms.** WTI crude slipped to **$102.13/bbl (-0.29%)** Thursday as the acute supply-fear premium cooled slightly, while the dollar index rose to **99.43 (+0.32%)**, supported by the higher-rate carry appeal following the hike. ([Trading Economics, Sep 17](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/crude-oil))
- **Lennar misses on rate-hit housing demand.** Reporting after Wednesday's close, homebuilder **Lennar** posted Q3 net earnings of **$284 million**, down from $591 million a year ago, as new orders fell 9%, deliveries dropped, and gross margin compressed to 15.8% from 17.5% — an early read on how higher-for-longer rates are squeezing rate-sensitive sectors just as the Fed hiked again. ([StockTitan, Sep 16](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/LEN/lennar-reports-third-quarter-2026-q0xey1vxxpxn.html))
- **On deck.** No CPI, PPI, or jobs prints fall in the immediate window — the next major scheduled data are the September jobs report (Oct 2) and September CPI (Oct 14) — but roughly nine more earnings reports are due out today.
