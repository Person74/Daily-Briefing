## Markets

**Bottom line:** Stocks rallied hard Monday on an AI-chip surge even as the Fed sits on a freshly hiked 3.75%–4.00% funds rate, with a data-light week leaning on a heavy slate of Fed speakers and Friday's PCE report.

## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** Three major shipping chokepoints — Hormuz, Bab al-Mandeb and the Panama Canal — are constrained simultaneously right now, and Trump and Xi meet Thursday trying to save a China trade truce that expires in November.

- **Hormuz status disputed between sources.** The National reported Sep 21 that vessel crossings through the Strait of Hormuz more than halved over the weekend (14 vs. 36 the prior weekend) and that Iran's Parliament Speaker says the strait stays closed until Tehran's conditions are met — a closure dating to Feb. 28 strikes on Iran, after a June ceasefire/MOU broke down in July. The same day, other market commentary attributed falling oil prices partly to signs that Hormuz flows had reached a six-month high. *These two Sep 21 reads conflict; treat current Hormuz throughput as unconfirmed pending clearer data.* ([The National, Sep 21](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/09/21/strait-of-hormuz-vessel-traffic-remains-thin-amid-middle-east-tensions/))
