# Morning Brief — Friday, November 13, 2026

*Two shocks hit rates and the AI trade at once: Jensen Huang quit Nvidia abruptly, and the 10-year Treasury yield pushed back above 5% after Trump said "we don't need a deal with Iran." October PPI at 8:30 a.m. ET is the next test for a December Fed hike that markets already treat as all but certain.*

Fuel remains the most direct market link to cruise-line margins.
- **The Fed and data.** After a hot October CPI (**+0.4% headline, +0.3% core**), markets are pricing a December hike ([The National, Nov 10](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/economy/2026/11/10/federal-reserve-inflation-interest-rates-december/)). Waller has said 50 bp should be considered if inflation runs hot ([Kalshi News](https://news.kalshi.com/p/fed-interest-rate-odds-traders-see-87-chance-of-quarter-point-hike-in-december); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/article/feds-waller-leans-toward-quarter-point-rate-hike-in-december-open-to-50-basis-points-090000672.html); *exact publication dates of these two pieces unconfirmed, likely this week*). ## Foreign Policy

**Bottom line:** US–Iran diplomacy is moving backward while the naval blockade holds and Hormuz stays mostly shut. Ukraine talks are still going, and the US–China tariff truce runs to January 10.

- **Iran: pressure without a deal.** Trump's Thursday reversal leaves the **US naval blockade** and economic pressure in place. Iran says talks can't start until Washington accepts **seven conditions**, and it denies that any talks were scheduled ([Washington Post, Nov 12](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/11/12/trump-shuts-down-vance-iran-negotiating-efforts-says-no-deal-needed/); [Iran International, Nov 12](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611129077)). *Status as of Nov 12; unconfirmed since.*
- **Hormuz still restricted.** Commercial traffic through the Strait of Hormuz is about **7% of normal**: roughly 6 AIS-visible transits a day against a pre-crisis average of about 85. One tracker calls the strait **closed to commercial shipping as of Nov 12**, and another calls it "severely restricted" ([Straits.live, Nov 12](https://straits.live/briefs/2026-11-12); [hormuz.now](https://hormuz.now/faq)). *These figures come from tracker sites, not a maritime authority. Treat the counts as approximate. Iranian claims of tankers hitting mines are unconfirmed.* Earlier reports that the strait had "reopened" are from spring and no longer hold.
- **Ukraine talks continue.** US envoys **Witkoff and Kushner** met Putin in Moscow, and both sides agreed to keep peace talks going. Separately, Abu Dhabi talks ended with a **prisoner-exchange agreement**, and Zelenskyy says the next round could be in the UAE or Switzerland ([Al Jazeera, Nov 11–12](https://www.aljazeera.com/tag/ukraine-russia-crisis/)). Russian strikes on energy infrastructure left about **850,000 people without power** in Dnipropetrovsk and Kharkiv ([same](https://www.aljazeera.com/tag/ukraine-russia-crisis/)). *No ceasefire is in effect as of Nov 12.*
- **US–China truce extended.** China extended its suspension of the **24% additional tariff** on US goods to **Jan 10, 2027**, and the 10% rate stays in place. Port fees on US vessels are also suspended until then ([Bloomberg, Nov 9](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/china-extends-pause-on-24-us-tariffs-to-january-keeps-10-rate); [gov.cn, Nov 9](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202611/09/content_WS6af18d6bc6d00ca5f9a0e308.html)). On Nov 13, Beijing said the two sides remain in "close communication" on implementing the tariff cuts. *Reports differ on the size of the reciprocal cut, ~$30B per side vs. $60B total* ([China.org.cn, Nov 13](http://www.china.org.cn/2026-11/13/content_118773064.shtml)).
- **Gaza and Cuba.** An Israeli strike on **Nasser Hospital** killed at least six people, including a surgeon. Israel says it targeted a senior Hamas member ([Drop Site News, Nov 12](https://www.dropsitenews.com/p/israel-bombs-gaza-hospital-killing)). China's Wang Yi pledged "firm support" for Cuba, which is under a **US fuel embargo** that has caused rolling blackouts ([Foreign Exchanges, Nov 12](https://www.foreignexchanges.news/p/world-roundup-november-12-2026)). That matters for Miami's Cuban-American community.
