## Florida and Miami

**Bottom line:** Florida voters passed the big homestead property tax cut, which matters for local-government budgets and Miami housing.

- **Amendment 3 passes.** It passed with about **62%** (60% needed) in unofficial results, raising the non-school homestead exemption to **$150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028**, effective Jan 1, 2027. The state's estimating conference projects roughly **$5B** in lost local funding in year one and $12B by year five. Amendment 2 (farm property) missed at 58.35%. ([News4Jax, Nov 3](https://www.news4jax.com/news/local/2026/11/03/florida-voters-to-decide-on-property-taxes-state-reserves-and-farm-equipment-exemptions/); [BayNews9, Nov 4](https://baynews9.com/fl/tampa/news/2026/11/04/florida-voters-approve-amendment--providing-property-tax-relief-for-homeowners))
