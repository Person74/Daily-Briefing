# Morning Brief — Saturday, August 22, 2026

*A US-Canada tariff reprieve collapsed overnight, triggering blanket 50% duties on Canadian goods just as an Iran-war-driven oil spike knocks cruise line stocks down 3-5% and the national debt clears $40 trillion — a rough backdrop heading into a Fed-heavy week.*

## National

**Bottom line:** A last-minute US-Canada tariff deal fell apart and 50% duties took effect today, landing alongside a $40 trillion debt milestone and an Iran-war oil shock that's rattling consumers and markets alike.

- **Canada tariffs take effect.** A tentative reprieve reached August 19 — under which Prime Minister Mark Carney said Washington would suspend tariffs through today while the two sides negotiated steel/aluminum duties down toward **25%** and autos toward **15%** — collapsed before the deadline. Blanket **50% tariffs** on roughly **$20.2 billion** of Canadian exports (electronics, industrial machinery, furniture, dairy) took effect early this morning, escalating tensions with America's largest trading partner. ([Washington Post, Aug 22](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/08/22/new-50-percent-tariffs-canada-go-into-effect-after-deal-with-us-falls-through/); [NPR, Aug 19](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/19/g-s1-139156/trump-canada-tariffs))
- **National debt tops $40 trillion.** Treasury data released Wednesday showed the debt hit **$40.05 trillion**, just five months after crossing $39 trillion in March. The government is now borrowing roughly **$6 billion a day**, more than half of it just to cover interest, with spending outpacing tax revenue by more than $2 trillion this year. ([CBS News, Aug 19](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/national-debt-tops-40-trillion-doubles/))
- **Iran war lifts gas prices.** With the US-Iran conflict grinding past its negotiation deadline and Trump saying no talks are underway, US gas prices climbed back above a **$4-a-gallon** national average — AAA says this could be the highest August on record. Cruise operators felt it directly: Royal Caribbean, Carnival and Norwegian Cruise Line all fell **3-5%** this week on fuel-cost and itinerary-disruption fears (more in Cruise Industry, below). ([CNN, Aug 21](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/21/world/live-news/iran-war-trump))
- **New Fed chair's first Jackson Hole.** Kevin Warsh delivers the marquee address August 28 at the Kansas City Fed's Jackson Hole symposium (Aug 27-29) — his first as chair, with the Fed reportedly split 9-3 on the path forward. Markets are treating it as the week's most consequential event despite no formal policy vote. ([Matterfact, Aug 19](https://www.matterfact.com/newsletter/2026-08-19-dollar-brief-jackson-hole))

## Cruise Industry

**Bottom line:** An oil-driven selloff hit every major cruise stock this week, but underlying fundamentals stayed strong — Royal Caribbean raised guidance even as shares fell, and Viking posted a blowout quarter — while Disney and MSC pushed ahead on new tonnage.

- **Royal Caribbean guidance beats amid selloff.** RCL shares fell roughly **4%** on August 20 to **$287.62** as rising crude spooked cruise and airline investors sector-wide — not on company-specific news. Full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance of **$17.73-$17.87** sits above the ~$17.51 analyst consensus, following a July-quarter beat ($4.21 EPS vs. $3.98 expected), leaving investors to weigh a still-record outlook against a stock near a rich valuation (average price target ~$353). ([24/7 Wall St, Aug 20](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/08/20/norwegian-cruise-line-drops-5-carnival-falls-4-royal-caribbean-slips-3-as-oil-climbs/); [Ad-Hoc-News, Aug 21](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/royal-caribbean-stock-trades-near-292-as-investors-weigh-guidance-and/69985612))
- **Norwegian hit hardest.** NCLH dropped about **5%** on August 20 to ~$16.50 (Carnival -4% to $25.65) in the same oil-driven selloff, compounding a rough stretch — down nearly **17% year-to-date** — after NCLH's late-July guidance cut trimmed full-year adjusted EPS to about **$1.50** and flipped full-year net yield guidance from +0.4% to a **-2.7% to -4.7%** decline, citing soft Norwegian-brand demand and Middle East-linked pressure on European itineraries. ([StockStory, Aug 20](https://markets.financialcontent.com/stocks/article/stockstory-2026-8-20-why-norwegian-cruise-line-nclh-shares-are-sliding-today))
- **Viking's blowout quarter.** Viking Holdings posted adjusted EPS of **$1.31** versus $0.95 expected (a 38% beat), with revenue up **16.5%** to $2.19 billion and net yield up 6.2%. As of August 9, Viking had already sold **96%** of 2026 capacity and 53% of 2027 capacity — a sharp contrast to the mass-market lines' fuel-cost worries, showing premium demand and pricing power holding firm. ([Business Wire, Aug 19](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260819475597/en/Viking-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results))
- **Disney's fleet expansion.** At its mid-August D23 event, Disney confirmed the **Disney Believe** (late 2027, themed around Encanto, Frozen, Snow White, Moana and The Little Mermaid) and an entirely new, mid-sized ship class debuting **2029**. Disney also plans to double sailings from San Diego and send Disney Wish to Europe starting summer 2027. ([Skift, Aug 19](https://skift.com/2026/08/19/disney-global-guest-cruise-growth/))
- **MSC World Asia begins sea trials.** The third vessel in MSC's WORLD-class series left its shipyard for sea trials on August 1, a milestone ahead of delivery, as MSC also expanded planned onboard retail for the ship. ([Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/keyword/msc-cruises))

- **Stocks rebound, still down on the week.** The S&P 500 rose **0.43%** to 7,674.37 and the Dow jumped **~1%** (517.80 points) to 53,277.01 Friday, led by crypto-adjacent financials (Robinhood +14%, Coinbase +8%) as investors found footing after a mid-week Treasury-yield sell-off — but all three major indexes still closed the week lower. ([CNBC, Aug 18](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/18/treasury-yields-.html))

Estée Lauder beat ($0.39 EPS vs. $0.32 expected) and jumped **16.3%**, while Toll Brothers beat on both lines and rose 4%. ([Yahoo Finance, Aug 20](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-news-aug-20-104200456.html))
